| Technique | Controls | Purpose |

#### Method

1. Combine beans, water, onion, garlic, bay leaf, cumin, oregano, salt, and oil in a pressure cooker.
2. Cook at high pressure for 35 min.
3. Allow pressure to release naturally for 20 min, then release any remaining pressure.

### PHASE B — REDUCE AND FINISH

#### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| lime-juice | [Lime juice](ref:ingredient/lime-juice) | 25 g | 6.25% | Lime juice. |
| fine-salt | [Fine salt](ref:ingredient/salt) | 3 g | 0.75% | Fine salt. |
| coriander-leaves-chopped | [Coriander leaves, chopped](ref:ingredient/fresh-coriander) | 20 g | 5.00% | Coriander leaves, chopped. |

#### Method

1. Discard onion and bay leaf. Simmer uncovered for 10 min–20 min until the liquor lightly coats the beans.
2. Stir in lime juice and coriander. Adjust salt.
3. For taco service, crush approximately 20% of the beans into the liquor to make the mixture cohesive.

Bean age changes pressure-cooking time substantially; add 5 min–10 min if the beans remain chalky.
Reconstructed as an unsoaked method. Do not fill the pressure cooker above half capacity when cooking dried beans.
