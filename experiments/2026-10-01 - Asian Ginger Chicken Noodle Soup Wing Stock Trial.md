This record documents Carlo's retrospective account on 2026-10-01. The cooking
date has not been recorded. Recipe Draft v3 reconstructs the reported change.
Carlo confirmed that the remaining ingredients, quantities, and method were
the same as the previous Canonical Recipe v2. Completion records the reported
evidence; Curator acceptance is recorded separately before Promotion.

## Hypothesis

Retrospectively stated expectation: adding 4 whole chicken wings to the stock
extraction gives the finished soup a fuller mouthfeel. This was not recorded
as a prospective hypothesis before cooking, and collagen extraction was not
measured.

## Procedure

1. Add 4 whole chicken wings at the start, in addition to the existing chicken.
   The wings were counted, not weighed. Carlo estimates 50–75 g per wing,
   or 200–300 g total; this is a retrospective estimate, not a measurement.
2. Follow the existing stock extraction: 85–90 C, skimming for 20 min,
   then gently simmering for 90 min.
3. Discard the additional wings after extraction.
4. Follow the remaining ingredients, quantities, and whole-Recipe procedure
   from the previous Canonical Recipe v2, including broth fortification,
   bowl components, and service. Carlo confirmed these were unchanged.
   Carlo additionally recommends describing the low simmer as only a few
   bubbles breaking the surface to preserve clarity; this wording is
   included in Recipe Draft v3.

## Results

Carlo reported better mouthfeel in the finished soup. There was perhaps some
additional fat, but not enough to require removal. Stock clarity and collagen
content were not reported as measured results.

## Decision

The reported better mouthfeel, with additional fat that did not require
removal, supports submitting Recipe Draft v3 for Curator Promotion review.
Carlo confirmed that it represents the complete Recipe cooked. Curator
acceptance, acceptance rationale, and known shortcomings remain to be recorded
separately before Promotion.
