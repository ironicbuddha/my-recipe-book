### PHASE A — CORN

#### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| corn-kernels | [Corn kernels](ref:ingredient/corn-kernels) | 250 g | 100.00% | Corn kernels. |

#### Method

1. Heat a dry cast-iron pan over high heat.
2. Char corn for 4 min–6 min, stirring only occasionally, then cool completely.

#### Method

1. Whisk lime juice, olive oil, salt, cumin, and black pepper.
2. Soak red onion in the dressing for 10 min.

| mint-leaves-finely-sliced | [Mint leaves, finely sliced](ref:ingredient/mint) | 20 g | 8.00% | Mint leaves, finely sliced. |
| coriander-leaves-chopped | [Coriander leaves, chopped](ref:ingredient/fresh-coriander) | 20 g | 8.00% | Coriander leaves, chopped. |

#### Method

1. Fold beans, corn, Peppadew, mint, and coriander through the dressed onion.
2. Rest for 15 min, then adjust salt and lime before serving.

Peppadew sweetness varies, so lime may need adjustment.
Add mint only after all cooked ingredients are cool to prevent discolouration.
