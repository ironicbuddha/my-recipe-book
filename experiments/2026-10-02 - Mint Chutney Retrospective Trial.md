#### Method

1. Chill the blender jar and ingredients for 15 min.
2. Blend yoghurt, lime juice, chilli, ginger, cumin, salt, sugar, and water until smooth.
3. Add mint and coriander. Blend in short pulses just until smooth and bright green.
4. Chill immediately and rest for 15 min before adjusting salt and acidity.

Cold ingredients and minimal blending protect the green colour.
Best used the day it is made.
