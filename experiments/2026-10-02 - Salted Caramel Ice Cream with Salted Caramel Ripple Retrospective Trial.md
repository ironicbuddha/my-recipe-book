### PHASE A — CARAMELISE SUGAR FOR BASE

#### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| whole-milk | [Whole milk](ref:ingredient/whole-milk) | 300 g | 100.00% | Whole milk. |
| cream-approximately-35-fat | [Cream, approximately 35% fat](ref:ingredient/heavy-cream) | 180 g | 60.00% | Cream, approximately 35% fat. |
| sucrose | [Sucrose](ref:ingredient/sugar) | 110 g | 36.67% | Sucrose. |

#### Method

1. Heat 110 g sucrose in a heavy saucepan to a dark amber caramel.
2. Carefully add 180 g warm cream in stages. Stir until smooth, then add milk.

### PHASE B — MIX AND PASTEURISE BASE

#### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| dextrose | [Dextrose](ref:ingredient/dextrose) | 25 g | 8.33% | Dextrose. |
| skim-milk-powder | [Skim milk powder](ref:ingredient/skim-milk-powder) | 25 g | 8.33% | Skim milk powder. |
| egg-yolk | [Egg yolk](ref:ingredient/egg-yolk) | 50 g | 16.67% | Egg yolk. |
| ice-cream-stabiliser | [Ice cream stabiliser](ref:ingredient/ice-cream-stabiliser) | 1.5 g | 0.50% | Ice cream stabiliser. |
| fine-salt | [Fine salt](ref:ingredient/salt) | 2 g | 0.67% | Fine salt. |

#### Method

1. Whisk dextrose, skim milk powder, stabiliser, and salt together. Blend into the caramel dairy mixture.
2. Temper egg yolk with the warm mixture, recombine, and heat while stirring to 82 C–84 C for 30 s.
3. Blend for 30 s and cool to below 4 C as quickly as possible.
4. Refrigerate for 6 h–12 h.

### PHASE C — CARAMEL RIPPLE

#### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| sucrose | [Sucrose](ref:ingredient/sugar) | 150 g | 50.00% | Sucrose. |
| glucose-syrup | [Glucose syrup](ref:ingredient/glucose-syrup) | 50 g | 16.67% | Glucose syrup. |
| cream-warm | [Cream, warm](ref:ingredient/heavy-cream) | 150 g | 50.00% | Cream, warm. |
| unsalted-butter | [Unsalted butter](ref:ingredient/butter) | 40 g | 13.33% | Unsalted butter. |
| fine-salt | [Fine salt](ref:ingredient/salt) | 3 g | 1.00% | Fine salt. |

#### Method

1. Combine sucrose and glucose syrup. Cook to a dark amber caramel.
2. Carefully add warm cream in stages and stir smooth.
3. Remove from heat and blend in butter and salt.
4. Cool completely. The ripple should be thick but pourable at 20 C.

#### Phase Outputs

| Key | Phase Output | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cold-caramel-ripple | Cold caramel ripple | Prepared intermediate for a later Phase. Use 150 g in three 50 g layers; retain any remainder. |

### PHASE D — CHURN AND LAYER

#### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Ice cream churning](ref:technique/ice-cream-churning) | Draw approximately -6 C | Churn before layering the cold ripple. |

#### Method

1. Churn the aged base to approximately -6 C.
2. Pack one-third of the ice cream into a chilled container and drizzle with 50 g ripple. Repeat twice, ending with ripple.
3. Fold once with a narrow spatula to marble; do not mix completely.
4. Harden at -18 C for at least 4 h.
5. Retain any prepared ripple beyond the selected 150 g portion.

Caramel colour is the main flavour control. Stop before it smells burnt or acrid.
Any remaining ripple keeps refrigerated for up to 2 weeks.
