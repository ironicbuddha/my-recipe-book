### PHASE A — SIMMER

#### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| tamarind-concentrate | [Tamarind concentrate](ref:ingredient/tamarind-concentrate) | 100 g | 100.00% | Tamarind concentrate. |
| water | [Water](ref:ingredient/water) | 350 g | 350.00% | Water. |
| pitted-dates-chopped | [Pitted dates, chopped](ref:ingredient/dates) | 150 g | 150.00% | Pitted dates, chopped. |
| jaggery-or-dark-brown-sugar | [Jaggery or dark brown sugar](ref:ingredient/jaggery) | 100 g | 100.00% | Jaggery or dark brown sugar. |
| ground-cumin-toasted | [Ground cumin, toasted](ref:ingredient/cumin) | 5 g | 5.00% | Ground cumin, toasted. |
| ground-ginger | [Ground ginger](ref:ingredient/ground-ginger) | 2 g | 2.00% | Ground ginger. |
| kashmiri-chilli-powder | [Kashmiri chilli powder](ref:ingredient/kashmiri-chili-powder) | 2 g | 2.00% | Kashmiri chilli powder. |
| fine-salt | [Fine salt](ref:ingredient/salt) | 5 g | 5.00% | Fine salt. |

#### Method

1. Combine tamarind concentrate, water, dates, jaggery, cumin, ginger, chilli, and salt.
2. Simmer gently for 20 min–25 min until the dates are soft and the mixture is lightly syrupy.
3. Retain the recorded Jaggery or dark brown sugar option; [the alternative](ref:ingredient/dark-brown-sugar) uses the same declared quantity, in place of the first material.

### PHASE B — BLEND AND STRAIN

#### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| garam-masala | [Garam masala](ref:ingredient/garam-masala) | 2 g | 2.00% | Garam masala. |

#### Method

1. Blend until smooth.
2. Pass through a fine sieve, pressing firmly on the solids.
3. Return to the heat and simmer to a pourable chutney consistency.
4. Stir in garam masala, cool rapidly, and refrigerate.

Commercial tamarind concentrates vary sharply in strength and acidity.
The chutney thickens as it cools. Stop the reduction while it is slightly looser than the target texture.

#### Phase Outputs Used

| --- | --- |
| Softened date mixture | Use `softened-date-mixture`. Blend and strain all. |
