## Executive answer

The 302 generated placeholders are evidence, not knowledge. On the settled domain boundaries and current direct Recipe usage, **203 observed labels are strong enough to enter a human Curation queue**: 45 Technique candidates, 36 Principle candidates, and 122 Ingredient candidates. The remaining **99 placeholders should not become subject identities or Knowledge Notes in their current form**: 5 aliases, 34 duplicates, 2 equipment entries, 4 quantity fragments, 23 process-state fragments, 19 compound labels, and 12 other artifacts.

**One direct Recipe use is sufficient evidence to queue a Knowledge Candidate. It is never sufficient evidence to perform Curation or create a Knowledge Note.** Recurrence only prioritizes Curator attention; it does not confer authority, establish identity, or prove that the generated label chose the right type or scope. This follows the settled rule that only a Curator resolves Curation and that generation/frequency may inform but cannot perform it ([`CONTEXT.md:101-119`](../../CONTEXT.md), [`CONTEXT.md:213-228`](../../CONTEXT.md)).

The result is therefore a migration disposition, not an automated content promotion. Candidate rows mean “retain the observation and queue it”; every merge, alias, normalized name, split component, or new subject remains provisional until Curator acceptance.

## Method

### Fixed universe and provenance

The inventory is every direct `generated: true` Markdown file under `techniques/`, `principles/`, and `ingredients/`, excluding each folder README. That yields 64 + 50 + 188 = 302. Each exhaustive row below links to its source placeholder. Those source files are generator-owned stubs, not Knowledge Notes: the generator writes the same minimal Purpose and Recipe backlinks and may overwrite or prune them ([`scripts/generate_crosslinks.py:267-314`](../../scripts/generate_crosslinks.py)).

Recipe usage was re-derived read-only from all 31 current direct Recipe Markdown files rather than trusting stale `Used In Recipes` blocks. The audit uses the repository generator’s exact parsing seams: `techniques` and `principles` flow-list frontmatter, component-table ingredients, and `primary_ingredient`, de-duplicated per Recipe ([`scripts/generate_crosslinks.py:40-89`](../../scripts/generate_crosslinks.py), [`scripts/generate_crosslinks.py:150-260`](../../scripts/generate_crosslinks.py), [`scripts/generate_crosslinks.py:363-401`](../../scripts/generate_crosslinks.py)). A displayed count is therefore the number of distinct current Recipes in which that normalized label is directly observed, not the number of table rows or stale backlinks.

Current evidence tiers are **105 recurrent labels** used by at least two distinct Recipes, **196 singletons**, and **one orphan**. Recurrence only affects queue priority: it can amplify systematic noise such as `Baking Paper`, while one use can be excellent signal for a specific material such as `Achiote Paste`.

Nineteen Recipes currently contain old generated Related Links blocks and twelve do not; 80 of the 302 stored backlink sets differ from current source usage. A fresh read-only extraction sees 101 Technique labels, 79 Principle labels, and 277 Ingredient labels. Of those, 64 + 50 + 187 match the 302-placeholder universe; **156 additional current labels (37 + 29 + 90) have no placeholder and are outside this ticket’s fixed inventory**. `Square Pan` is the sole placeholder with no current direct extraction: its legacy backlink survives, while the current parser strips the parenthetical source text to `Pan`. Those 156 labels require a later candidate-ingestion pass; silently adding them here would make the stated 302 impossible to audit.

### Classification tests and precedence

The tests come directly from the settled glossary:

- An **Ingredient** is a culinary material independent of amount, preparation, timing, and purpose; those belong to an Ingredient Use. A same-Recipe intermediate is a Phase Output, never an Ingredient ([`CONTEXT.md:57-75`](../../CONTEXT.md), [`CONTEXT.md:187-198`](../../CONTEXT.md)).
- A **Technique** is an intentional repeatable practice; phase-specific controls and purpose belong to its Technique Application ([`CONTEXT.md:77-85`](../../CONTEXT.md), [`CONTEXT.md:199-204`](../../CONTEXT.md)).
- A **Principle** must be a causal explanation that predicts how conditions influence outcomes. A method, outcome label, broad theme, or tip is not enough ([`CONTEXT.md:87-89`](../../CONTEXT.md)).
- A concept cannot share one identity across Technique and Principle types, and every accepted subject receives a typed immutable identity only after Curation ([`CONTEXT.md:205-211`](../../CONTEXT.md), [`docs/adr/0002-decouple-culinary-identity-from-presentation.md`](../adr/0002-decouple-culinary-identity-from-presentation.md)).

Rows receive one exclusive bucket using this precedence: non-food equipment; relative quantity/allocation fragments; Phase Output or preparation/state fragments; multi-subject compound labels; true aliases; duplicate/narrow application labels; other wrong-type or underspecified artifacts; otherwise Curation candidate. “Alias” is reserved for an alternate name; “duplicate” covers a redundant node caused by a qualifier or application context. Both merge without owning a Stable Identity or Knowledge Note.

### Compound-label migration rule

No compound label becomes an identity or Alias. For `and` / `+`, migration authors one Ingredient Use per actual material. For `or` / `/`, the Curator must establish which material the Canonical Recipe actually means, or explicitly model supported alternatives without making one Culinary Reference target multiple identities. Every legitimate component already represented by a candidate merges to that candidate; every legitimate missing component is queued as a **new, separate Knowledge Candidate** with the compound row retained as provenance. Curation is still human-controlled for those new candidates.
