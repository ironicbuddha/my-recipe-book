## Core Mechanism

Starch dispersed in cooking water helps suspend fat into a cohesive sauce.

## Conditions and Controls

Starch concentration, agitation, fat, and water temperature control stability.

## Culinary Implications

Combine starchy water and fat gradually while tossing to make a glossy sauce.
