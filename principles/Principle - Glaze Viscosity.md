## Core Mechanism

A glaze coats food when dissolved solids and reduction create suitable viscosity.

## Conditions and Controls

Heat, reduction, sugar, starch, and water content control thickness.

## Culinary Implications

Reduce until the glaze clings, then avoid overcooking it into a sticky or burnt coating.
