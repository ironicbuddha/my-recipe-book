## Core Mechanism

Dry skin loses moisture and becomes crisp when its fat renders under heat.

## Conditions and Controls

Dryness, surface exposure, heat, and rendered fat determine crispness.

## Culinary Implications

Dry skin thoroughly and finish at sufficient heat for a brittle crackling layer.
