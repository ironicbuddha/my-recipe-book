## Core Mechanism

Molasses contributes both dark sweetness and bitterness; other sweet and acidic ingredients change how that intensity is perceived.

## Conditions and Controls

Cowboy Beans measures molasses with dark brown sugar, apple cider vinegar, mustard, and tomato, then reduces the sauce to a spoon-coating endpoint.

## Culinary Implications

The balance should remain savoury and rounded after reduction rather than bitter or candy-sweet.
