## Core Mechanism

Cream can separate under excessive or uneven heat, leaving a grainy fat-and-liquid appearance.

## Conditions and Controls

Gratin Dauphinois uses a 170 C oven without fan, loose foil during the first bake, and gentle optional warming of 35% cream before assembly.

## Culinary Implications

A stable cream phase helps the potato gratin bake evenly and remain cohesive; if it splits, lower or even out the heat.
