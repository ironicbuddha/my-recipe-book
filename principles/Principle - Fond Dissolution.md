## Core Mechanism

Water-based cooking liquid dissolves flavourful browned deposits left on the pot after meat and aromatics brown.

## Conditions and Controls

The Spanish stew reduces red wine to a glaze, then adds tomatoes and other liquids while stirring the pot. Avoid burning the fond before liquid arrives.

## Culinary Implications

The dissolved deposits join the stew sauce, carrying the earlier chicken and chorizo browning into the finished dish.
