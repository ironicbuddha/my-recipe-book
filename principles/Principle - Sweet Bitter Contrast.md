## Core Mechanism

Relative honey sweetness and coffee bitterness shape the balance of the Manchego plate.

## Conditions and Controls

Control coffee dose and particle size; keep the dusting restrained and the honey aromatic.

## Culinary Implications

If coffee dominates, reduce the dust and remove coarse particles rather than changing the plate into a coffee crust.
