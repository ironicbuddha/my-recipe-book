## Core Mechanism

Heat softens and melts solid animal fat, allowing it to separate from the
surrounding tissue and become available as a cooking medium.

## Conditions and Controls

Moderate heat gives fat time to release before proteins and sugars on the solids
burn. The ingredient's fat content, cut size, pan contact, and retained moisture
change the rate and completeness of rendering.

## Culinary Implications

Controlled rendering creates a flavour-carrying medium and can crisp the
remaining solids. Excessive heat can scorch the solids before enough fat releases,
while retaining too much rendered fat can make the dish heavy.
