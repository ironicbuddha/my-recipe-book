## Core Mechanism

Adding water to pressed concentrate reduces its concentration while increasing beverage mass.

## Conditions and Controls

Measure actual concentrate recovery and final mass. Added water does not remove suspended fines.

## Culinary Implications

Use bypass water to set Competition Cup final mass and sensory strength; do not describe it as physical filtration or proven extraction improvement.
