## Core Mechanism

Dose and particle distribution influence whether coffee reads as roast complexity or dominant bitterness.

## Conditions and Controls

Use a fine, sifted powder and a restrained pinch; the source records no measured sensory threshold.

## Culinary Implications

Judge the plate as authored and reduce coffee if it becomes the dominant flavour; do not claim measured threshold evidence.
