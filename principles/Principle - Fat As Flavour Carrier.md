## Core Mechanism

Fat dissolves and distributes many aromatic compounds that water cannot carry well.

## Conditions and Controls

Fat type, quantity, heat, and emulsification affect flavour distribution.

## Culinary Implications

Use sufficient fat to carry aroma while balancing richness in the finished dish.
