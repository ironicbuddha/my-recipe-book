## Core Mechanism

Water ratio, grind, temperature, agitation, and contact time affect the coffee compounds entering the brew.

## Conditions and Controls

Keep the two AeroPress profiles distinct. Beverage strength and extraction yield are different quantities; dilution alone does not establish a higher extraction yield.

## Culinary Implications

Use each source Recipe corrective controls for sour, flat, or harsh cups without treating either profile as experimentally proven.
