## Purpose

Disperse ground spices through hot fat and develop aroma before adding wet ingredients.

## Controls

Cowboy Beans blooms spices for 30–60 s; the Spanish stew uses 60–90 s with tomato paste; Bredie blooms gently after onions and garlic. Heat and time must stop short of scorching.

## Process

1. Heat the fat and cook the source aromatics.
2. Add the measured ground spices and stir briefly until fragrant.
3. Add the next wet ingredient before the spices burn.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dusty or burnt flavour | Spices were not bloomed or were left too long in hot fat | Stir over controlled heat and add the next ingredient promptly. |
