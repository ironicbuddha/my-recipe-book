## Purpose

Develop crisp roast-potato edges after parboiling, steam-drying and roughing.

## Controls

The recorded potato procedure preheats to 200 C, uses a loose single layer and turns every 8 min–10 min. Its 25 min–35 min cook ends at deeply golden and crisp; load and potato variety affect timing.

## Process

1. Parboil, drain, steam-dry and rough the potatoes before coating with the selected fat.
2. Preheat the air fryer and arrange a loose single layer; cook in two batches if necessary.
3. Turn at the recorded intervals and stop at the crisp endpoint before adding finishing salt and pepper.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soft crust | Crowded basket or wet potatoes | Steam-dry and cook in a loose single layer. |
