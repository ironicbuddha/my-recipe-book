## Purpose

Concentrate a liquid by evaporation to intensify flavour and change its body.

## Controls

Control pan surface area, heat, starting saltiness, and endpoint viscosity.

## Process

1. Simmer the liquid in an uncovered pan, stirring as it thickens, and stop before it becomes excessively salty.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overly salty sauce | The liquid was reduced too far. | Add unsalted liquid or stop the reduction earlier next time. |
