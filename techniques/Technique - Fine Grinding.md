## Purpose

Produce very fine coffee powder for a restrained seasoning.

## Controls

Use 2.5 g medium-roast beans; approach a powder texture and sift before service.

## Process

1. Grind the beans finely, then pass the powder through a fine sieve.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coarse particles dominate the bite | The grind was uneven or too coarse. | Grind more finely and sift out coarse particles. |
