---
title: "Dry Brining"
identity: technique/dry-brining
---

## Purpose

Season food with salt in advance so it distributes inward and improves flavour and moisture retention.

## Controls

Control salt amount, contact time, exposed surface, and refrigeration.

## Process

1. Apply measured salt evenly, refrigerate uncovered or loosely covered for the planned time, then cook without rinsing unless specified.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Uneven seasoning | Salt was applied inconsistently or held too briefly. | Weigh the salt, distribute it evenly, and allow adequate time. |
