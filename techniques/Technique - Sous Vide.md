## Purpose

Cook sealed food in a precisely controlled water bath to reach a uniform target temperature.

## Controls

Control bath temperature, time, bag seal, and cooling or finishing sequence.

## Process

1. Seal the prepared food, cook at the target temperature for the planned duration, dry it, and finish with a suitable high-heat step if needed.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Watery finish | The cooked food was not dried before finishing. | Drain and dry thoroughly before any sear or glaze. |
