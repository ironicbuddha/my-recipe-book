## Purpose

Season and lightly preserve produce with a brief acid-and-salt brine while retaining fresh texture.

## Controls

Control acid-to-water ratio, salt, sugar, cut size, and steeping time.

## Process

1. Slice the produce evenly, cover with a balanced brine, and chill until seasoned but still crisp.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flabby vegetables | The pieces sat too long or were cut too thin. | Shorten the steep and use thicker, uniform cuts. |
