## Purpose

Bring dried beans to a just-tender state before they enter a sweet and acidic sauce.

## Controls

Cowboy Beans covers 112.5 g dried beans with about three times their volume of water, soaks 8–12 h, then simmers in fresh water for 30–45 min. Tenderness is the endpoint.

## Process

1. Cover dried beans with cold water and soak.
2. Drain, add fresh water, and simmer until just tender.
3. Drain while reserving bean liquid for the later sauce.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hard beans in finished sauce | Beans were underhydrated before the sauce stage | Continue the plain-water simmer until just tender before combining. |
