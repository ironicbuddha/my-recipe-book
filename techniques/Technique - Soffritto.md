## Purpose

Build a softened aromatic base before the main liquid and pulses enter a soup.

## Controls

Fakes cooks onion and carrot in olive oil for 5–7 min until softened and lightly translucent, then garlic for about 30 s. Avoid browning the garlic.

## Process

1. Heat olive oil over medium heat.
2. Cook diced onion and carrot until softened.
3. Add garlic briefly before tomatoes and lentils.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Harsh garlic flavour | Garlic cooked too long before liquid was added | Add garlic only near the end of the aromatic stage. |
