## Purpose

Combine ingredients into a uniform paste, sauce, or liquid with a controlled texture.

## Controls

Control blade speed, duration, batch size, and added liquid so the mixture moves without overheating.

## Process

1. Add ingredients in an order that keeps the blades moving, then blend only until the required texture is reached.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grainy paste | Pieces were too large or the batch was too dry. | Scrape down, add measured liquid if appropriate, and blend in short intervals. |
