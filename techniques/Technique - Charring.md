identity: technique/charring

## Purpose

Create selected darkened areas with very high heat for bitter-sweet roasted flavour while preserving the ingredient's structure.

## Controls

Control distance from heat, turning frequency, and the proportion of char so darkening remains intentional.

## Process

1. Expose dry surfaces to intense heat, turn as blistering develops, and remove once the desired char is reached.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Acrid bitterness | Too much of the surface burned. | Use higher heat for less time and turn before the char spreads. |
