## Purpose

Cook dry beans in their declared liquid and aromatic base before open-pot finishing.

## Controls

Use the equipment's high-pressure setting: 35 min for the recorded black beans or 40 min for charro beans. Allow 20 min natural release before releasing remaining pressure. No calibrated pressure measurement was supplied.

## Process

1. Load the declared beans, liquid and aromatics, following the equipment instructions.
2. Cook at the Recipe's specified high-pressure setting and timing.
3. Allow the stated natural release, release remaining pressure according to equipment instructions, then open and reduce or finish as recorded.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beans not ready for finishing | Bean condition or equipment response differs | Check the bean endpoint before reducing the liquor; follow the equipment instructions. |
