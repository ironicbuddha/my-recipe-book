## Purpose

Use potato during the final simmer to tighten Tomato Bredie gravy.

## Controls

Bredie adds 150 g chunked potato after the lamb softens, then simmers uncovered for 30–40 min. The sauce should become thick and clingy, not soupy.

## Process

1. Add the chunked potatoes to the softened lamb stew.
2. Simmer uncovered until the potatoes are cooked and the gravy clings.
3. Adjust the endpoint by continued gentle reduction rather than asserting a rice-like absorption mechanism.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Watery gravy | Potatoes or uncovered simmer had too little time | Continue the gentle uncovered simmer to the clingy endpoint. |
