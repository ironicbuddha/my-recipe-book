## Purpose

Wet and distribute coffee grounds with bounded stirring.

## Controls

Stir Competition Cup gently for 5–10 s; Everyday Cup specifies only a brief wetting stir.

## Process

1. Stir to wet the grounds without extending or intensifying agitation beyond the Recipe.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Muddy or harsh cup | Agitation exceeded the authored control. | Reduce stirring as the Recipe directs. |
