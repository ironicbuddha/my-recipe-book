## Purpose

Arrange thin potato slices so cream and seasoning reach the gratin evenly.

## Controls

Gratin Dauphinois uses 2–3 mm waxy-potato slices, the approved salt-pepper-garlic mixture between layers, and cream poured until just covered.

## Process

1. Butter the shallow dish.
2. Arrange the potato slices tightly in even layers, distributing the seasoning mixture between them.
3. Pour cream until just covered and tap to release trapped air.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unevenly seasoned or raw centre | Layers were uneven or seasoning was concentrated at one point | Keep slice thickness and seasoning distribution consistent. |
