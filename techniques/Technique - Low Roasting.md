## Purpose

Roast at a moderate, steady temperature to cook a large item evenly and retain a tender interior.

## Controls

Control oven temperature, internal temperature, airflow, and resting time.

## Process

1. Roast until the centre approaches its target temperature, then rest before carving or a separate crisping finish.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dry exterior | The roast cooked too long before reaching its target. | Use a probe thermometer and remove at the planned internal temperature. |
