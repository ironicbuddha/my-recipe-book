## Purpose

Use a short high-heat finish to dry and crisp an exterior without overcooking the interior.

## Controls

Control surface dryness, oven or grill temperature, distance from heat, and finishing time.

## Process

1. Dry the surface, raise the heat for the final stage, and watch closely until crisp.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soft crust | The surface was wet or the finishing heat was insufficient. | Dry the surface thoroughly and finish at a higher temperature. |
