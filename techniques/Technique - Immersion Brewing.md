## Purpose

Brew coffee by keeping the grounds in contact with the declared water before pressing.

## Controls

Preserve each Recipe dose, water mass, temperature, setup orientation, agitation, and steep time.

## Process

1. Assemble the specified brewer, add coffee and water, wet the grounds, steep to the authored time, then press.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sour or harsh brew | Grind or contact controls differed from the Recipe. | Restore the Recipe controls and use its stated corrective adjustments. |
