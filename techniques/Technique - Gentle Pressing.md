## Purpose

Press brewed coffee with controlled, steady pressure.

## Controls

Competition Cup takes 20–30 s; Everyday Cup takes 30 s and stops when resistance spikes.

## Process

1. Flip or position the brewer as authored and press gently for its stated duration and stop condition.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Muddy mouthfeel or bitter finish | Pressing was too fast or continued beyond the stated stop. | Slow the press and respect the Recipe endpoint. |
