## Purpose

Finish browned chicken in a seasoned tomato liquid so meat becomes tender and sauce tightens.

## Controls

The Spanish stew covers chicken at a gentle simmer for 30–45 min, heats drained beans only for the final 8–10 min, and uncovers near the end if the sauce needs tightening.

## Process

1. Build the aromatic, spice, wine, and tomato base.
2. Return browned chicken and reserved chorizo, cover, and simmer gently.
3. Add drained beans near the end and uncover briefly if needed.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dry chicken or broken beans | A hard boil or early bean addition | Keep a gentle simmer and add beans only near service. |
