## Purpose

Add an acid near service so its brightness remains perceptible in the finished dish.

## Controls

Fakes removes the soup from heat before stirring in 10 g lemon juice or red wine vinegar. Taste after the main simmer because the broth has already concentrated.

## Process

1. Finish the soup cooking and take the pot off heat.
2. Stir in the measured acid, taste, and serve with the raw olive-oil drizzle.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dull finish | Acid added too early and cooked through the simmer | Add the finishing acid after heat is removed. |
