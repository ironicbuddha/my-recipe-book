---
title: "Ice-bath shocking"
identity: technique/ice-bath-shocking
---

## Purpose

Stop carryover cooking rapidly and preserve or set a cooked surface texture.

## Controls

Use sufficient ice and water to keep the bath cold for the complete stated time, and submerge the food fully.

## Process

1. Prepare an ice-water bath before cooking finishes.
2. Transfer the food directly into the bath.
3. Chill for the specified duration, then drain before serving or further cooking.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm or flabby surface | The bath warmed too quickly or was too brief. | Add more ice and fully submerge for the stated time. |
