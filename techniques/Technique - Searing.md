---

## Purpose

Apply brief, intense direct heat to form a browned exterior while keeping the interior at its intended doneness.

## Controls

Control surface dryness, contact heat, duration, and resting temperature; use short exposure to limit overcooking.

## Process

1. Dry and season the food, heat the cooking surface, sear each side briefly, then rest or finish as required.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grey band | The food spent too long over high heat. | Increase surface heat and shorten the sear. |
