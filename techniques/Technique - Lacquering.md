## Purpose

Build a glossy, flavourful coating by applying and setting thin layers of a reduced glaze.

## Controls

Control glaze thickness, heat, application frequency, and turning so layers set without burning.

## Process

1. Brush a thin layer onto hot food, let it set, then repeat until the coating is even.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Burnt glaze | The glaze was too thick or heat was too aggressive. | Thin the glaze or apply smaller layers over gentler heat. |
