## Purpose

Coat rested chicken with warm glossy sauce.

## Controls

Brush warm sauce on once, or coat over low heat for 2–3 min; serve extra sauce separately.

## Process

1. Prepare and rest the chicken, warm the sauce, then brush or coat immediately before service.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Softened skin | Sauce was applied too early. | Glaze after the rest and just before serving. |
