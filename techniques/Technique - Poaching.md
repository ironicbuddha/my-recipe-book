identity: technique/poaching
---

## Purpose

Cook food gently in a surrounding liquid while retaining a tender texture.

## Controls

Keep the liquid below a rolling boil and allow residual heat to finish the food when the method calls for it.

## Process

1. Bring the flavoured liquid close to a boil.
2. Add the food, return the liquid only briefly to the boil, then reduce to a bare simmer.
3. Hold for the stated time and rest in the liquid where specified.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dry, stringy meat | The liquid boiled aggressively. | Reduce to a bare simmer and shorten the active cooking time. |
