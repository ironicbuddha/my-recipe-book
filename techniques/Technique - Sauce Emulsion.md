## Purpose

Combine fat- and water-based elements into a cohesive sauce.

## Controls

Use the stated temperature and mix promptly so the fat disperses evenly rather than separating.

## Process

1. Prepare the water-based and fat-based elements.
2. Add the fat at the stated temperature.
3. Stir or blend until the sauce is cohesive.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oil pools on the sauce | The mixture was too cool or not mixed promptly. | Restore the stated temperature and mix immediately. |
