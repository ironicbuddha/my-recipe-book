## Purpose

Cook lentils gently until tender while allowing a soup broth to thicken slightly.

## Controls

Fakes brings lentils, tomatoes, aromatics, and liquid to a boil, then simmers gently for 35–45 min, uncovered or partly covered. Add water only if a looser texture is needed.

## Process

1. Bring the assembled soup to a boil.
2. Reduce to a gentle simmer and stir occasionally.
3. Stop when lentils are tender and broth has thickened slightly.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hard lentils or thin broth | The simmer stopped before the texture endpoint | Continue gentle cooking and reassess tenderness and liquid. |
