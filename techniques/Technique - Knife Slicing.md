## Purpose

Prepare thin celery slices and apple matchsticks for the slaw.

## Controls

Slice celery thinly on a strong bias and julienne apples immediately before dressing.

## Process

1. Cut celery across its fibres on a bias, julienne the apples, and assemble close to service.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stringy celery or softened apple | Celery cuts were thick or apples were prepared too early. | Cut thinly and prepare the apple at assembly. |
