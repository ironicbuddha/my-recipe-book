## Purpose

Transfer soluble flavour and aroma from an ingredient into a liquid or fat.

## Controls

Control temperature, steeping time, ingredient size, and straining so extraction is balanced.

## Process

1. Warm the carrier as appropriate, add the flavouring ingredient, steep until aromatic, then strain or use as the recipe requires.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Harsh flavour | The infusion was overheated or steeped too long. | Lower the temperature and shorten the steeping time. |
