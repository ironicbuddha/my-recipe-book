## Purpose

Distribute the authored salt dose evenly over the food.

## Controls

Apply 12 g salt to 1000 g dried chicken thighs with the stated pepper and 20–30 min rest.

## Process

1. Pat the chicken dry, season evenly, and keep the authored rest before searing.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Uneven seasoning | Salt was distributed inconsistently. | Season all pieces evenly using the declared dose. |
