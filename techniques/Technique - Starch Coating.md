## Purpose

Apply a thin starch layer to create a crisp surface or help sauce cling after cooking.

## Controls

Control starch amount, food dryness, coating evenness, and cooking heat.

## Process

1. Dry the food, toss lightly with starch, shake off excess, and cook promptly.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gummy coating | Too much starch was applied or it hydrated before cooking. | Use a thinner coating and cook immediately after dusting. |
