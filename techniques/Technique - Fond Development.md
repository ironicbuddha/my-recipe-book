## Purpose

Build browned deposits in the cooking vessel that can later flavour a sauce.

## Controls

Cowboy Beans browns beef after rendering bacon; Bredie browns lamb neck in batches. Avoid crowding so meat browns rather than steams.

## Process

1. Render or heat the specified cooking fat.
2. Brown the meat in batches until deep colour forms on both meat and pot.
3. Keep the pot deposits for the subsequent aromatic and liquid stages.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pale, weak sauce base | Meat crowded the pot and released too much moisture | Brown smaller batches before adding wet ingredients. |
