## Purpose

Separate fine coffee dust from coarse particles.

## Controls

Choose a fine sieve and retain only the fine fraction for the plate.

## Process

1. Sift the ground coffee and use the fine dust sparingly.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Concentrated bitter hits | Coarse particles remained. | Sift again and reduce the dusting. |
