title: "Browning"
identity: technique/browning
---

## Purpose

Develop colour and savoury flavour on a food surface before or during subsequent cooking.

## Controls

Control surface dryness, pan heat, fat amount, and crowding so the food contacts the pan without steaming.

## Process

1. Preheat the pan, dry the food, arrange it with space, and turn after a brown crust forms.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pale surface | The pan was cool or overcrowded. | Cook in smaller batches over higher heat after preheating. |
