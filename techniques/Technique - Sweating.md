## Purpose

Soften aromatic vegetables gently in fat without browning them.

## Controls

Control low heat, salt, covered time, and stirring frequency so moisture releases slowly.

## Process

1. Warm fat over low heat, add the aromatics with a pinch of salt, and cook until translucent and soft.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brown edges | The heat was too high or the pan became dry. | Lower the heat and add a small amount of fat or liquid. |
