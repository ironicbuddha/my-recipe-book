title: "Rolling and Tying"

## Purpose

Shape a filled or layered cut into a compact, even roast and secure it so the
form holds during cooking and slicing.

## Controls

Roll with even tension, distribute the filling without gaps, and tie at regular
intervals tightly enough to hold the shape without cutting deeply into the meat.

## Process

1. Arrange the filling and meat so the intended outer surface is exposed.
2. Roll the meat into an even cylinder or parcel.
3. Tie along its length to hold the shape through cooking and resting.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Uneven slices | The roll was loose or irregular. | Re-roll with even tension and add evenly spaced ties. |
| Filling escapes | The ends or seam were not secured. | Tuck the ends and tie the seam before cooking. |
