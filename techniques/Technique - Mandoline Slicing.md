## Purpose

Make repeatable thin slices for an even cooking and setting endpoint.

## Controls

The Gratin source specifies 2–3 mm waxy-potato slices. Do not rinse them afterward because the source relies on surface starch.

## Process

1. Set the mandoline to the specified thickness and use its hand guard.
2. Slice potatoes uniformly and keep the slices unrinsed for assembly.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thick slices remain firm | Slice thickness varied or exceeded the specified range | Reset the mandoline and check a slice before processing the full batch. |
