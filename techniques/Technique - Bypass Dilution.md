title: "Bypass Dilution"
identity: technique/bypass-dilution
---

## Purpose

Adjust pressed coffee strength by adding water after extraction.

## Controls

Use actual concentrate mass and add water to the approved 200–220 g final Competition beverage.

## Process

1. Weigh the pressed concentrate, add hot water to the target final mass, and swirl once.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wrong final strength or mass | Input water was mistaken for recovered concentrate. | Measure the actual concentrate and final cup rather than assuming complete recovery. |
