---

## Purpose

Wake the aroma of whole spices before grinding and adding them to a braise.

## Controls

Tomato Bredie dry-toasts coriander seed and cloves just until fragrant; cinnamon stays whole and is added later. Stop before the spices scorch.

## Process

1. Heat coriander seed and cloves in a dry pan until fragrant.
2. Grind coarsely and reserve the specified portion of coriander for finishing.

## Failure Modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bitter spice flavour | Whole spices scorched during toasting | Use moderate heat and stop as soon as the spices become fragrant. |
