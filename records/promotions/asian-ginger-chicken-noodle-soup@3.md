## Rationale

Carlo explicitly accepted this exact Recipe Version after reviewing the
Completed whole-Recipe Experiment and its limitations. Four additional whole
chicken wings, added from the start and discarded after stock extraction,
gave the finished soup better mouthfeel. The additional fat was not enough
to require removal. The remaining ingredients, quantities, and procedure
were unchanged from Canonical Recipe v2.

Version 3 also describes the existing gentle stock simmer as only a few
bubbles breaking the surface, making the clarity control easier to follow.
Promote v3 and preserve v2 unchanged as referenceable history.

## Known Shortcomings

- The wings were counted, not weighed. The Recipe specifies 4 whole wings
  per 1000 g chicken; their retrospective estimated mass is 200–300 g total.
  Differences in wing size may affect stock body and fat.
- Collagen content and stock clarity were not measured. Better mouthfeel
  and acceptable additional fat are the Curator's qualitative observations.
- The cooking date was not recorded. The Experiment was documented
  retrospectively on 2026-10-01, with a retrospectively stated hypothesis.
