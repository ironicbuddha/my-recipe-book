## Rationale

Carlo accepted this subject and the substantive scope on 2026-10-02 as part of
the concrete 63-Ingredient and three-Technique set. Establish this material
without merging distinct alternatives or creating permanent aliases.

Grated nutmeg is an aromatic spice used in two separate lasagna preparations.
Add 0.5 g to the ragù with its simmering ingredients and 1 g to the béchamel at seasoning.
Keep those Uses and stages separate rather than combine all nutmeg into one component.
