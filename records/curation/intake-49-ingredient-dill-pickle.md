Carlo accepted this subject and the substantive scope on 2026-10-02 as part of
the concrete 63-Ingredient and three-Technique set. Establish this material
without merging distinct alternatives or creating permanent aliases.

Prepared pickled cucumber contributes pickle flavour and small textured pieces to burger sauce.
Dice finely and fold through the completely cooled tomato base.
It is a cold finishing ingredient, not fresh cucumber simmered into the tomato reduction.
