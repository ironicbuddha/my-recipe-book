Carlo accepted this subject and the substantive scope on 2026-10-02 as part of
the concrete 63-Ingredient and three-Technique set. Establish this material
without merging distinct alternatives or creating permanent aliases.

A whole vanilla pod supplies seeds and an infusion in the vanilla custard.
Split and scrape into milk, add the pod, heat to 70 C and infuse covered for 30 min.
Remove the pod before cooling, aging and churning; retain seed specks without adding a decorative pod at service.
