## Rationale

Carlo accepted this subject and the substantive scope on 2026-10-02 as part of
the concrete 63-Ingredient and three-Technique set. Establish this material
without merging distinct alternatives or creating permanent aliases.

The cheese-sauce formulation uses sodium citrate to support a smooth cheese emulsion in milk.
Dissolve the recorded 10 g in milk at 75 C–80 C before gradual grated-cheese addition.
Smooth each addition before the next and reheat gently. This material-specific practice does not authorize an unrelated cream-emulsion Principle.
