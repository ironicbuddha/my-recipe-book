## Rationale

Carlo accepted this subject and the substantive scope on 2026-10-02 as part of
the concrete 63-Ingredient and three-Technique set. Establish this material
without merging distinct alternatives or creating permanent aliases.

Milk chocolate is the chocolate material in the malted ice cream, distinct from dark chocolate.
Retain Lindt as Use-level product specificity; chop and pour the hot dairy mixture over it.
Rest for 1 min before blending until glossy and homogeneous. Do not replace the separately declared Milo or malted milk powder with more chocolate.
