Carlo accepted this subject and the substantive scope on 2026-10-02 as part of
the concrete 63-Ingredient and three-Technique set. Establish this material
without merging distinct alternatives or creating permanent aliases.

The pizza onion preparation uses balsamic vinegar for sweet acidity at the end of caramelisation.
Add the recorded 15 g after the onion is soft and deeply coloured, then cool the preparation before topping dough.
It deglazes the onion pan; it is not the finishing olive oil or a separate topping sauce.
