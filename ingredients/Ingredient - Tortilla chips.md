## Functional Profile

Prepared crisp corn chips form the two Nachos layers.

## Handling

Distribute toppings across two thinner layers, bake briefly, then add wet garnishes in small spoonfuls.

## Culinary Use

Serve immediately to retain crispness; the source does not call for making chips internally.
