## Functional Profile

Worcestershire sauce is a savoury acidic seasoning in Cowboy Beans and the Spanish stew.

## Handling

It is optional at 2.5 g in Cowboy Beans and measured at 15 ml in Spanish stew; preserve those separate Uses.

## Culinary Use

Add to the wet sauce ingredients so it disperses before the final simmer.
