## Functional Profile

Chicken thigh contains enough fat to remain moist during direct grilling.

## Handling

Use the form specified by each Recipe: boneless for the glazed hibachi tacos,
or bone-in and skin-on for Monkey Gland Chicken. Pat the boneless pieces dry
before their thin starch coating; dry the bone-in thighs and skin thoroughly
before seasoning and searing. Preserve each Recipe's own cooking controls.

## Culinary Use

Supply the 1000 g basis for the glazed hibachi chicken tacos or Monkey Gland
Chicken, retaining their distinct preparations and finishing methods.
