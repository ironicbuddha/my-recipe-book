## Functional Profile

Green beans retain a crisp-tender structure when briefly charred over high
heat.

## Handling

Trim and dry before direct grilling; stop while the centres remain vibrant.

## Culinary Use

The legacy Hibachi Pork source offers green beans as an alternative to the
Curator-selected broccoli in its 300 g greens component.
