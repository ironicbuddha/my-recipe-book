## Functional Profile

Halved sprouts supply browned cut faces and tender interiors beneath the miso-butter coating.

## Handling

Trim and halve, then place cut-side down on the preheated tray at 230 C.

## Culinary Use

Roast for 18 min–25 min until browned and tender before adding the selected butter portion; glazing is a later step.
