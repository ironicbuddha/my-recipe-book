## Functional Profile

This grated cheese is the specified pizza topping, distinct from wet fresh mozzarella.

## Handling

Grate and distribute over proofed dough with the cooled onion and sausage.

## Culinary Use

Preserve the low-moisture product specification and recorded 250 g topping quantity rather than assume a wetter substitution behaves the same.
