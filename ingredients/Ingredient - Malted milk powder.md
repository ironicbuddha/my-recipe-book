## Functional Profile

Malted dairy powder is a distinct material alongside Milo and milk chocolate in the malted ice cream.

## Handling

Dry-mix the 30 g with Milo, sugars, stabiliser and salt before blending.

## Culinary Use

Keep all three chocolate-and-malt materials separate; the source supplies no product nutrition or substitution equivalence.
