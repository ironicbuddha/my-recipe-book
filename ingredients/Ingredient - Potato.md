## Functional Profile

Generic potato is the chunked starch-bearing vegetable added to Tomato Bredie.

## Handling

Add 150 g after the lamb has softened and simmer uncovered for 30–40 min.

## Culinary Use

The potatoes contribute body to the gravy; the source does not specify a waxy variety here.
