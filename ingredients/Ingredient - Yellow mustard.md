## Functional Profile

Yellow mustard is the first-listed mustard option in Cowboy Beans.

## Handling

Add the measured 7.5 g with molasses, sugar, tomato, and vinegar before combining with beans.

## Culinary Use

Dijon mustard remains the explicit alternative with its existing Ingredient identity.
