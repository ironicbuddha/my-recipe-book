## Functional Profile

The source specifies cured Spanish chorizo, whose rendered fat flavours the stew base.

## Handling

Slice 150 g, fry 2–3 min until fat releases and edges crisp, then reserve the slices while browning chicken.

## Culinary Use

Return the reserved chorizo with the chicken before the gentle stew.
