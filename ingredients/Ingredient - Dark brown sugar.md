## Functional Profile

Dark brown sugar adds sweetness and molasses character to the Cowboy Beans sauce.

## Handling

Dissolve the measured 15 g with molasses, mustard, vinegar, and tomato before reducing the pot.

## Culinary Use

It helps balance the source's bitter-sweet molasses profile without replacing the measured molasses.
