## Functional Profile

Brown lentils are the dry pulse forming the body of Fakes soup.

## Handling

Rinse the measured 166.67 g; the source permits a brief optional boil and drain before the main simmer.

## Culinary Use

Simmer with tomatoes and aromatics until tender and the broth thickens slightly, about 35–45 min in the source.
