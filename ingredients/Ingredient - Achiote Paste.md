## Functional Profile

Achiote paste brings earthy, peppery annatto flavour and colour to a marinade.

## Handling

Disperse the paste thoroughly in the wet marinade so it coats the meat evenly.

## Culinary Use

Use in the pork al pastor marinade before the bounded refrigerated rest.
