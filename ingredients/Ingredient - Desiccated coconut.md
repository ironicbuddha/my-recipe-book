## Functional Profile

Dried coconut appears as a toasted finish in the banana side and a finely ground dry addition to rendang.

## Handling

Toast the banana-side coconut to pale gold and cool; preserve the already toasted, finely ground state specified for rendang.

## Culinary Use

Use 60 g in the banana assembly and 20 g on top. Rendang receives its separate 100 g Use during the fry-down.
