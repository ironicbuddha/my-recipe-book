## Functional Profile

Pecorino is the named cheese variation for adding richness to the slaw.

## Handling

Shave before the optional addition; retain Parmesan as the separate named alternative.

## Culinary Use

Add discretionarily rather than assigning an invented baseline dose or substituting it automatically for Parmesan.
