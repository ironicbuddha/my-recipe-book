## Functional Profile

Milo is the branded malted cocoa beverage powder specified alongside chocolate and malted milk powder.

## Handling

Whisk the recorded 45 g with the dry powders before blending into dairy.

## Culinary Use

Retain brand specificity without inventing nutrition values or assuming equivalence to plain cocoa powder.
