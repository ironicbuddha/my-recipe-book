## Functional Profile

Fresh green chilli supplies heat and green material to mint chutney; variety and heat vary.

## Handling

Blend with yoghurt, lime, ginger and seasonings before adding herbs in short pulses.

## Culinary Use

Retain the generic green-chilli specification without assuming a particular jalapeño cultivar or fixed heat level.
