## Functional Profile

This named mild curry blend supplies warm spice to Tomato Bredie without being the dominant flavour.

## Handling

Bloom the measured 2.5 g gently with toasted coriander, cloves, ginger, and garlic; avoid scorching.

## Culinary Use

Preserve the blend name and mild intensity rather than substituting generic curry powder.
