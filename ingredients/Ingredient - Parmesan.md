## Functional Profile

Parmesan adds savoury depth and salt to the firm polenta served with the porcini ragout.

## Handling

Stir the measured 17.5 g, finely grated, into cooked polenta off heat with butter.

## Culinary Use

The source specifies Parmesan; the migrated Recipe should not claim a vegetarian tag on this evidence alone.
