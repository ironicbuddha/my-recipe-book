## Functional Profile

Prepared sliced pickled chilli is the Nachos finishing material, distinct from fresh jalapeño in charro beans.

## Handling

Add the recorded 50 g after baking with the fresh coriander.

## Culinary Use

Late addition preserves the intended cold garnish; its brine is a separate cheese-sauce Ingredient.
