## Functional Profile

Jalapeno is an optional fresh chili in the Cowboy Beans aromatic base.

## Handling

Dice the measured 3.75 g and soften it with onion before garlic and dry spices are added.

## Culinary Use

Omission is an allowed source option; it does not change the identity of the other spices.
