## Functional Profile

Dried ground ginger is the spice material in tamarind chutney, distinct from fresh ginger.

## Handling

Combine the declared 2 g with tamarind, water, dates and other simmering ingredients.

## Culinary Use

Preserve that dose and stage rather than equate it with the same weight of fresh rhizome.
