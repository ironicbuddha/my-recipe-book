## Functional Profile

Curry powder is a variable spice blend used in both chicken curry and tomato chutney.

## Handling

Cook briefly with the aromatics: 20 g in curry, 10 g in chutney, retaining the separate cumin and turmeric Uses.

## Culinary Use

Keep blend strength variability explicit rather than define an unrecorded universal spice mixture.
