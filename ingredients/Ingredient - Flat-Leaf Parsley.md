## Functional Profile

Flat-leaf parsley contributes a fresh, herbaceous aroma and green colour.

## Handling

Wash and dry before chopping. Add at the phase specified so a garnish stays
fresh while the herb stuffing and salsa verde receive the intended flavour.

## Culinary Use

Use in the Hibachi Pork green sauce, sausage garnish, guanciale sauce, and
porchetta stuffing, salsa verde, or slaw where parsley is chosen.
