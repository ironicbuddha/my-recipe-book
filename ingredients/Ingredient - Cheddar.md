## Functional Profile

Cheddar supplies the cheese sauce's body and flavour and is also a grated Nachos topping.

## Handling

Grate finely for sauce and add in four additions, smoothing each before the next. Cheese moisture varies by product.

## Culinary Use

Reheat the sauce gently; the topping melts during the short tray bake. Monterey Jack is a recorded topping alternative, not an alias.
