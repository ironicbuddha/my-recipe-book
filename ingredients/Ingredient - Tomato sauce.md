## Functional Profile

Tomato sauce is the first-listed 30 g tomato option in Cowboy Beans.

## Handling

Add with molasses, sugar, mustard, and vinegar before the beans and beer; stir until glossy.

## Culinary Use

Crushed tomato remains an explicit alternative and is not silently treated as the same prepared sauce.
