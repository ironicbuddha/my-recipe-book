## Functional Profile

Pinto beans are the first-listed dried bean in Cowboy Beans and a tinned alternative in the Spanish stew.

## Handling

For Cowboy Beans, soak 112.5 g dry beans for 8–12 h and simmer until just tender; for Spanish stew, drain the tinned beans and add near the end.

## Culinary Use

Keep dried and tinned forms in their respective Ingredient Uses rather than splitting the same bean into duplicate identities.
