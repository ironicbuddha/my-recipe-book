## Functional Profile

Dextrose is a distinct sugar material in the recorded ice-cream formulations, alongside sucrose.

## Handling

Premix the declared dose with other dry powders before blending into dairy.

## Culinary Use

Retain its separate quantity; the formulations do not authorize replacing it gram-for-gram with sucrose or glucose syrup.
