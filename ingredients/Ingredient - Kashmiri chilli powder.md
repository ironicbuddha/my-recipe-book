## Functional Profile

The named ground chilli material seasons tamarind chutney separately from cumin, ginger and garam masala.

## Handling

Add the recorded 2 g before the date-softening simmer.

## Culinary Use

Preserve the specification rather than silently substituting cayenne or generic chilli powder; product heat varies.
