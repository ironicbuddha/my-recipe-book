## Functional Profile

Aged Manchego is a firm cheese used for its concentrated flavour and crystalline texture against honey and coffee.

## Handling

Retain the source-specified age of at least 12 months; temper for 45–60 min and cut into 5–7 mm wedges.

## Culinary Use

Serve at the authored 18–20 C with warm thyme honey and a restrained coffee dusting.
