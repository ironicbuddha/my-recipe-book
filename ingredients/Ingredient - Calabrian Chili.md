## Functional Profile

Calabrian chili is a named optional source of heat in the slaw.

## Handling

The source does not specify fresh, dried, or prepared form; preserve that uncertainty instead of assuming a paste.

## Culinary Use

Use discretionarily as the source alternative to chili flakes, without inventing a measured addition.
