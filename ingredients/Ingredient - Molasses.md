## Functional Profile

Molasses contributes dark sweetness and bitterness to the Cowboy Beans sauce.

## Handling

Add 20 g with brown sugar, mustard, vinegar, and tomato, then reduce the pot until thick and spoon-coating.

## Culinary Use

Its intensity is balanced by the other measured sweet and acidic ingredients.
