## Functional Profile

Galangal is the separately declared aromatic rhizome in rendang, not a label for the ginger Use.

## Handling

Blend the recorded 50 g with onion, garlic, ginger, chilli, lemongrass and spices.

## Culinary Use

Fry it as part of the complete paste until aromatic and oil begins to separate; do not silently substitute more ginger.
