## Functional Profile

White and pale-green leek layers are the vegetable material in the butter braise.

## Handling

Keep root ends intact, halve lengthwise and wash between layers before draining.

## Culinary Use

Braise gently until completely tender and transfer carefully before glazing; aggressive boiling or handling can separate the layers.
