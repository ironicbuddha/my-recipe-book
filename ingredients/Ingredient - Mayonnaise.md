## Functional Profile

Prepared mayonnaise is the emulsion folded into burger sauce after tomato reduction.

## Handling

Cool the tomato base completely before folding in the recorded 50 g with pickle and mustard.

## Culinary Use

It is a cold finish; the Recipe neither heats it in the reduction nor specifies a homemade mayonnaise procedure.
