## Functional Profile

Glucose syrup is the syrup material in salted caramel ripple, separate from powdered dextrose.

## Handling

Combine the recorded 50 g with sucrose before cooking to dark amber.

## Culinary Use

Keep the product's concentration unspecified: the source records its mass, not solids content or a measured concentration.
