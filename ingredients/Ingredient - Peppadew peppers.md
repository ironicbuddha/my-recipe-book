## Functional Profile

These sweet pickled peppers contribute piquant pieces to the bean-corn salsa.

## Handling

Drain and dice before folding with cooled corn, beans and fresh herbs.

## Culinary Use

Product sweetness varies, so the recorded lime adjustment remains relevant. Preserve the Peppadew specification rather than infer fresh peppers.
