## Functional Profile

Grated citrus peel contributes a separate lime component to crema.

## Handling

Whisk the recorded 2 g into the smooth crème fraîche with lime juice, water and seasonings.

## Culinary Use

Retain zest and juice as separate Uses; zest mass is not interchangeable with juice mass.
