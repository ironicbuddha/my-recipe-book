## Functional Profile

Cultured dairy material contributes the creamy element to mint chutney and banana dressing.

## Handling

Use chilled blending for chutney and gentle folding through the banana dressing.

## Culinary Use

Keep the material plain rather than infer sweetened or flavoured yoghurt; the recorded dairy-free and dry alternatives have no separate testing report.
