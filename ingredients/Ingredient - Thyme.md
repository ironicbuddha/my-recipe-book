## Functional Profile

Thyme supplies an infused base note and a fresh finishing aroma in the porcini ragout.

## Handling

Steep 1 g sprigs with dried porcini, then add 1.5 g fresh leaves near the end of the cream simmer.

## Culinary Use

Sprigs and leaves are forms of one herb identity; their different phase uses remain explicit.
