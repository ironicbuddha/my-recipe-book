## Functional Profile

Ripe but firm banana supplies sweet, soft pieces in the cold curry accompaniment.

## Handling

Slice and fold gently through the lime-yoghurt dressing. Assemble no more than 30 min before service.

## Culinary Use

Firmness matters: keep recognisable pieces instead of mashing the banana into the dressing. The recorded pineapple alternative is separate from the base formulation.
