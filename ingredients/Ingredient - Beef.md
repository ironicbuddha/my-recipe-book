## Functional Profile

Beef is a general meat material, with the cut chosen at the Ingredient Use.

## Handling

The taco Use retains 800 g skirt or flank, marinated under refrigeration and drained before grilling.

## Culinary Use

Retain the recorded 54 C–57 C core endpoint, rest and slice across the grain. Cut-specific and ground-beef subjects remain distinct.
