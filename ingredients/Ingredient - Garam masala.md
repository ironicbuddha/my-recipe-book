## Functional Profile

This spice blend finishes the curry and tamarind chutney; its composition is product-dependent.

## Handling

Add curry's 5 g with lemon juice near the end. Stir chutney's 2 g in after straining and final reduction.

## Culinary Use

Retain finishing placement without inventing a universal blend formula or claiming it was fried with the base spices.
