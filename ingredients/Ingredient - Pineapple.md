## Functional Profile

Pineapple brings fruit sweetness, acidity, and moisture to a salsa.

## Handling

Grill until the edges char, then dice as the al pastor Recipe directs.

## Culinary Use

Use for the 66.67 g charred-pineapple component of the al pastor salsa.
