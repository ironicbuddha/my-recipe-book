## Functional Profile

This prepared smoked chilli includes its adobo sauce, unlike a dry chilli powder.

## Handling

Mince and cook with garlic, cumin and oregano in the charro bean aromatic base.

## Culinary Use

Keep the pepper-and-sauce preparation identifiable. It is also a recorded optional marinade addition for tacos.
