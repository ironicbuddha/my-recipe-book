## Functional Profile

Malt vinegar is one of the recorded acidic liquids for tomato-onion chutney, distinct from apple cider vinegar.

## Handling

Use 80 g of the selected vinegar with tomato and sugar before reduction.

## Culinary Use

The choice remains a Use-level alternative, not an identity merge or new measured acidity specification.
