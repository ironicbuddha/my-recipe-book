## Functional Profile

Chuck is the first-listed beef cut in Cowboy Beans and contributes browned meat to the bean pot.

## Handling

Brown the 75 g portion thoroughly after bacon renders, then cook gently with the beans until tender.

## Culinary Use

Preserve ground beef as the explicit alternative rather than merging the two forms into a generic beef Use.
