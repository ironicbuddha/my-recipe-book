## Functional Profile

Tomato contributes acidity, liquid, and body to Tomato Bredie and is a crushed alternative in Cowboy Beans.

## Handling

The Bredie source specifies 200 g good Italian tinned tomato but not whole or plum form; retain that wording without inferring a variety.

## Culinary Use

Use crushed tomato only where the source names it as an alternative to tomato sauce.
