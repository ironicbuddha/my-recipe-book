## Functional Profile

Courgette supplies tender vegetable flesh and moisture to a charred side.

## Handling

Cut for direct grilling and cook until the surface chars without collapsing
the interior.

## Culinary Use

The legacy Hibachi Pork source offers courgettes as an alternative to the
Curator-selected broccoli in its 300 g greens component.
