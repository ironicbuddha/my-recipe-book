## Functional Profile

Coarse polenta hydrates and thickens into the firm base for the mushroom ragout.

## Handling

Whisk 110 g into boiling water and milk, cook 35–40 min until tender, then chill at least 2 h before cutting and frying.

## Culinary Use

The cooked and set polenta are Phase Outputs; the dry grain remains the Ingredient.
