## Functional Profile

Cinnamon supplies a warm aromatic spice note to the tomato sauce.

## Handling

Use the stick or ground form specified for the Ingredient Use. Remove a whole
stick before serving; ground cinnamon remains dispersed in the sauce.

## Culinary Use

Use in the Italian sausage and Puy lentil Recipe's spiced tomato sauce.
