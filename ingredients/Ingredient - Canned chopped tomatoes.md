## Functional Profile

Chopped canned tomato is the chunky tomato material in burger sauce, distinct from smooth passata or whole canned tomatoes.

## Handling

Simmer uncovered with the aromatic base until thick, then cool completely.

## Culinary Use

Retain the pieces and reduce until free liquid does not pool. The smooth variation is an alternative, not the base texture.
