## Functional Profile

Coconut cooking liquid forms the rich liquid base of both rendang and chicken curry.

## Handling

Keep the curry at a gentle simmer after addition; reduce rendang progressively and stir more often as it dries.

## Culinary Use

The endpoints differ: curry retains gravy, while rendang reaches a dark paste that clings to beef with separated coconut oil.
