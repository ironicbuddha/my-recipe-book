## Functional Profile

Oregano is an optional dried herb in Fakes soup.

## Handling

Add the stated 0.67 g dried form with tomatoes and bay leaves before the lentil simmer.

## Culinary Use

Its omission is allowed without changing the soup's lentil, tomato, and acid-finishing structure.
