## Functional Profile

Chili powder is the prepared spice blend named in Cowboy Beans, distinct from a single ground chili.

## Handling

Add the measured 1.25 g with cumin and paprika; stir in rendered fat for 30–60 s without burning.

## Culinary Use

The blend seasons the meat and aromatic base before the molasses-tomato sauce is added.
