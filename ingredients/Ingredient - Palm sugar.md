## Functional Profile

Palm sugar is the sweetener material in rendang, separate from the jaggery alternative in tamarind chutney.

## Handling

Add the recorded 45 g with coconut milk, tamarind and salt before the slow simmer.

## Culinary Use

Dissolve into the cooking liquid and retain the dry rendang endpoint; do not silently merge distinct sweetener identities.
