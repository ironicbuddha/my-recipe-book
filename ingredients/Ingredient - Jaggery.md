## Functional Profile

Jaggery is a sweetener option in tamarind chutney, distinct from dark brown sugar.

## Handling

Use the declared 100 g in place of dark brown sugar and dissolve during the date-softening simmer.

## Culinary Use

Preserve the alternative and product variability without claiming a universal sweetness equivalence or separate variation trial.
