## Functional Profile

Ground turmeric is a distinct spice in rendang paste, chicken seasoning and tomato chutney.

## Handling

Blend into rendang paste, coat the curry chicken before browning, or cook briefly in the chutney aromatic base as specified.

## Culinary Use

Keep each application and dose local to its Recipe; colour alone does not establish interchangeable spice mixtures.
