## Functional Profile

Bourbon is the specified spirit in the maple-butter pecan coating, distinct from Tennessee whiskey.

## Handling

Remove the pan from heat before adding the recorded 30 g, then return to low heat for 2 min.

## Culinary Use

Fold in toasted pecans and cool completely. Brief cooking is a procedure control, not a measured claim about residual alcohol.
