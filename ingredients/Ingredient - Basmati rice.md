## Functional Profile

Basmati is the first-listed dry rice option served with the Spanish stew.

## Handling

The source measures 160 g dry rice and 320 g water; cook to a tender, separate-grain endpoint.

## Culinary Use

Spoon the finished chicken, chorizo, and bean stew over the cooked rice.
