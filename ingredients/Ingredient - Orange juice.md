## Functional Profile

Orange juice forms the citrus-garlic beef marinade alongside a separate lime juice Use.

## Handling

Combine the recorded 100 g with the marinade ingredients and refrigerate coated beef for 2 h–4 h.

## Culinary Use

Drain excess marinade before grilling; the original input is not a measured retained coating or taco sauce portion.
