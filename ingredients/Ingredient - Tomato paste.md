## Functional Profile

Tomato paste is concentrated tomato used to deepen the Bredie and Spanish stew bases.

## Handling

Cook the measured 15 g briefly with aromatics and spices until slightly darkened, before adding wetter ingredients.

## Culinary Use

This concentrated form is distinct from tinned tomato or tomato sauce.
