## Functional Profile

Prepared smooth cheddar sauce is a companion food in the Nachos layers, distinct from grated topping cheese.

## Handling

Prepare [its Recipe](ref:recipe/nacho-cheese-sauce) separately and reheat gently if chilled.

## Culinary Use

Measure 400 g for the two Nachos layers, retain any remainder and keep the prepared portion separate from raw sauce ingredients.
