## Functional Profile

Commercial instant yeast is the leavening material in the Roman pizza dough; the Recipe does not specify a sourdough culture.

## Handling

Mix the declared 2 g with flour and initial water; preserve the four folds and covered 18 h–24 h refrigeration.

## Culinary Use

Bring dough to room temperature and proof until aerated before topping. Do not infer a yeast-and-lactic-acid culture from cold fermentation alone.
