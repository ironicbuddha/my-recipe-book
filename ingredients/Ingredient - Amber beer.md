## Functional Profile

Amber beer contributes liquid and a fuller malt profile to the Cowboy Beans sauce.

## Handling

Use as the source-named alternative to lager; the recipe measures 62.5 g of beer and adjusts bean liquid to cover.

## Culinary Use

The beer joins the beans after molasses, tomato, mustard, and vinegar are combined.
