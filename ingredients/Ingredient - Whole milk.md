## Functional Profile

Whole milk contributes liquid and dairy fat to the firm polenta.

## Handling

Bring 80 g milk with 280 g water and salt to a low boil before whisking in the coarse polenta.

## Culinary Use

The milk remains part of the polenta base that sets, chills, and is later fried.
