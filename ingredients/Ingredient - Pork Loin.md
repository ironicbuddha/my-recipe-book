## Functional Profile

Pork loin is a relatively lean cut with less fat buffering during high-heat
finishing than a marbled cut.

## Handling

Use only if selecting the loin alternative and track the stated internal
temperature closely during finishing.

## Culinary Use

The legacy Hibachi Pork source offers loin alongside shoulder and collar as a
500 g cut choice; shoulder is the Curator-selected default.
