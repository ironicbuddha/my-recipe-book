## Functional Profile

Prepared cultured-cream condiment is the spoonable or squeezable Nachos finish.

## Handling

Prepare [its Recipe](ref:recipe/creme-fraiche-lime-crema) separately and keep refrigerated before service.

## Culinary Use

Use the selected 150 g after baking, retaining any remainder. Do not count its constituent dairy and seasonings again in Nachos.
