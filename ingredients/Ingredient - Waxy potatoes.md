## Functional Profile

Waxy potatoes are the specified 500 g structural base of Gratin Dauphinois.

## Handling

Slice 2–3 mm thick without rinsing, then layer with the approved salt, white pepper, and garlic mixture before adding cream.

## Culinary Use

Their even slices and retained surface starch help the gratin set while holding a sliceable layered form.
