## Functional Profile

Navy beans are the source-named alternative to dried pinto beans in Cowboy Beans.

## Handling

Soak and simmer to the source's just-tender endpoint before combining with the sauce; timing may depend on the beans.

## Culinary Use

Keep the 112.5 g dry-bean basis and reserve cooking liquid for adjusting the final pot.
