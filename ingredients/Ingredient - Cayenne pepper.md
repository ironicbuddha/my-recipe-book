## Functional Profile

Ground hot pepper contributes heat to the cheese sauce; it is distinct from the smoked paprika in that formulation.

## Handling

Blend the recorded 0.5 g into the cheese emulsion with the other seasonings.

## Culinary Use

Keep this small heat dose separately identifiable rather than substituting it for all paprika.
