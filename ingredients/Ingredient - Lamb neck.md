## Functional Profile

Bone-in lamb neck supplies meat, fat, and connective tissue to Tomato Bredie.

## Handling

Brown the 500 g portion in batches, then simmer covered for 90–120 min until the meat softens and begins to collapse.

## Culinary Use

The long, gentle braise develops a tight stew sauce before potatoes are added.
