## Functional Profile

Beef fillet is the specific steak cut forming the 650 g basis of the reverse-seared plate.

## Handling

Season evenly, roast at 110 C to 46–48 C internally, then finish over the hibachi and rest for 10 min.

## Culinary Use

Use the source final target of 52–54 C; actual thickness and internal temperature govern completion rather than the illustrative timetable.
