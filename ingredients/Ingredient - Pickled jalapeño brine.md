## Functional Profile

The pickling liquid seasons the cheese sauce separately from drained pepper pieces.

## Handling

Blend the recorded 25 g into the smooth cheese emulsion with paprika, garlic powder and cayenne.

## Culinary Use

Preserve the liquid specification; adding sliced peppers would alter the smooth sauce texture.
