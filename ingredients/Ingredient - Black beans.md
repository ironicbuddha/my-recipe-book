## Functional Profile

Black beans are one of the named tinned bean alternatives in the Spanish stew.

## Handling

Drain before use and add only for the final 8–10 min of gentle simmering so the beans remain intact.

## Culinary Use

They replace the first-listed cannellini option at the same stated 240 g drained quantity.
