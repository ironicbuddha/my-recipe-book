## Functional Profile

This small chili provides the heat named in the Spanish chicken and chorizo stew.

## Handling

Mince the measured 8 g and add with paprika, cumin, coriander, and tomato paste; bloom briefly without scorching.

## Culinary Use

Its heat is distributed through the chorizo fat before wine and tomatoes loosen the pot.
