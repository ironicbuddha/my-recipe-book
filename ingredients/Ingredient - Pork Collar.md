## Functional Profile

Pork collar is a marbled cut that supplies pork flavour and fat to a grilled
preparation.

## Handling

Use only if selecting the collar alternative in the Hibachi Pork Recipe and
follow the Recipe's stated temperature and timing controls.

## Culinary Use

The legacy Hibachi Pork source offers collar alongside shoulder and loin as a
500 g cut choice; shoulder is the Curator-selected default.
