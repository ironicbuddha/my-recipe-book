## Functional Profile

Corn supplies sweet pieces and a charred surface to the cold bean salsa.

## Handling

Char in a dry hot cast-iron pan for 4 min–6 min, stirring occasionally, then cool completely.

## Culinary Use

Combine only cooled corn with the mint, coriander and dressed onion to protect the fresh finish.
