## Functional Profile

Prepared bean-corn-herb condiment is a companion food, distinct from its separate raw materials.

## Handling

Prepare [its Recipe](ref:recipe/black-bean-corn-mint-peppadew-salsa) separately, cooling the corn before fresh herbs.

## Culinary Use

Use the selected 250 g portion after Nachos baking and retain any remainder; do not duplicate the preparation inside Nachos.
