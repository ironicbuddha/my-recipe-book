## Functional Profile

Cannellini beans are the first-listed tinned bean in the Spanish stew, contributing a creamy but intact bean texture.

## Handling

Drain the 240 g portion and add for the final 8–10 min at a gentle simmer.

## Culinary Use

Keep kidney, black, and pinto beans as explicit alternatives.
