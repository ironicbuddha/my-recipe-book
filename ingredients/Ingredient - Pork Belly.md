## Functional Profile

Pork belly combines a fatty interior with a skin layer that can form crackling when dried and heated.

## Handling

Keep the skin intact for the porchetta; score the meat side without piercing the skin.

## Culinary Use

Roll around the filling, dry uncovered, roast gently, then crisp at high heat.
