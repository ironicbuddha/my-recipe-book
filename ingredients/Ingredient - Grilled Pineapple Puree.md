## Functional Profile

This prepared pineapple puree contributes fruit sweetness and acidity to the
al pastor marinade.

## Handling

Measure the prepared puree as supplied. Its raw pineapple quantity and
preparation yield are not established by the source Recipe.

## Culinary Use

Use 40 g in the marinade as authored; keep it distinct from the salsa pineapple.
