## Functional Profile

Prepared chicken cooking liquid forms part of the coconut curry gravy.

## Handling

Add the recorded 300 g with coconut milk and return the browned chicken to the pot. Product salt content varies.

## Culinary Use

Retain a gentle simmer and the specified chicken and potato endpoints rather than infer gravy yield from input mass.
