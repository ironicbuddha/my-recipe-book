## Functional Profile

Dry dairy material forms part of the vanilla, maple and salted caramel ice-cream formulations.

## Handling

Premix with dry sugars, stabiliser and salt before dispersion into dairy.

## Culinary Use

Preserve each formulation's declared mass and keep powder distinct from the liquid whole-milk basis.
