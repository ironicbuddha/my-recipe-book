## Functional Profile

Celery leaves are the optional leafy material used alongside the slaw stalks and parsley.

## Handling

Keep the optional 10 g separate from the 300 g stalk basis and reserve with the torn parsley.

## Culinary Use

Add to the chilled slaw assembly without replacing the stalk Ingredient Use.
