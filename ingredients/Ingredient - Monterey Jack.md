## Functional Profile

This cheese is a recorded alternative to cheddar in the grated Nachos topping, not an alias for cheddar.

## Handling

Grate and use the same 150 g declared topping quantity in place of cheddar.

## Culinary Use

Distribute between the two layers and melt during the short bake; this alternative does not replace the cheese in the companion sauce.
