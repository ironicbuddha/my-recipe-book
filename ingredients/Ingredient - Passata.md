## Functional Profile

Smooth tomato product supplies the ragù's tomato liquid, unlike chunky canned tomatoes.

## Handling

Add after the wine has reduced and milk has been absorbed, with stock and seasonings.

## Culinary Use

Reduce gently until rich and spoonable while retaining the source's 400 g input and selected assembly portion.
