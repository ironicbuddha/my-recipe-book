## Functional Profile

Otherwise unspecified white rice is a source-named alternative to basmati and jasmine in the Spanish stew.

## Handling

The source measures 160 g dry rice with 320 g water and 2 g salt; cook to a tender endpoint.

## Culinary Use

Serve the cooked rice under the chicken, chorizo, and bean stew.
