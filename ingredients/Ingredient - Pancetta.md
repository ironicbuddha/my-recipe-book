## Functional Profile

Diced cured pork is the initial fat-and-meat material in ragù.

## Handling

Render over medium-low heat for 5 min before adding olive oil and the diced vegetables.

## Culinary Use

Preserve it separately from bacon or guanciale rather than using a shared cured-pork label to authorize substitution.
