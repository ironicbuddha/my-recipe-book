## Functional Profile

Whole mustard seed supplies the tempered spice element of tomato-onion chutney.

## Handling

Heat in oil until it begins to pop before adding onion and salt.

## Culinary Use

Popping is the recorded cue; this whole-seed application is distinct from Dijon mustard in the cold sauces.
