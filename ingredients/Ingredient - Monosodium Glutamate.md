## Functional Profile

Monosodium glutamate is the discretionary savoury seasoning specified for the finished fries.

## Handling

Use the source pinch alongside salt and smoked paprika after the second fry; no measured dose is inferred.

## Culinary Use

Season the hot fries immediately after draining so the seasoning accompanies their final service.
