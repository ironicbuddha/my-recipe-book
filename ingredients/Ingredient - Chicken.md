## Functional Profile

Chicken is a general meat material; thighs and drumsticks remain cut-specific descriptions rather than a new compound identity.

## Handling

For the curry use 1200 g combined bone-in thighs and drumsticks in flexible proportions, season and brown in batches.

## Culinary Use

Simmer until the chicken reaches at least 74 C and potatoes are tender. This general subject does not retire the existing narrower thigh subject.
