## Functional Profile

Green apple supplies the acidic, crisp secondary structure in the celery slaw.

## Handling

Julienne the authored 250 g immediately before dressing; early cutting or extended dressing can brown or soften the apple.

## Culinary Use

Combine with celery and herbs, dress close to service, and retain the short 5–10 min rest.
