## Functional Profile

Apricot chutney contributes fruit sweetness and tang to the Monkey Gland sauce as a prepared condiment.

## Handling

Combine the authored 120 g with ketchup, vinegar, mustard, and savoury seasonings; do not replace it with plain apricot.

## Culinary Use

Simmer into the glossy glaze and adjust the source-specified sweet and sour balance to taste.
