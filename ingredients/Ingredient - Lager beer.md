## Functional Profile

Lager is the first-listed beer option in Cowboy Beans and contributes cooking liquid and malt flavour.

## Handling

Use the source's 62.5 g beer quantity; add reserved bean liquid separately only until the mixture is just covered.

## Culinary Use

Amber beer remains an explicit alternative, not a synonym for lager.
