## Functional Profile

Italian pork sausages provide the main protein and seasoned fat in the braise.

## Handling

Brown whole without piercing, then finish cooking in the lentils and tomato sauce.

## Culinary Use

Keep the browned sausages as a Phase Output of this same Ingredient.
