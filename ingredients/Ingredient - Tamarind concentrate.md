## Functional Profile

Concentrated sour fruit preparation supplies acidity to rendang and tamarind chutney.

## Handling

Add rendang's dose before its simmer; combine chutney's dose with water and dates before softening and blending.

## Culinary Use

Product strength varies and is unrecorded. Retain authored masses without inventing a universal dilution or acidity measurement.
