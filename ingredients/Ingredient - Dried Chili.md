## Functional Profile

Dried chili contributes heat and a concentrated dried-pepper aroma.

## Handling

Add early enough in the sauce preparation for its flavour to infuse the oil.
The source does not specify a colour or variety.

## Culinary Use

Use the measured 1 g in the Italian sausage and Puy lentil spiced tomato sauce.
