Ripe banana, toasted coconut, lime, and gentle chilli for cooling sweet contrast beside curry

## PHASE A — TOAST COCONUT

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| desiccated-coconut | [Desiccated coconut](ref:ingredient/desiccated-coconut) | 80 g | 22.86% | Desiccated coconut. |

### Method

1. Toast desiccated coconut in a dry pan over medium-low heat for 3 min–5 min until pale gold. Cool completely.

### Phase Outputs

| Key | Phase Output | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| toasted-coconut | Toasted coconut | Prepared intermediate for a later Phase. Fold in 60 g and finish with the remaining 20 g. |

## PHASE B — ASSEMBLE

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ripe-but-firm-banana-sliced | [Ripe but firm banana, sliced](ref:ingredient/banana) | 350 g | 100.00% | Ripe but firm banana, sliced. |
| lime-juice | [Lime juice](ref:ingredient/lime-juice) | 35 g | 10.00% | Lime juice. |
| plain-yoghurt | [Plain yoghurt](ref:ingredient/plain-yoghurt) | 100 g | 28.57% | Plain yoghurt. |
| fine-salt | [Fine salt](ref:ingredient/salt) | 3 g | 0.86% | Fine salt. |
| ground-cumin-toasted | [Ground cumin, toasted](ref:ingredient/cumin) | 1 g | 0.29% | Ground cumin, toasted. |
| red-chilli-finely-chopped | [Red chilli, finely chopped](ref:ingredient/fresh-red-chili) | 5 g | 1.43% | Red chilli, finely chopped. |
| mint-leaves-finely-sliced | [Mint leaves, finely sliced](ref:ingredient/mint) | 10 g | 2.86% | Mint leaves, finely sliced. |

### Method

1. Whisk lime juice, yoghurt, salt, cumin, and chilli.
2. Fold banana gently through the dressing.
3. Fold in 60 g toasted coconut and mint.
4. Transfer to a serving bowl and finish with remaining 20 g coconut.

Assemble no more than 30 min before serving to prevent the banana softening.

Recorded alternatives (no separate variation-specific testing observations were supplied):

- **Dry version:** Omit yoghurt and increase lime juice to 45 g.
- **Pineapple:** Replace 100 g banana with 100 g finely diced pineapple.

### Phase Outputs Used

| Phase Output | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Toasted coconut | Use `toasted-coconut`. Fold in 60 g and finish with the remaining 20 g. |

## FAILURE MODES

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mushy banana | rough mixing | fold gently |
| Soggy coconut | early assembly | finish near service |
