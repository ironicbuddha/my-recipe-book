Grilled marinated beef over rice vermicelli with nước chấm.

## PHASE A — MARINATE BEEF

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| beef | [Beef sirloin](ref:ingredient/beef-sirloin) | 250 g | 100.00% | Slice thinly across the grain. |
| fish-sauce | [Fish sauce](ref:ingredient/fish-sauce) | 12.5 g | 5.00% | Season the marinade. |
| light-soy | [Light soy sauce](ref:ingredient/light-soy-sauce) | 7.5 g | 3.00% | Season the marinade. |
| sugar | [Sugar](ref:ingredient/sugar) | 10 g | 4.00% | Balance salt and browning. |
| honey | [Honey](ref:ingredient/honey) | 5 g | 2.00% | Add sweetness. |
| garlic | [Garlic](ref:ingredient/garlic) | 6 g | 2.40% | Mince. |
| lemongrass | [Lemongrass](ref:ingredient/lemongrass) | 12.5 g | 5.00% | Mince the white part. |
| shallot | [Shallot](ref:ingredient/shallot) | 15 g | 6.00% | Mince. |
| pepper | [Black pepper](ref:ingredient/black-pepper) | 1.5 g | 0.60% | Season. |
| oil | [Neutral oil](ref:ingredient/neutral-oil) | 7.5 g | 3.00% | Coat the beef. |

### Method

1. Combine the marinade, coat the beef, and refrigerate for 1 h minimum or 6–12 h preferred.

## PHASE B — GRILL BEEF

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Grilling](ref:technique/grilling) | 250 C+ surface; 60–90 s per side; 52–54 C centre | Caramelize without overcooking. |

### Principles

- [Maillard reaction](ref:principle/maillard-reaction) — High heat browns the beef before its centre dries.

### Method

1. Oil hot grates and grill the beef briefly on both sides; rest briefly.

## PHASE C — MAKE NOODLES AND SERVE

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fish-sauce | [Fish sauce](ref:ingredient/fish-sauce) | 30 g | 12.00% | Season nước chấm. |
| water | [Water](ref:ingredient/water) | 30 g | 12.00% | Dilute sauce. |
| rice-vinegar | [Rice vinegar](ref:ingredient/rice-vinegar) | 30 g | 12.00% | Acidify sauce. |
| sugar | [Sugar](ref:ingredient/sugar) | 15 g | 6.00% | Balance sauce. |
| lime | [Lime juice](ref:ingredient/lime-juice) | 15 g | 6.00% | Brighten sauce. |
| garlic | [Garlic](ref:ingredient/garlic) | 5 g | 2.00% | Mince. |
| chili | [Fresh red chili](ref:ingredient/fresh-red-chili) | 2.5 g | 1.00% | Slice. |
| carrot | [Carrot](ref:ingredient/carrot) | 25 g | 10.00% | Slice. |
| vermicelli | [Rice vermicelli](ref:ingredient/rice-vermicelli) | 200 g | 80.00% | Cook, rinse, and drain. |
| lettuce | [Lettuce](ref:ingredient/lettuce) | 60 g | 24.00% | Line bowls. |
| coriander | [Fresh coriander](ref:ingredient/fresh-coriander) | 20 g | 8.00% | Finish bowls. |
| mint | [Mint](ref:ingredient/mint) | 10 g | 4.00% | Finish bowls. |
| basil | [Thai basil](ref:ingredient/thai-basil) | 10 g | 4.00% | Finish bowls. |
| peanuts | [Roasted peanuts](ref:ingredient/roasted-peanuts) | 20 g | 8.00% | Crush over bowls. |

### Method

1. Dissolve sugar in water, add sauce ingredients, and rest 15 min.
2. Assemble noodles, lettuce, herbs, grilled beef, and peanuts; serve with sauce.

## FAILURE MODES

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tough beef | It was sliced with the grain or overcooked. | Slice across the grain and grill briefly. |
| Flat sauce | Acid and sweetness are unbalanced. | Adjust lime juice or sugar in small increments. |
