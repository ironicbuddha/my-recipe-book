Grilled pork and patties with noodles, herbs, and dipping sauce.

## PHASE A — MARINATE AND FORM PORK

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| pork-shoulder | [Pork shoulder](ref:ingredient/pork-shoulder) | 250 g | 100.00% | Slice thinly. |
| pork-mince | [Pork mince](ref:ingredient/pork-mince) | 100 g | 40.00% | Form into small patties. |
| fish-sauce | [Fish sauce](ref:ingredient/fish-sauce) | 15 g | 6.00% | Season both pork preparations. |
| sugar | [Sugar](ref:ingredient/sugar) | 12.5 g | 5.00% | Balance the marinade. |
| honey | [Honey](ref:ingredient/honey) | 7.5 g | 3.00% | Support caramelisation. |
| garlic | [Garlic](ref:ingredient/garlic) | 6 g | 2.40% | Mince finely. |
| shallot | [Shallot](ref:ingredient/shallot) | 20 g | 8.00% | Mince finely. |
| black-pepper | [Black pepper](ref:ingredient/black-pepper) | 1.5 g | 0.60% | Season the pork. |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Marination](ref:technique/marination) | Refrigerate 1 h minimum; overnight preferred. | Season both sliced pork and patties. |

### Method

1. Combine the marinade, coat sliced pork with half, and mix the remaining half into the mince.
2. Form patties and refrigerate both preparations.

## PHASE B — GRILL PORK

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| neutral-oil | [Neutral oil](ref:ingredient/neutral-oil) | 5 g | 2.00% | Brush the grates. |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Grilling](ref:technique/grilling) | 250 C surface; cook patties to 68 C and rest 3–5 min. | Char and cook the pork. |

### Principles

- [Maillard reaction](ref:principle/maillard-reaction) — Hot grilling creates browned, savoury complexity.

### Method

1. Grill sliced pork and patties until caramelised and cooked through.

## PHASE C — MAKE NOODLES AND SERVE

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| rice-vermicelli | [Rice vermicelli](ref:ingredient/rice-vermicelli) | 200 g | 80.00% | Cook, rinse, and drain. |
| mixed-herbs | [Mixed herbs](ref:ingredient/mixed-herbs) | 100 g | 40.00% | Serve with the noodles. |
| sauce-water | [Water](ref:ingredient/water) | 30 g | 12.00% | Form the dipping sauce. |
| rice-vinegar | [Rice vinegar](ref:ingredient/rice-vinegar) | 30 g | 12.00% | Acidify the sauce. |
| lime-juice | [Lime juice](ref:ingredient/lime-juice) | 15 g | 6.00% | Brighten the sauce. |
| red-chili | [Red chili](ref:ingredient/red-chili) | 2.5 g | 1.00% | Slice into the sauce. |
| carrot | [Carrot](ref:ingredient/carrot) | 25 g | 10.00% | Slice into the sauce. |

### Method

1. Make nước chấm and rest the carrot in it for 15 min.
2. Serve the grilled pork with noodles, herbs, and sauce.

## FAILURE MODES

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dry patties | Pork was cooked beyond 70 C. | Pull at 68 C and rest. |
| Harsh sauce | Salt, acid, and sugar are unbalanced. | Add water or sugar in small increments. |
