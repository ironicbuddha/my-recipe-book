Grilled pork and patties with rice vermicelli and dipping sauce.

## PHASE A — MARINATE AND FORM PORK

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| shoulder | [Pork shoulder](ref:ingredient/pork-shoulder) | 250 g | 100.00% | Slice thinly. |
| mince | [Pork mince](ref:ingredient/pork-mince) | 100 g | 40.00% | Form patties. |
| fish-sauce | [Fish sauce](ref:ingredient/fish-sauce) | 15 g | 6.00% | Season. |
| sugar | [Sugar](ref:ingredient/sugar) | 12.5 g | 5.00% | Balance. |
| honey | [Honey](ref:ingredient/honey) | 7.5 g | 3.00% | Sweeten. |
| garlic | [Garlic](ref:ingredient/garlic) | 6 g | 2.40% | Mince. |
| shallot | [Shallot](ref:ingredient/shallot) | 20 g | 8.00% | Mince. |
| pepper | [Black pepper](ref:ingredient/black-pepper) | 1.5 g | 0.60% | Season. |

### Method

1. Combine seasoning, marinate the sliced pork with half, mix the remainder into mince, form 40 g patties, and marinate 1 h minimum.

## PHASE B — GRILL PORK

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| oil | [Neutral oil](ref:ingredient/neutral-oil) | 5 g | 2.00% | Brush grates. |

### Method

1. Heat a grill to a 250 C+ surface, oil grates, and grill pork to 68 C; rest 3–5 min.

## PHASE C — MAKE NOODLES AND SERVE

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fish-sauce | [Fish sauce](ref:ingredient/fish-sauce) | 30 g | 12.00% | Season nước chấm. |
| water | [Water](ref:ingredient/water) | 30 g | 12.00% | Dilute. |
| rice-vinegar | [Rice vinegar](ref:ingredient/rice-vinegar) | 30 g | 12.00% | Acidify. |
| sugar | [Sugar](ref:ingredient/sugar) | 15 g | 6.00% | Balance. |
| lime | [Lime juice](ref:ingredient/lime-juice) | 15 g | 6.00% | Brighten. |
| garlic | [Garlic](ref:ingredient/garlic) | 5 g | 2.00% | Mince. |
| chili | [Fresh red chili](ref:ingredient/fresh-red-chili) | 2.5 g | 1.00% | Slice. |
| carrot | [Carrot](ref:ingredient/carrot) | 25 g | 10.00% | Slice. |
| vermicelli | [Rice vermicelli](ref:ingredient/rice-vermicelli) | 200 g | 80.00% | Cook and drain. |
| lettuce | [Lettuce](ref:ingredient/lettuce) | 60 g | 24.00% | Serve with herbs. |
| coriander | [Fresh coriander](ref:ingredient/fresh-coriander) | 20 g | 8.00% | Finish. |

### Method

1. Mix sauce, rest with carrot, cook noodles, then serve pork with noodles, lettuce, herbs, and sauce.

## FAILURE MODES

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dry patties | Pork cooked beyond its endpoint. | Pull at 68 C and rest. |
| Harsh sauce | Salt, sugar, and acid are unbalanced. | Adjust with water or sugar in small increments. |
