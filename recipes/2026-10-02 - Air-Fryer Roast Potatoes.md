Parboiled, roughed-up potatoes with crisp craggy edges and fluffy centres

## PHASE A — PARBOIL

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| floury-potatoes-peeled-and-cut-into-50-mm-pieces | [Floury potatoes, peeled and cut into 50 mm pieces](ref:ingredient/potato) | 1000 g | 100.00% | Floury potatoes, peeled and cut into 50 mm pieces. |
| water | [Water](ref:ingredient/water) | 2000 g | 200.00% | Water. |
| fine-salt | [Fine salt](ref:ingredient/salt) | 20 g | 2.00% | Fine salt. |
| bicarbonate-of-soda | [Bicarbonate of soda](ref:ingredient/baking-soda) | 4 g | 0.40% | Bicarbonate of soda. |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Blanching](ref:technique/blanching) | 8 min–10 min; exterior tender, centre firm | Parboil before roughing. |

### Method

1. Bring water, 20 g salt, and bicarbonate of soda to a boil.
2. Add potatoes and simmer for 8 min–10 min until the exteriors are tender but the centres remain firm.
3. Drain and steam-dry for 5 min.

### Phase Outputs

| Key | Phase Output | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| parboiled-potatoes | Parboiled potatoes | Prepared intermediate for a later Phase. Use all. |

## PHASE B — ROUGH AND COAT

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| duck-fat-or-neutral-oil | [Duck fat or neutral oil](ref:ingredient/duck-fat) | 70 g | 7.00% | Duck fat or neutral oil. |

### Method

1. Shake potatoes in the pot until a starchy paste forms on the edges.
2. Fold through fat or oil. Cool uncovered for 10 min.
3. Retain the recorded Duck fat or neutral oil option; [the alternative](ref:ingredient/neutral-oil) uses the same declared quantity, in place of the first material.

## PHASE C — AIR FRY

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fine-salt-for-finishing | [Fine salt, for finishing](ref:ingredient/salt) | 5 g | 0.50% | Fine salt, for finishing. |
| black-pepper | [Black pepper](ref:ingredient/black-pepper) | 2 g | 0.20% | Black pepper. |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Air frying](ref:technique/air-frying) | 200 C; 25 min–35 min; turn every 8 min–10 min | Develop crisp craggy edges. |

### Method

1. Heat air fryer to 200 C.
2. Cook potatoes in a single loose layer for 25 min–35 min, turning every 8 min–10 min, until deeply golden and crisp.
3. Season immediately with finishing salt and pepper.

Do not crowd the basket; cook in two batches if necessary.

Recorded alternatives (no separate variation-specific testing observations were supplied):

- **Garlic and rosemary:** Toss with 5 g rosemary and 10 g roasted-garlic paste for the final 5 min.
- **Beef fat:** Replace duck fat or oil with rendered beef fat.

### Phase Outputs Used

| Phase Output | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Coated potatoes | Use `coated-potatoes`. Use all. |

## FAILURE MODES

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pale edges | insufficient drying | steam-dry before coating |
| Soft crust | crowded basket | cook in a loose single layer |
