Clear chicken broth with herbs and separate red and green jeow condiments.

## PHASE A — BLANCH WING TIPS

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| wing-tips | [Chicken wing tips](ref:ingredient/chicken-wing-tips) | 250 g | 33.33% | Blanch to remove blood proteins. |
| water | [Water](ref:ingredient/water) | As needed | — | Cover the wing tips. |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Blanching](ref:technique/blanching) | Hard boil; 3–5 min | Remove surface impurities before making broth. |

### Method

1. Boil the wing tips in water for 3–5 min, drain, rinse thoroughly, and reserve.

## PHASE B — MAKE BROTH AND POACH CHICKEN

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| chicken | [Whole chicken](ref:ingredient/whole-chicken) | 750 g | 100.00% | Poach in the fortified broth. |
| onion | [Onion](ref:ingredient/onion) | As needed | — | Halve for the broth. |
| ginger | [Fresh ginger](ref:ingredient/fresh-ginger) | 25 g | 3.33% | Slice for the broth. |
| garlic | [Garlic](ref:ingredient/garlic) | As needed | — | Add to the broth. |
| lemongrass | [Lemongrass](ref:ingredient/lemongrass) | As needed | — | Bruise for the broth. |
| coriander-seed | [Coriander seed](ref:ingredient/coriander-seed) | 1.5 g | 0.20% | Season the broth. |
| white-peppercorns | [White peppercorns](ref:ingredient/white-peppercorns) | 1 g | 0.13% | Season the broth. |
| water | [Water](ref:ingredient/water) | As needed | — | Fully submerge the chicken and wing tips. |
| salt | [Salt](ref:ingredient/salt) | As needed | — | Season lightly. |

### Principles

- [Emulsion clarity](ref:principle/emulsion-clarity) — A gentle simmer avoids emulsifying fat and particles into the broth.

### Method

1. Combine the blanched wing tips, chicken, aromatics, water, and salt; bring gradually to a gentle simmer, remove the chicken after 45–55 min, and simmer the wing tips for a further 30–45 min.

## PHASE C — MAKE RED JEOW

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| dried-chili | [Dried red chili](ref:ingredient/dried-red-chili) | As needed | — | Toast and soak for the paste. |
| garlic | [Garlic](ref:ingredient/garlic) | As needed | — | Char for the paste. |
| shallot | [Shallot](ref:ingredient/shallot) | As needed | — | Char for the paste. |
| oil | [Neutral oil](ref:ingredient/neutral-oil) | 15 g | 2.00% | Fry the paste. |
| fish-sauce | [Fish sauce](ref:ingredient/fish-sauce) | As needed | — | Season the condiment. |
| lime-juice | [Lime juice](ref:ingredient/lime-juice) | As needed | — | Acidify the condiment. |
| padek | [Padek](ref:ingredient/padek) | 2.5 g | 0.33% | Add optional fermented depth, distinct from fish sauce. |
| sticky-rice-powder | [Toasted sticky rice powder](ref:ingredient/toasted-sticky-rice-powder) | As needed | — | Add optional body. |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Mortar pounding](ref:technique/mortar-pounding) | Pound to a coarse paste | Preserve the jeow's rough texture. |
| [Paste frying](ref:technique/paste-frying) | Gentle heat until aromatic | Cook out the raw paste flavour. |

### Method

1. Toast and soak the chilies, char the garlic and shallot, pound them to a coarse paste, fry it in oil until aromatic, then season with fish sauce, lime juice, and optional padek or sticky rice powder.

## PHASE D — MAKE GREEN JEOW AND SERVE

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| shallot | [Shallot](ref:ingredient/shallot) | As needed | — | Pound into the fresh condiment. |
| fish-sauce | [Fish sauce](ref:ingredient/fish-sauce) | As needed | — | Season the condiment and bowls. |
| lime-juice | [Lime juice](ref:ingredient/lime-juice) | As needed | — | Brighten the condiment and bowls. |
| padek | [Padek](ref:ingredient/padek) | As needed | — | Add optional fermented depth, distinct from fish sauce. |
| flat-rice-noodles | [Flat rice noodles](ref:ingredient/flat-rice-noodles) | As needed | — | Cook for the bowls. |
| fresh-coriander | [Fresh coriander](ref:ingredient/fresh-coriander) | As needed | — | Finish the bowls. |
| mint | [Mint](ref:ingredient/mint) | As needed | — | Finish the bowls. |
| thai-basil | [Thai basil](ref:ingredient/thai-basil) | As needed | — | Finish the bowls. |

### Principles

- [Condiment layering](ref:principle/condiment-layering) — Keep the red and green jeow separate so each remains a distinct adjustment at the bowl.

### Method

1. Pound shallot into a coarse green jeow, season it with fish sauce, lime juice, and optional padek; strain the broth without pressing, shred the chicken, cook the noodles, and assemble bowls with broth, herbs, and separate red and green jeow.

## FAILURE MODES

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cloudy broth | The broth boiled hard or solids were pressed while straining. | Maintain a gentle simmer and strain without pressing. |
| Bitter red jeow | The chilies burned while toasting. | Toast only until fragrant and keep them moving. |
