Sweet-sour tomato and onion accompaniment tempered with warm curry spices

## PHASE A — TEMPER

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| onion-thinly-sliced | [Onion, thinly sliced](ref:ingredient/onion) | 300 g | 50.00% | Onion, thinly sliced. |
| neutral-oil | [Neutral oil](ref:ingredient/neutral-oil) | 30 g | 5.00% | Neutral oil. |
| curry-powder | [Curry powder](ref:ingredient/curry-powder) | 10 g | 1.67% | Curry powder. |
| mustard-seed | [Mustard seed](ref:ingredient/mustard-seed) | 5 g | 0.83% | Mustard seed. |
| ground-cumin | [Ground cumin](ref:ingredient/cumin) | 3 g | 0.50% | Ground cumin. |
| ground-turmeric | [Ground turmeric](ref:ingredient/turmeric) | 2 g | 0.33% | Ground turmeric. |
| fresh-ginger-finely-grated | [Fresh ginger, finely grated](ref:ingredient/fresh-ginger) | 15 g | 2.50% | Fresh ginger, finely grated. |
| garlic-finely-grated | [Garlic, finely grated](ref:ingredient/garlic) | 8 g | 1.33% | Garlic, finely grated. |
| fine-salt | [Fine salt](ref:ingredient/salt) | 9 g | 1.50% | Fine salt. |
| red-chilli-finely-chopped | [Red chilli, finely chopped](ref:ingredient/fresh-red-chili) | 10 g | 1.67% | Red chilli, finely chopped. |

### Method

1. Heat oil over medium heat. Add mustard seed and cook until it begins to pop.
2. Add onion and salt; cook for 12 min–15 min until soft and lightly golden.
3. Add curry powder, cumin, turmeric, ginger, garlic, and chilli; cook for 1 min.

## PHASE B — REDUCE

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ripe-tomato-diced | [Ripe tomato, diced](ref:ingredient/tomato) | 600 g | 100.00% | Ripe tomato, diced. |
| brown-sugar | [Brown sugar](ref:ingredient/brown-sugar) | 60 g | 10.00% | Brown sugar. |
| malt-or-apple-cider-vinegar | [Malt or apple cider vinegar](ref:ingredient/malt-vinegar) | 80 g | 13.33% | Malt or apple cider vinegar. |

### Method

1. Add tomato, sugar, and vinegar.
2. Simmer uncovered for 35 min–45 min, stirring frequently, until thick but still spoonable.
3. Cool, then refrigerate for at least 12 h before serving.
4. Retain the recorded Malt or apple cider vinegar option; [the alternative](ref:ingredient/apple-cider-vinegar) uses the same declared quantity, in place of the first material.

Tomato sweetness and water content will change the final reduction time.
The chutney should balance sweetness, acidity, salt, and warm spice rather than taste like tomato jam.

Recorded alternatives (no separate variation-specific testing observations were supplied):

- **Smooth:** Pulse briefly after cooling while retaining some texture.
- **Raisins:** Add 60 g raisins for the final 20 min of cooking.

## FAILURE MODES

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jam-like sweetness | unbalanced reduction | check acidity and spice balance |
| Scorched spices | excessive dry heat | add tomato after recorded brief spice cooking |
