Beef slowly cooked in coconut milk and aromatics until the sauce fries down to a dark, clinging paste

## PHASE A — SPICE PASTE

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| shallot-or-red-onion-chopped | [Shallot or red onion, chopped](ref:ingredient/shallot) | 300 g | 18.75% | Shallot or red onion, chopped. |
| garlic | [Garlic](ref:ingredient/garlic) | 40 g | 2.50% | Garlic. |
| ginger | [Ginger](ref:ingredient/fresh-ginger) | 60 g | 3.75% | Ginger. |
| galangal | [Galangal](ref:ingredient/galangal) | 50 g | 3.13% | Galangal. |
| red-chilli-seeded-as-desired | [Red chilli, seeded as desired](ref:ingredient/fresh-red-chili) | 100 g | 6.25% | Red chilli, seeded as desired. |
| lemongrass-tender-inner-core | [Lemongrass, tender inner core](ref:ingredient/lemongrass) | 50 g | 3.13% | Lemongrass, tender inner core. |
| ground-coriander | [Ground coriander](ref:ingredient/coriander-seed) | 10 g | 0.63% | Ground coriander. |
| ground-cumin | [Ground cumin](ref:ingredient/cumin) | 5 g | 0.31% | Ground cumin. |
| ground-turmeric | [Ground turmeric](ref:ingredient/turmeric) | 5 g | 0.31% | Ground turmeric. |
| neutral-oil | [Neutral oil](ref:ingredient/neutral-oil) | 80 g | 5.00% | Neutral oil. |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Paste frying](ref:technique/paste-frying) | Medium heat; 12 min–15 min; oil begins to separate | Cook the aromatic paste. |

### Method

1. Blend onion, garlic, ginger, galangal, chilli, lemongrass, spices, and oil to a fine paste.
2. Fry the paste in a wide heavy pot over medium heat for 12 min–15 min until aromatic and the oil begins to separate.
3. Retain the recorded Shallot or red onion, chopped option; [the alternative](ref:ingredient/red-onion) uses the same declared quantity, in place of the first material.

### Phase Outputs

| Key | Phase Output | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| fried-spice-paste | Fried spice paste | Prepared intermediate for a later Phase. Use the entire prepared paste; approximately 700 g is a finished-weight estimate. |

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Simmering](ref:technique/simmering) | Low gentle simmer; 2.5 h–3.5 h | Tenderise the beef. |

### Method

1. Add beef and turn to coat. Add coconut milk, bruised lemongrass, lime leaves, tamarind, sugar, and salt.
2. Bring to a gentle simmer. Cook uncovered over low heat for 2.5 h–3.5 h, stirring more frequently as the liquid reduces.

## PHASE C — FRY DOWN

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| toasted-desiccated-coconut-finely-ground | [Toasted desiccated coconut, finely ground](ref:ingredient/desiccated-coconut) | 100 g | 6.25% | Toasted desiccated coconut, finely ground. |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Reduction](ref:technique/reduction) | 20 min–40 min; dry paste clings to beef | Reach the dry rendang endpoint. |

### Method

1. When the beef is tender and the coconut oil separates, add ground toasted coconut.
2. Continue cooking and folding gently for 20 min–40 min until the paste is dark, dry, and clings to the beef.
3. Remove lemongrass and lime leaves. Rest for at least 30 min before serving.

A wide pot makes the final evaporation controllable. Stir carefully once the beef is tender.

Recorded alternatives (no separate variation-specific testing observations were supplied):

- **Make ahead:** Cool after the slow-cook phase, refrigerate overnight, then complete the fry-down the next day.
- **Milder:** Reduce fresh chilli to 50 g and add 50 g red pepper.

### Phase Outputs Used

| Phase Output | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Tender beef | Use `tender-beef`. Use all beef and its reducing coconut liquid. |

## FAILURE MODES

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wet sauce | incomplete reduction | continue controlled reduction |
| Scorched paste | insufficient stirring late in cooking | lower heat and fold frequently |
