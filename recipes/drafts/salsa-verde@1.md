Italian-style parsley, caper, anchovy, and lemon sauce for roast meat, fish, and vegetables

## PHASE A — CHOP

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| flat-leaf-parsley-leaves | [Flat-leaf parsley leaves](ref:ingredient/flat-leaf-parsley) | 100 g | 100.00% | Flat-leaf parsley leaves. |
| capers-rinsed-and-drained | [Capers, rinsed and drained](ref:ingredient/capers) | 35 g | 35.00% | Capers, rinsed and drained. |
| anchovy-fillets | [Anchovy fillets](ref:ingredient/anchovy-fillets) | 25 g | 25.00% | Anchovy fillets. |
| garlic | [Garlic](ref:ingredient/garlic) | 8 g | 8.00% | Garlic. |

### Method

1. Dry parsley thoroughly.
2. Finely chop parsley, capers, anchovy, and garlic by hand, keeping visible texture.

| Key | Phase Output | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |

## PHASE B — EMULSIFY

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| extra-virgin-olive-oil | [Extra-virgin olive oil](ref:ingredient/extra-virgin-olive-oil) | 180 g | 180.00% | Extra-virgin olive oil. |
| lemon-juice | [Lemon juice](ref:ingredient/lemon-juice) | 35 g | 35.00% | Lemon juice. |
| dijon-mustard | [Dijon mustard](ref:ingredient/dijon-mustard) | 15 g | 15.00% | Dijon mustard. |
| fine-salt | [Fine salt](ref:ingredient/salt) | 2 g | 2.00% | Fine salt. |
| black-pepper | [Black pepper](ref:ingredient/black-pepper) | 1 g | 1.00% | Black pepper. |

### Method

1. Whisk lemon juice and mustard together.
2. Stir in chopped ingredients, then fold in olive oil.
3. Season with salt and pepper. Rest for 20 min before serving.

The project history does not identify whether the original was Italian or tomatillo-based; this draft uses the Italian style associated with roast meats.
Do not blend to a smooth purée; hand-chopping preserves texture and colour.

Recorded alternatives (no separate variation-specific testing observations were supplied):

- **Mint:** Replace 25 g parsley with 25 g mint leaves.
- **Without anchovy:** Omit anchovy and increase capers by 15 g.
