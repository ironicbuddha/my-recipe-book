Cool, tangy finishing sauce for tacos, nachos, and grilled food

## PHASE A — MIX

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| cr-me-fra-che | [Crème fraîche](ref:ingredient/creme-fraiche) | 250 g | 100.00% | Crème fraîche. |
| lime-juice | [Lime juice](ref:ingredient/lime-juice) | 35 g | 14.00% | Lime juice. |
| lime-zest | [Lime zest](ref:ingredient/lime-zest) | 2 g | 0.80% | Lime zest. |
| water | [Water](ref:ingredient/water) | 30 g | 12.00% | Water. |
| fine-salt | [Fine salt](ref:ingredient/salt) | 3 g | 1.20% | Fine salt. |
| garlic-finely-grated | [Garlic, finely grated](ref:ingredient/garlic) | 2 g | 0.80% | Garlic, finely grated. |
| ground-cumin | [Ground cumin](ref:ingredient/cumin) | 1 g | 0.40% | Ground cumin. |

### Method

1. Whisk crème fraîche until smooth.
2. Whisk in lime juice, zest, water, salt, garlic, and cumin.
3. Rest under refrigeration for 20 min.
4. Adjust with 5 g–15 g water to reach a spoonable or squeezable consistency.

Lime acidity varies, so add the final 10 g juice to taste.
Keeps refrigerated for up to 3 days.

Recorded alternatives (no separate variation-specific testing observations were supplied):

- **Herb crema:** Blend in 20 g coriander leaves.
- **Smoky crema:** Add 10 g chipotle in adobo.

## FAILURE MODES

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Too thick | insufficient dilution | whisk in water gradually |
| Uneven seasoning | inadequate mixing | whisk thoroughly and rest |
