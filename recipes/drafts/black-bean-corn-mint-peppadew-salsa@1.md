Sweet corn, black beans, fresh mint, and piquant Peppadew peppers in a lime dressing

## PHASE A — CORN

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| corn-kernels | [Corn kernels](ref:ingredient/corn-kernels) | 250 g | 100.00% | Corn kernels. |

### Method

1. Heat a dry cast-iron pan over high heat.
2. Char corn for 4 min–6 min, stirring only occasionally, then cool completely.

### Phase Outputs

| Key | Phase Output | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| charred-corn | Charred corn | Prepared intermediate for a later Phase. Use all, cooled. |

## PHASE B — DRESSING

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| red-onion-finely-diced | [Red onion, finely diced](ref:ingredient/red-onion) | 80 g | 32.00% | Red onion, finely diced. |
| lime-juice | [Lime juice](ref:ingredient/lime-juice) | 50 g | 20.00% | Lime juice. |
| extra-virgin-olive-oil | [Extra-virgin olive oil](ref:ingredient/extra-virgin-olive-oil) | 30 g | 12.00% | Extra-virgin olive oil. |
| fine-salt | [Fine salt](ref:ingredient/salt) | 6 g | 2.40% | Fine salt. |
| ground-cumin | [Ground cumin](ref:ingredient/cumin) | 2 g | 0.80% | Ground cumin. |
| black-pepper | [Black pepper](ref:ingredient/black-pepper) | 1 g | 0.40% | Black pepper. |

### Method

1. Whisk lime juice, olive oil, salt, cumin, and black pepper.
2. Soak red onion in the dressing for 10 min.

### Phase Outputs

| Key | Phase Output | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| onion-dressing | Onion dressing | Prepared intermediate for a later Phase. Use all. |

## PHASE C — ASSEMBLY

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| cooked-black-beans-drained | [Cooked black beans, drained](ref:ingredient/black-beans) | 250 g | 100.00% | Cooked black beans, drained. |
| peppadew-peppers-drained-and-diced | [Peppadew peppers, drained and diced](ref:ingredient/peppadew-peppers) | 100 g | 40.00% | Peppadew peppers, drained and diced. |
| mint-leaves-finely-sliced | [Mint leaves, finely sliced](ref:ingredient/mint) | 20 g | 8.00% | Mint leaves, finely sliced. |
| coriander-leaves-chopped | [Coriander leaves, chopped](ref:ingredient/fresh-coriander) | 20 g | 8.00% | Coriander leaves, chopped. |

### Method

1. Fold beans, corn, Peppadew, mint, and coriander through the dressed onion.
2. Rest for 15 min, then adjust salt and lime before serving.

Peppadew sweetness varies, so lime may need adjustment.
Add mint only after all cooked ingredients are cool to prevent discolouration.

Recorded alternatives (no separate variation-specific testing observations were supplied):

- **Hotter:** Add 15 g finely diced fresh jalapeño.
- **Creamy:** Fold in 150 g diced avocado immediately before serving.

### Phase Outputs Used

| Phase Output | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Charred corn | Use `charred-corn`. Use all, cooled. |
| Onion dressing | Use `onion-dressing`. Use all. |

## FAILURE MODES

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wilted herbs | corn still hot | cool before combining |
| Watery salsa | poor drainage | drain beans and peppers |
