A gentle, comforting chicken curry with a coconut-rich gravy, soft potatoes, and peas

## PHASE A — SEASON AND BROWN

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| bone-in-chicken-thighs-and-drumsticks | [Bone-in chicken thighs and drumsticks](ref:ingredient/chicken) | 1200 g | 100.00% | Bone-in chicken thighs and drumsticks. Use bone-in thighs and drumsticks in flexible proportions; their separate weights are unrecorded. |
| fine-salt | [Fine salt](ref:ingredient/salt) | 18 g | 1.50% | Fine salt. |
| ground-turmeric | [Ground turmeric](ref:ingredient/turmeric) | 4 g | 0.33% | Ground turmeric. |
| neutral-oil | [Neutral oil](ref:ingredient/neutral-oil) | 40 g | 3.33% | Neutral oil. |

### Method

1. Season chicken with salt and turmeric; rest for 20 min.
2. Heat oil in a heavy pot. Brown chicken in batches for 3 min–4 min per side. Reserve.

### Phase Outputs

| Key | Phase Output | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| browned-chicken | Browned chicken | Prepared intermediate for a later Phase. Return all chicken to the curry. |

## PHASE B — BUILD CURRY

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| onion-thinly-sliced | [Onion, thinly sliced](ref:ingredient/onion) | 350 g | 29.17% | Onion, thinly sliced. |
| garlic-finely-grated | [Garlic, finely grated](ref:ingredient/garlic) | 25 g | 2.08% | Garlic, finely grated. |
| ginger-finely-grated | [Ginger, finely grated](ref:ingredient/fresh-ginger) | 25 g | 2.08% | Ginger, finely grated. |
| curry-powder | [Curry powder](ref:ingredient/curry-powder) | 20 g | 1.67% | Curry powder. |
| ground-cumin | [Ground cumin](ref:ingredient/cumin) | 5 g | 0.42% | Ground cumin. |
| ground-coriander | [Ground coriander](ref:ingredient/coriander-seed) | 6 g | 0.50% | Ground coriander. |
| tomato-chopped | [Tomato, chopped](ref:ingredient/tomato) | 300 g | 25.00% | Tomato, chopped. |
| coconut-milk | [Coconut milk](ref:ingredient/coconut-milk) | 800 g | 66.67% | Coconut milk. |
| chicken-stock | [Chicken stock](ref:ingredient/chicken-stock) | 300 g | 25.00% | Chicken stock. |

### Method

1. Cook onion in the same pot over medium heat for 15 min–20 min until golden.
2. Add garlic and ginger; cook for 2 min. Add curry powder, cumin, and coriander; cook for 1 min.
3. Add tomato and cook for 8 min–10 min until reduced and glossy.
4. Add coconut milk and stock. Return chicken to the pot and simmer gently, covered, for 25 min.

### Phase Outputs

| --- | --- | --- |
| curry-base | Curry base | Prepared intermediate for a later Phase. Use the entire pot, including chicken and gravy. |

## PHASE C — POTATOES AND PEAS

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| potato-peeled-and-cut-into-35-mm-pieces | [Potato, peeled and cut into 35 mm pieces](ref:ingredient/potato) | 600 g | 50.00% | Potato, peeled and cut into 35 mm pieces. |
| frozen-peas | [Frozen peas](ref:ingredient/peas) | 200 g | 16.67% | Frozen peas. |
| garam-masala | [Garam masala](ref:ingredient/garam-masala) | 5 g | 0.42% | Garam masala. |
| lemon-juice | [Lemon juice](ref:ingredient/lemon-juice) | 25 g | 2.08% | Lemon juice. |
| coriander-leaves-chopped | [Coriander leaves, chopped](ref:ingredient/fresh-coriander) | 30 g | 2.50% | Coriander leaves, chopped. |

### Method

1. Add potatoes and simmer uncovered for 25 min–30 min until tender and the chicken reaches at least 74 C.
2. Add peas and cook for 4 min.
3. Stir in garam masala and lemon juice. Rest for 10 min, then finish with coriander.

The strength of curry powder varies widely.
Keep the simmer gentle after adding coconut milk to avoid splitting the gravy.

Recorded alternatives (no separate variation-specific testing observations were supplied):

- **Boneless:** Use 900 g boneless thigh and reduce the first simmer to 15 min.
- **Hotter:** Add 20 g sliced green chilli with the garlic and ginger.

| Curry base | Use `curry-base`. Use the entire pot, including chicken and gravy. |

## FAILURE MODES

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Firm potatoes | insufficient simmering | cook until tender |
| Harsh spice flavour | insufficient spice cooking | bloom spices before adding liquids |
