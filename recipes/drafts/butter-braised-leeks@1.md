Leeks gently braised until silky, sweet, and glazed with their cooking liquor

## PHASE A — PREPARATION

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| trimmed-leeks-white-and-pale-green-parts | [Trimmed leeks, white and pale-green parts](ref:ingredient/leek) | 800 g | 100.00% | Trimmed leeks, white and pale-green parts. |

### Method

1. Cut leeks into 120 mm–150 mm lengths and halve lengthwise, keeping the root ends intact.
2. Wash thoroughly between the layers and drain.

### Phase Outputs

| Key | Phase Output | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| washed-leeks | Washed leeks | Prepared intermediate for a later Phase. Use all. |

## PHASE B — BRAISE

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| unsalted-butter | [Unsalted butter](ref:ingredient/butter) | 50 g | 6.25% | Unsalted butter. |
| vegetable-stock | [Vegetable stock](ref:ingredient/vegetable-stock) | 250 g | 31.25% | Vegetable stock. |
| fine-salt | [Fine salt](ref:ingredient/salt) | 8 g | 1.00% | Fine salt. |
| white-pepper | [White pepper](ref:ingredient/white-pepper) | 1 g | 0.13% | White pepper. |
| thyme | [Thyme](ref:ingredient/thyme) | 2 g | 0.25% | Thyme. |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Braising](ref:technique/braising) | 160 C; 30 min–40 min; completely tender | Soften the leeks without separating their layers. |

### Method

1. Melt 50 g butter in a wide pan. Arrange leeks cut-side down in one layer.
2. Add stock, salt, white pepper, and thyme. Bring to a gentle simmer.
3. Cover with a lid or parchment cartouche and braise at 160 C for 30 min–40 min, until completely tender.

### Phase Outputs Used

| Phase Output | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Washed leeks | Use `washed-leeks`. Use all. |

### Phase Outputs

| braised-leeks | Braised leeks | Prepared intermediate for a later Phase. Transfer all carefully to a warm plate. |
| braising-liquid | Braising liquid | Prepared intermediate for a later Phase. Reduce all, then finish with butter and lemon. |

## PHASE C — GLAZE

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| finishing-butter | [Unsalted butter](ref:ingredient/butter) | 30 g | 3.75% | Whisk into reduced braising liquid. |
| lemon-juice | [Lemon juice](ref:ingredient/lemon-juice) | 15 g | 1.88% | Lemon juice. |

### Method

1. Transfer leeks carefully to a warm plate.
2. Reduce the braising liquid over medium heat until lightly syrupy.
3. Whisk in remaining 30 g butter and lemon juice. Spoon over the leeks.

The exact stock needed depends on pan width.
Avoid boiling aggressively, which can separate the layers before the core is tender.

Recorded alternatives (no separate variation-specific testing observations were supplied):

- **Brown-butter finish:** Brown the final 30 g butter before whisking it into the reduced liquor.
- **Mustard glaze:** Add 10 g Dijon mustard with the lemon juice.

### Phase Outputs Used

| Phase Output | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Braised leeks | Use `braised-leeks`. Transfer all carefully to a warm plate. |
| Braising liquid | Use `braising-liquid`. Reduce all, then finish with butter and lemon. |

## FAILURE MODES

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gritty leeks | inadequate washing | wash between layers |
| Broken leeks | rough handling | transfer carefully |
