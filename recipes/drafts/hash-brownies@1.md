Draft variation of [Masterclass Chocolate Brownie v1](ref:recipe/masterclass-chocolate-brownie@1). If using an infused fat, use a commercially prepared, lab-tested product and follow its label and local laws.

## PHASE A — MELT AND MIX

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| butter | [Butter](ref:ingredient/butter) | 250 g | 100.00% | Melt with the chocolate. |
| chocolate | [Dark chocolate](ref:ingredient/dark-chocolate) | 200 g | 80.00% | Melt gently. |
| caster-sugar | [Caster sugar](ref:ingredient/caster-sugar) | 300 g | 120.00% | Whisk with eggs. |
| egg | [Egg](ref:ingredient/egg) | 200 g | 80.00% | Whisk until glossy. |

### Method

1. Melt butter and chocolate gently, then whisk caster sugar and eggs until glossy.
2. Fold the melted mixture into the egg mixture.

### Phase Outputs

| Key | Phase Output | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| draft-batter | Draft brownie batter | Chocolate base ready for dry ingredients. |

## PHASE B — FINISH AND BAKE

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| flour | [All-purpose flour](ref:ingredient/all-purpose-flour) | 100 g | 40.00% | Sift before folding. |
| cocoa | [Cocoa powder](ref:ingredient/cocoa-powder) | 50 g | 20.00% | Sift before folding. |
| salt | [Salt](ref:ingredient/salt) | 2 g | 0.80% | Season the batter. |
| vanilla | [Vanilla extract](ref:ingredient/vanilla-extract) | 5 g | 2.00% | Flavour the batter. |
| walnuts | [Walnuts](ref:ingredient/walnuts) | 150 g | 60.00% | Optional addition. |

### Method

1. Fold in the dry ingredients, bake at 170 C for 22–30 min to 88–92 C, and cool completely.
2. Slice into 16 equal squares.

### Phase Outputs Used

| Phase Output | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Draft brownie batter | Fold the dry ingredients into `draft-batter`. |

## FAILURE MODES

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Greasy texture | Chocolate was too hot. | Cool it before combining with eggs. |
