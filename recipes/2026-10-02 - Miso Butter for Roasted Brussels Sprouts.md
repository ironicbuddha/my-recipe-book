Hard-roasted Brussels sprouts glazed with savoury-sweet miso butter and rice vinegar

## PHASE A — MISO BUTTER

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| unsalted-butter-softened | [Unsalted butter, softened](ref:ingredient/butter) | 100 g | 12.50% | Unsalted butter, softened. |
| white-miso | [White miso](ref:ingredient/white-miso) | 50 g | 6.25% | White miso. |
| honey | [Honey](ref:ingredient/honey) | 20 g | 2.50% | Honey. |
| rice-vinegar | [Rice vinegar](ref:ingredient/rice-vinegar) | 20 g | 2.50% | Rice vinegar. |
| soy-sauce | [Soy sauce](ref:ingredient/soy-sauce) | 10 g | 1.25% | Soy sauce. |
| garlic-finely-grated | [Garlic, finely grated](ref:ingredient/garlic) | 5 g | 0.63% | Garlic, finely grated. |
| sesame-oil | [Sesame oil](ref:ingredient/sesame-oil) | 5 g | 0.63% | Sesame oil. |

### Method

1. Beat butter, miso, honey, rice vinegar, soy sauce, garlic, and sesame oil until homogeneous.
2. Reserve 120 g for this recipe; refrigerate the remainder.

### Phase Outputs

| Key | Phase Output | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| miso-butter | Miso butter | Prepared intermediate for a later Phase. Use 120 g; refrigerate the remainder. |

## PHASE B — ROAST

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| brussels-sprouts-trimmed-and-halved | [Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved](ref:ingredient/brussels-sprouts) | 800 g | 100.00% | Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved. |
| neutral-oil | [Neutral oil](ref:ingredient/neutral-oil) | 40 g | 5.00% | Neutral oil. |
| fine-salt | [Fine salt](ref:ingredient/salt) | 8 g | 1.00% | Fine salt. |
| black-pepper | [Black pepper](ref:ingredient/black-pepper) | 2 g | 0.25% | Black pepper. |

### Method

1. Heat oven and a heavy baking tray to 230 C.
2. Toss sprouts with oil, salt, and pepper.
3. Place cut-side down on the hot tray and roast for 18 min–25 min until deeply browned and tender.

### Phase Outputs

| Key | Phase Output | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| roasted-sprouts | Roasted sprouts | Prepared intermediate for a later Phase. Use all sprouts; approximately 650 g is a source finished-weight estimate. |

## PHASE C — GLAZE

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| toasted-sesame-seeds | [Toasted sesame seeds](ref:ingredient/sesame-seeds) | 15 g | 1.88% | Toasted sesame seeds. |

### Method

1. Use the reserved 120 g miso-butter portion; retain the refrigerated remainder.
2. Transfer hot sprouts to a bowl and toss with miso butter until glossy.
3. Return to the oven for 2 min, then finish with sesame seeds.

Miso salinity varies, so taste before adding extra salt.
The butter is reusable and keeps refrigerated for up to 7 days.

Recorded alternatives (no separate variation-specific testing observations were supplied):

- **Citrus:** Add 3 g finely grated orange zest to the butter.
- **Spicy:** Add 8 g chilli crisp after roasting.

### Phase Outputs Used

| Phase Output | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Miso butter | Use `miso-butter`. Use 120 g; refrigerate the remainder. |
| Roasted sprouts | Use `roasted-sprouts`. Use all sprouts; approximately 650 g is a source finished-weight estimate. |

## FAILURE MODES

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pale sprouts | insufficient heat or crowded tray | preheat and arrange cut-side down |
| Excessive salt | salty miso plus added salt | taste before adding more |
