Reduced tomato, onion, pickle, and mustard sauce with enough texture to stay put on a burger

## PHASE A — COOK TOMATO BASE

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| canned-chopped-tomato | [Canned chopped tomato](ref:ingredient/canned-chopped-tomatoes) | 400 g | 100.00% | Canned chopped tomato. |
| onion-finely-diced | [Onion, finely diced](ref:ingredient/onion) | 120 g | 30.00% | Onion, finely diced. |
| neutral-oil | [Neutral oil](ref:ingredient/neutral-oil) | 20 g | 5.00% | Neutral oil. |
| tomato-paste | [Tomato paste](ref:ingredient/tomato-paste) | 30 g | 7.50% | Tomato paste. |
| brown-sugar | [Brown sugar](ref:ingredient/brown-sugar) | 25 g | 6.25% | Brown sugar. |
| apple-cider-vinegar | [Apple cider vinegar](ref:ingredient/apple-cider-vinegar) | 35 g | 8.75% | Apple cider vinegar. |
| worcestershire-sauce | [Worcestershire sauce](ref:ingredient/worcestershire-sauce) | 15 g | 3.75% | Worcestershire sauce. |
| fine-salt | [Fine salt](ref:ingredient/salt) | 6 g | 1.50% | Fine salt. |
| smoked-paprika | [Smoked paprika](ref:ingredient/smoked-paprika) | 2 g | 0.50% | Smoked paprika. |
| black-pepper | [Black pepper](ref:ingredient/black-pepper) | 1 g | 0.25% | Black pepper. |

### Method

1. Sweat onion in oil over medium heat for 8 min without browning.
2. Add tomato paste and paprika; cook for 2 min.
3. Add tomato, sugar, vinegar, Worcestershire sauce, salt, and pepper.
4. Simmer uncovered for 25 min–35 min until thick and chunky. Cool completely.

### Phase Outputs

| Key | Phase Output | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| reduced-tomato-base | Reduced tomato base | Prepared intermediate for a later Phase. Use the stated 420 g cooled base portion; retain any remainder. |

## PHASE B — FINISH

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| dill-pickle-finely-diced | [Dill pickle, finely diced](ref:ingredient/dill-pickle) | 60 g | 15.00% | Dill pickle, finely diced. |
| dijon-mustard | [Dijon mustard](ref:ingredient/dijon-mustard) | 25 g | 6.25% | Dijon mustard. |
| mayonnaise | [Mayonnaise](ref:ingredient/mayonnaise) | 50 g | 12.50% | Mayonnaise. |

### Method

1. Fold pickle, mustard, and mayonnaise through the cold tomato base.
2. Refrigerate for 1 h before serving.

Keeps refrigerated for up to 5 days.

Recorded alternatives (no separate variation-specific testing observations were supplied):

- **Smooth:** Blend the cooked tomato base before cooling, then add finely minced pickle.
- **Hot:** Add 15 g minced pickled jalapeño.

### Phase Outputs Used

| Phase Output | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Reduced tomato base | Use `reduced-tomato-base`. Use the stated 420 g cooled base portion; retain any remainder. |

## FAILURE MODES

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Liquid pooling | insufficient reduction | reduce base further |
| Split finish | mayonnaise added while hot | cool tomato base before folding |
