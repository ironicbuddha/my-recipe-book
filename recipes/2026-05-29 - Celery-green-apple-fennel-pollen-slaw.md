Bright, acidic slaw designed to balance rich porchetta while keeping crunch-focused texture and aromatic fennel continuity.

## PHASE A — VEGETABLE PREPARATION

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| celery | [Celery stalks, thinly sliced](ref:ingredient/celery) | 300 g | 100.00% | Celery stalks, thinly sliced. |
| green-apple | [Green apples, julienned](ref:ingredient/green-apple) | 250 g | 83.33% | Green apples, julienned. |
| flat-leaf-parsley | [Flat-leaf parsley leaves](ref:ingredient/flat-leaf-parsley) | 40 g | 13.33% | Flat-leaf parsley leaves. |
| celery-leaves | [Celery leaves, optional](ref:ingredient/celery-leaves) | 10 g | 3.33% | Celery leaves, optional. |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Knife slicing](ref:technique/knife-slicing) | Thin bias-cut celery; apple matchsticks cut at assembly | Keep a crisp, manageable slaw texture. |

### Method

1. Slice celery thinly on a strong bias for maximum surface area and texture.
2. Julienne apples into thin matchsticks immediately before dressing.
3. Roughly tear parsley leaves and reserve with optional celery leaves.

### Phase Outputs

| Key | Phase Output | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| prepared-vegetables | Prepared celery, apple, and herbs | Thin celery slices, apple matchsticks, and torn herbs. |

## PHASE B — FENNEL DRESSING

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| lemon-juice | [Lemon juice](ref:ingredient/lemon-juice) | 40 g | 13.33% | Lemon juice. |
| extra-virgin-olive-oil | [Extra virgin olive oil](ref:ingredient/extra-virgin-olive-oil) | 35 g | 11.67% | Extra virgin olive oil. |
| apple-cider-vinegar | [Cider vinegar](ref:ingredient/apple-cider-vinegar) | 10 g | 3.33% | Cider vinegar. |
| fennel-seeds | [Fennel seeds, toasted and crushed](ref:ingredient/fennel-seeds) | 4 g | 1.33% | Fennel seeds, toasted and crushed. |
| fennel-pollen | [Fennel pollen](ref:ingredient/fennel-pollen) | 1 g | 0.33% | Fennel pollen. |
| salt | [Kosher salt](ref:ingredient/salt) | 5 g | 1.67% | Kosher salt. |
| black-pepper | [Coarse black pepper](ref:ingredient/black-pepper) | 2 g | 0.67% | Coarse black pepper. |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Dry toasting spices](ref:technique/dry-toasting-spices) | Dry pan until fennel seeds are fragrant | Develop fennel aroma before crushing. |
| [Emulsification](ref:technique/emulsification) | Whisk dressing until lightly emulsified | Distribute the oil and acidic seasonings. |

### Principles

- [Acid balance](ref:principle/acid-balance) — Lemon and cider vinegar balance the rich roast pork pairing.

### Method

1. Toast fennel seeds lightly in a dry pan until fragrant.
2. Crush fennel seeds coarsely using a mortar and pestle.
3. Combine lemon juice, cider vinegar, olive oil, crushed fennel seed, fennel pollen, salt, and black pepper.
4. Whisk until lightly emulsified.

### Phase Outputs

| Key | Phase Output | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| fennel-dressing | Fennel dressing | Lightly emulsified acid, oil, fennel, and seasonings. |

Lemon juice and cider vinegar cut through the rich pork pairing, while fennel seed and pollen preserve aromatic continuity with fennel-seasoned porchetta.

## PHASE C — ASSEMBLY AND SERVICE

### Method

1. Combine celery, apple, parsley, and optional celery leaves in a chilled bowl.
2. Dress lightly and toss thoroughly.
3. Rest 5–10 min before serving.
4. Serve cold beside rich roast pork or porchetta sandwiches.

### Phase Outputs Used

| Phase Output | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Prepared celery, apple, and herbs | Use all of `prepared-vegetables` from PHASE A as directed above. |
| Fennel dressing | Dress lightly with `fennel-dressing` and toss thoroughly. |

## FAILURE MODES

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apples brown or soften before service. | Apples were cut too early or held in dressing too long. | Julienne apples immediately before assembly and dress close to service. |
| Slaw tastes oily or flat. | Dressing ratio is too oil-heavy for the celery and apple mass. | Increase lemon juice or cider vinegar in small increments and re-season with salt. |
| Celery tastes tough or stringy. | Stalks were cut too thick or parallel to the fiber. | Slice thinly on a strong bias and use tender inner stalks where possible. |

## VARIATIONS

- More bitter: add sliced [radicchio](ref:ingredient/radicchio).
- More richness: add shaved [pecorino](ref:ingredient/pecorino) or [Parmesan](ref:ingredient/parmesan).
- More heat: add [chili flakes](ref:ingredient/chili-flakes) or [Calabrian chili](ref:ingredient/calabrian-chili).
- More herbaceous: add [mint](ref:ingredient/mint) or [dill](ref:ingredient/dill) sparingly.
