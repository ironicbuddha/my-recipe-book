Sweet, tangy, umami-rich South African steakhouse-style sauce glazed over crisp-skinned chicken. Yield: 4 portions. Roast to the source internal endpoint of 74 C.

## PHASE A — SEASON AND REST

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| chicken-thigh | [Chicken thighs (bone-in, skin-on)](ref:ingredient/chicken-thigh) | 1000 g | 100.00% | Chicken thighs (bone-in, skin-on). |
| salt | [Salt](ref:ingredient/salt) | 12 g | 1.20% | Salt. |
| black-pepper | [Black pepper](ref:ingredient/black-pepper) | 3 g | 0.30% | Black pepper. |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Salting](ref:technique/salting) | 12 g salt; 1000 g chicken; 20–30 min rest | Season evenly before searing. |

### Principles

- [Skin dehydration](ref:principle/skin-dehydration) — Dry skin supports the later browning step.

### Method

1. Pat chicken very dry (skin included).
2. Season evenly with salt and pepper.
3. Rest 20–30 min at room temperature.

### Phase Outputs

| Key | Phase Output | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| seasoned-chicken | Seasoned chicken thighs | Dried, seasoned chicken after its short rest. |

## PHASE B — SEAR AND ROAST

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| neutral-oil | [Neutral oil](ref:ingredient/neutral-oil) | 20 g | 2.00% | Neutral oil. |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Searing](ref:technique/searing) | Skin-side down for 6–8 min | Brown the skin and render its fat. |
| [Oven roasting](ref:technique/oven-roasting) | 200 C oven; 74 C internal endpoint; about 12–18 min | Finish chicken without extending beyond its endpoint. |

### Principles

- [Maillard reaction](ref:principle/maillard-reaction) — A dry, hot sear builds savoury depth.
- [Carryover cooking](ref:principle/carryover-cooking) — Keep the authored rest and internal endpoint before glazing.

### Method

1. Preheat oven to 200 C.
2. Heat a heavy oven-safe pan over medium-high heat and add oil.
3. Place chicken skin-side down; press lightly for full contact.
4. Sear 6–8 min until deeply golden and fat rendered.
5. Flip and transfer pan to oven.
6. Roast until thickest piece reaches 74 C (about 12–18 min, depending on size).
7. Rest 5–10 min before glazing.

Dry skin and a hard sear build savoury depth. Rest after roasting before adding the glaze.

## PHASE C — MONKEY GLAND SAUCE

**May overlap:** PHASE B — SEAR AND ROAST while the chicken roasts or rests.

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| neutral-oil | [Neutral oil or rendered chicken fat](ref:ingredient/neutral-oil) | As needed | — | Use neutral oil or [rendered chicken fat](ref:ingredient/chicken-fat), as the source allows. Source amount: as needed. |
| garlic | [Garlic, minced](ref:ingredient/garlic) | 10 g | 1.00% | Garlic, minced. |
| fresh-ginger | [Fresh ginger, grated](ref:ingredient/fresh-ginger) | 10 g | 1.00% | Fresh ginger, grated. |
| ketchup | [Tomato sauce (ketchup)](ref:ingredient/ketchup) | 150 g | 15.00% | Tomato sauce (ketchup). |

| dijon-mustard | [Dijon mustard](ref:ingredient/dijon-mustard) | 20 g | 2.00% | Dijon mustard. |
| red-wine-vinegar | [Red wine vinegar](ref:ingredient/red-wine-vinegar) | 20 g | 2.00% | Red wine vinegar. |
| soy-sauce | [Soy sauce](ref:ingredient/soy-sauce) | 15 g | 1.50% | Soy sauce. |
| brown-sugar | [Brown sugar (optional)](ref:ingredient/brown-sugar) | 15 g | 1.50% | Brown sugar (optional). |
| smoked-paprika | [Smoked paprika](ref:ingredient/smoked-paprika) | 3 g | 0.30% | Smoked paprika. |
| chili-flakes | [Chili flakes](ref:ingredient/chili-flakes) | 2 g | 0.20% | Chili flakes. |
| water | [Water](ref:ingredient/water) | 60 g | 6.00% | Water. |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Simmering](ref:technique/simmering) | Gentle simmer for 5–8 min | Make a glossy sauce that clings. |

### Principles

- [Acid balance](ref:principle/acid-balance) — Vinegar balances the chutney and ketchup sweetness.

### Method

1. Warm a small splash of oil (or rendered chicken fat) in a saucepan over medium heat.
2. Sauté garlic and ginger 30–60 s until fragrant.
3. Add remaining ingredients.
4. Simmer gently 5–8 min until glossy and slightly thickened (sauce should cling, not run).
5. Adjust to taste: more vinegar for tang, more chutney/sugar for sweetness, more soy/Worcestershire for depth.

### Phase Outputs

| Key | Phase Output | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| monkey-gland-sauce | Monkey gland sauce | Warm glossy sauce ready for glazing. |

Gentle simmering reduces water and concentrates the source sugar, acid, and salt balance into a clinging glaze.

## FAILURE MODES

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pale, rubbery skin. | Skin not dried; pan not hot enough; poor skin contact. | Pat very dry, preheat pan, press skin-side down, and don't move chicken until it releases. |
| Sauce is thin and watery. | Under-reduced or too much water added. | Simmer longer at a gentle bubble until it coats a spoon; add water only to control thickness. |
| Sauce tastes flat or cloyingly sweet. | Not enough acid/salt/umami to balance chutney/ketchup. | Add vinegar for brightness and soy/Worcestershire for depth; simmer 1–2 min and re-taste. |
| Chicken is dry. | Overcooked beyond 74 C or held hot too long before serving. | Pull at 74 C, rest briefly, and glaze just before serving. |
