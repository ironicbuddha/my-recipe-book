Service-timed steakhouse plate built around low-temperature reverse sear, charcoal crust, and staggered finishing. Aim to serve at 18:30 with a final steak temperature of 52–54 C. The run-sheet is illustrative; temperatures and tenderness govern completion. If the fillet is unusually thick, start PHASE B 5–10 min earlier.

## PHASE A — GOCHUJANG SAUCE BASE (ADVANCE PREP)

Illustrative service timing:

- 17:15–17:20 or earlier.
- Active time: about 5 min.
- Cool and hold until Phase F.

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| neutral-oil | [Neutral oil](ref:ingredient/neutral-oil) | 15 g | 2.31% | Neutral oil. |
| garlic | [Garlic, grated](ref:ingredient/garlic) | 5 g | 0.77% | Garlic, grated. |
| fresh-ginger | [Ginger, grated](ref:ingredient/fresh-ginger) | 5 g | 0.77% | Ginger, grated. |
| gochujang | [Gochujang](ref:ingredient/gochujang) | 40 g | 6.15% | Gochujang. |
| soy-sauce | [Soy sauce](ref:ingredient/soy-sauce) | 15 g | 2.31% | Soy sauce. |
| rice-vinegar | [Rice vinegar](ref:ingredient/rice-vinegar) | 20 g | 3.08% | Rice vinegar. |
| honey | [Honey](ref:ingredient/honey) | 10 g | 1.54% | Honey. |
| worcestershire-sauce | [Worcestershire sauce (optional)](ref:ingredient/worcestershire-sauce) | 3 g | 0.46% | Worcestershire sauce (optional). |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Paste frying](ref:technique/paste-frying) | Gochujang for 20–30 s after aromatics | Bloom the paste for a smooth sauce base. |

### Method

1. Heat neutral oil in a small pan over medium heat.
2. Add garlic and ginger; cook 30 s until fragrant.
3. Add gochujang and fry 20–30 s to bloom the paste.
4. Stir in soy sauce, rice vinegar, honey, and Worcestershire if using.
5. Simmer 1–2 min until smooth and lightly thickened.
6. Cool and reserve for final service.

### Phase Outputs

| Key | Phase Output | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| sauce-base | Reserved gochujang sauce base | Cooled sauce base for final warming and finishing. |

## PHASE B — STEAK SEASONING AND REVERSE SEAR

Illustrative service timing:

- 17:30 Salt steak.
- 17:45 Steak into 110 C oven.
- 18:05–18:10 Pull at 46–48 C.

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| beef-fillet | [Beef fillet](ref:ingredient/beef-fillet) | 650 g | 100.00% | Beef fillet. |
| salt | [Kosher salt](ref:ingredient/salt) | 6 g | 0.92% | Kosher salt. |

### Principles

- [Protein denaturation](ref:principle/protein-denaturation) — Bring the centre toward the authored final target before the brief crusting step.

### Method

1. Remove steak from refrigeration 60 min before service (17:30 for the illustrative 18:30 service).
2. Season evenly with kosher salt on all sides.
3. Preheat oven to 110 C.
4. Place steak on a rack set over a tray.
5. Roast until internal temperature reaches 46–48 C.
6. Remove and hold at room temperature while the hibachi is prepared.

### Phase Outputs

| Key | Phase Output | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| warmed-fillet | Oven-warmed fillet | Fillet at 46–48 C internally, held for the hibachi finish. |

The low oven step brings the fillet close to its final target before the crusting finish, reducing the source concern about grey banding.

## PHASE C — STEAKHOUSE FRIES (SOAK + FIRST FRY)

**May overlap:** PHASE B — STEAK SEASONING AND REVERSE SEAR while the fillet warms.

Illustrative service timing:

- 17:40 Cut and start soaking.

2. Soak 20 min in cold water, then drain and dry thoroughly.

## PHASE D — HIBACHI CABBAGE

**May overlap:** PHASE B — STEAK SEASONING AND REVERSE SEAR while coals establish.

Illustrative service timing:

- 17:50 Light hibachi; let coals establish.
- 18:08 Cabbage onto hibachi.
- 18:12 Flip.
- 18:16 Off the fire; dress immediately.

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| cabbage | [Baby red cabbages](ref:ingredient/cabbage) | As needed | — | Use exactly 2 baby red cabbage heads, halved through the core; preserve this non-mass count. |
| neutral-oil | [Neutral oil](ref:ingredient/neutral-oil) | 10 g | 1.54% | Neutral oil. |
| salt | [Salt](ref:ingredient/salt) | 3 g | 0.46% | Salt. |
| rice-vinegar | [Rice vinegar](ref:ingredient/rice-vinegar) | 15 g | 2.31% | Rice vinegar. |
| sesame-oil | [Sesame oil](ref:ingredient/sesame-oil) | 5 g | 0.77% | Sesame oil. |
| honey | [Honey](ref:ingredient/honey) | 5 g | 0.77% | Honey. |
| sesame-seeds | [Sesame seeds (optional)](ref:ingredient/sesame-seeds) | As needed | — | Sesame seeds (optional). Source amount: to finish. |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Charring](ref:technique/charring) | Very hot coals for 3–4 min, then cooler heat for 3–4 min | Char cut faces and finish the cabbage core. |

### Principles

- [Acid balance](ref:principle/acid-balance) — Dress the cabbage with vinegar, oils, and honey.

### Method

1. Cut cabbages in half through the core.
2. Lightly oil and salt the cut faces.
3. Place cut-side down over very hot coals and char 3–4 min without moving.
4. Flip and move to slightly cooler heat.
5. Cook 3–4 min more until tender through the core.
6. Remove and dress with rice vinegar, sesame oil, honey, and sesame seeds if using.

### Phase Outputs

| Key | Phase Output | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| charred-cabbage | Charred cabbage | Four dressed cabbage halves for plating. |

The source allows cabbage a short hold before plating so it can leave the fire before the steak sear.

## PHASE E — HIBACHI SEAR AND REST

Illustrative service timing:

- 18:16–18:18 Hibachi sear.
- 18:18–18:28 Rest.
- Keep slicing board and knife ready during the rest.

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| neutral-oil | [Neutral oil](ref:ingredient/neutral-oil) | 10 g | 1.54% | Neutral oil. |
| butter | [Butter](ref:ingredient/butter) | 20 g | 3.08% | Butter. |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Searing](ref:technique/searing) | 45–60 s per side; briefly sear edges; 10 min rest | Build a charcoal crust quickly. |

### Principles

- [Maillard reaction](ref:principle/maillard-reaction) — Dry the fillet before the hot charcoal finish.
- [Carryover cooking](ref:principle/carryover-cooking) — Retain the 10 min rest and 52–54 C final target.

### Method

1. Pat steak dry and rub lightly with neutral oil.
2. Sear over the hibachi 45–60 s per side.
3. Rotate once per side if needed for even crust.
4. Briefly sear the edges.
5. Remove from the heat and add butter to coat.
6. Rest 10 min before slicing.

The brief hibachi finish adds radiant heat and smoke while limiting overshoot of the interior.

## FAILURE MODES

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pale or weak steak crust. | Surface moisture remained after the oven phase, or coals were not fully established. | Pat dry immediately before searing and wait for aggressive radiant heat before the hibachi finish. |
| Fries turn limp before plating. | First fry did not dry enough internally, or the second fry started too early. | Rest the first fry on a rack, then hold the second fry until the steak is nearly ready to rest. |
| Cabbage is burnt outside but rigid in the core. | Heat stayed too intense after the first char. | Move the cabbage to a slightly cooler zone after flipping and finish over moderated coals. |
| Sauce tastes harsh or greasy. | Gochujang was under-cooked in the base, or butter and sesame oil were added over excessive heat. | Bloom the paste fully in Phase A and whisk in finishing fats only over low heat. |
