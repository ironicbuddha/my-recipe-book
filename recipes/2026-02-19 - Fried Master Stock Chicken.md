Soy-aromatic poached chicken, air-dried and fried for blistered skin.

## PHASE A — MAKE MASTER STOCK

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| water | [Water](ref:ingredient/water) | 1500 g | 200.00% | Form the stock. |
| light-soy | [Light soy sauce](ref:ingredient/light-soy-sauce) | 150 g | 20.00% | Season the stock. |
| dark-soy | [Dark soy sauce](ref:ingredient/dark-soy-sauce) | 37.5 g | 5.00% | Add colour. |
| shaoxing | [Shaoxing wine](ref:ingredient/shaoxing-wine) | 75 g | 10.00% | Add aroma. |
| rock-sugar | [Rock sugar](ref:ingredient/rock-sugar) | 45 g | 6.00% | Balance the stock. |
| ginger | [Ginger](ref:ingredient/ginger) | 37.5 g | 5.00% | Crush for the stock. |
| garlic | [Garlic](ref:ingredient/garlic) | 22.5 g | 3.00% | Smash for the stock. |
| spring-onion | [Spring onion](ref:ingredient/spring-onion) | 60 g | 8.00% | Crush for the stock. |
| star-anise | [Star anise](ref:ingredient/star-anise) | 3 g | 0.40% | Perfume the stock. |
| cassia | [Cassia cinnamon](ref:ingredient/cassia-cinnamon) | 2.5 g | 0.33% | Perfume the stock. |
| clove | [Clove](ref:ingredient/clove) | 1 g | 0.13% | Perfume the stock. |
| mandarin-peel | [Dried mandarin peel](ref:ingredient/dried-mandarin-peel) | 1.5 g | 0.20% | Optional aromatic. |
| white-pepper | [White pepper](ref:ingredient/white-pepper) | 1.5 g | 0.20% | Season the stock. |

### Principles

- [Aromatic infusion](ref:principle/aromatic-infusion) — Gentle simmering extracts the aromatics into the stock.

### Method

1. Combine the stock ingredients, simmer gently for 10 min, and adjust with water if it tastes aggressively salty.

## PHASE B — POACH AND DRY CHICKEN

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| chicken | [Whole chicken](ref:ingredient/whole-chicken) | 750 g | 100.00% | Poach in the master stock. |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Poaching](ref:technique/poaching) | 85–90 C; 35–45 min; 72–75 C thigh. | Cook the chicken without boiling it. |
| [Air drying](ref:technique/air-drying) | Rest 20–30 min, then refrigerate uncovered 4–12 h. | Dry the skin before frying. |

### Method

1. Lower the chicken into barely simmering stock until cooked through, then cool and dry it uncovered.

## PHASE C — FRY AND GLAZE

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| cornstarch | [Cornstarch](ref:ingredient/cornstarch) | 15 g | 2.00% | Lightly dust the dry skin. |
| frying-oil | [Neutral frying oil](ref:ingredient/neutral-frying-oil) | As needed | — | Fry the chicken at 180 C. |
| honey | [Honey](ref:ingredient/honey) | 10 g | 1.33% | Optional glaze sweetener. |
| rice-vinegar | [Rice vinegar](ref:ingredient/rice-vinegar) | 10 g | 1.33% | Optional glaze acid. |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Deep frying](ref:technique/deep-frying) | 180 C; 6–8 min; rotate for even colour. | Blister and crisp the skin. |
| [Reduction](ref:technique/reduction) | Simmer reserved stock 3–5 min until syrupy. | Make the optional glaze. |

### Method

1. Dust the chicken lightly, fry until deeply golden, and rest 5 min.
2. Optionally reduce reserved stock with honey and vinegar and brush it over carved chicken.

## FAILURE MODES

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soft pale skin | Chicken was insufficiently dry. | Refrigerate uncovered until the skin feels papery. |
| Dry meat | Stock boiled or poaching ran too long. | Hold 85–90 C and pull at 72–75 C. |
