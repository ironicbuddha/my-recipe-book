Soy-aromatic poached chicken, air-dried and fried for blistered, crisp skin.

## PHASE A — MAKE MASTER STOCK

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| water | [Water](ref:ingredient/water) | 1500 g | 200.00% | Form the poaching liquid. |
| light-soy | [Light soy sauce](ref:ingredient/light-soy-sauce) | 150 g | 20.00% | Season the stock. |
| dark-soy | [Dark soy sauce](ref:ingredient/dark-soy-sauce) | 37.5 g | 5.00% | Add colour and depth. |
| shaoxing | [Shaoxing wine](ref:ingredient/shaoxing-wine) | 75 g | 10.00% | Add aromatic depth. |
| rock-sugar | [Rock sugar](ref:ingredient/rock-sugar) | 45 g | 6.00% | Balance the stock. |
| ginger | [Fresh ginger](ref:ingredient/fresh-ginger) | 37.5 g | 5.00% | Crush for the stock. |
| garlic | [Garlic](ref:ingredient/garlic) | 22.5 g | 3.00% | Smash for the stock. |
| spring-onion | [Spring onion](ref:ingredient/spring-onion) | 60 g | 8.00% | Crush for the stock. |
| star-anise | [Star anise](ref:ingredient/star-anise) | 3 g | 0.40% | Perfume the stock. |
| cassia | [Cassia cinnamon](ref:ingredient/cassia-cinnamon) | 2.5 g | 0.33% | Perfume the stock. |
| cloves | [Cloves](ref:ingredient/cloves) | 1 g | 0.13% | Perfume the stock. |
| mandarin-peel | [Dried mandarin peel](ref:ingredient/dried-mandarin-peel) | 1.5 g | 0.20% | Add optional citrus aroma. |
| white-pepper | [White pepper](ref:ingredient/white-pepper) | 1.5 g | 0.20% | Season the stock. |

### Principles

- [Aromatic infusion](ref:principle/aromatic-infusion) — A gentle simmer extracts the spices without harshness.

### Method

1. Combine the stock ingredients, bring them to a gentle simmer, and cook for 10 min.

## PHASE B — POACH CHICKEN

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| chicken | [Whole chicken](ref:ingredient/whole-chicken) | 750 g | 100.00% | Submerge in the master stock. |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Master stock poaching](ref:technique/master-stock-poaching) | 85–90 C; 35–45 min; 72–75 C thigh | Cook through while preserving tenderness. |

### Method

1. Lower the chicken into the barely simmering stock, poach to 72–75 C at the thigh, then drain on a rack.

## PHASE C — DRY AND FRY CHICKEN

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| cornstarch | [Cornstarch](ref:ingredient/cornstarch) | 15 g | 2.00% | Lightly dust the dried skin. |
| rice-flour | [Rice flour](ref:ingredient/rice-flour) | 15 g | 2.00% | Use instead of cornstarch as the alternative dusting. |
| frying-oil | [Neutral oil](ref:ingredient/neutral-oil) | As needed | — | Deep-fry the chicken. |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Air drying](ref:technique/air-drying) | 20–30 min at room temperature, then 4–12 h uncovered and chilled | Dehydrate the skin before frying. |
| [Deep frying](ref:technique/deep-frying) | 180 C; 6–8 min | Blister and crisp the skin. |

### Principles

- [Skin dehydration](ref:principle/skin-dehydration) — Dry skin fries more quickly and crisply.

### Method

1. Cool the poached chicken for 20–30 min, refrigerate it uncovered for 4–12 h, choose cornstarch or rice flour for a light dusting, then fry at 180 C until deeply golden.

## PHASE D — GLAZE AND SERVE

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| honey | [Honey](ref:ingredient/honey) | 10 g | 1.33% | Sweeten the optional glaze. |
| sugar | [Sugar](ref:ingredient/sugar) | 10 g | 1.33% | Use instead of honey to sweeten the optional glaze. |
| rice-vinegar | [Rice vinegar](ref:ingredient/rice-vinegar) | 10 g | 1.33% | Brighten the optional glaze. |

### Method

1. Reduce 125 g reserved master stock with either honey or sugar and rice vinegar until syrupy, brush it over the fried chicken if using, then rest the chicken for 5 min and carve.

## FAILURE MODES

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soft skin | The chicken was not dried sufficiently. | Refrigerate uncovered until the skin is dry before frying. |
| Tough meat | The stock boiled or the chicken overcooked. | Hold the stock at 85–90 C and stop at 72–75 C. |
