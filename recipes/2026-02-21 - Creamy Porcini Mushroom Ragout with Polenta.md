Fresh mushroom and dried porcini ragout with thyme and cream, served over crisp-fried set polenta. The porcini provide both rehydrated solids and a strained aromatic liquor; chilling fully cooked polenta gives clean edges for frying.

## PHASE A — REHYDRATE PORCINI

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| porcini | [Dried porcini](ref:ingredient/dried-porcini) | 15 g | 6.00% | Rehydrate, squeeze gently, and chop. |
| steep-water | [Water](ref:ingredient/water) | 150 g | 60.00% | Heat to 80–90 C for the infusion. |
| infusion-thyme | [Thyme](ref:ingredient/thyme) | 1 g | 0.40% | Use whole sprigs in the infusion. |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Rehydration](ref:technique/rehydration) | Covered steep for 20 min in 80–90 C water | Soften porcini and extract their aroma. |

### Principles

- [Aromatic extraction](ref:principle/aromatic-extraction) — Hot water captures porcini and thyme flavour for the ragout.

### Method

1. Combine dried porcini, thyme sprigs, and 150 g hot water in a heatproof bowl; cover and steep for 20 min.
2. Lift out the porcini, squeeze gently, and chop.
3. Strain the soaking liquid through fine mesh or a coffee filter to remove grit. Top up the strained liquor with water to 140 g total for the ragout.

### Phase Outputs

| Key | Phase Output | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| rehydrated-porcini | Rehydrated porcini | Chopped porcini solids for the ragout. |
| porcini-liquor | Porcini liquor | Strained, topped-up aromatic liquid, 140 g total. |

## PHASE B — COOK FIRM POLENTA

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| polenta-water | [Water](ref:ingredient/water) | 280 g | 112.00% | Liquid for the firm polenta. |
| milk | [Whole milk](ref:ingredient/whole-milk) | 80 g | 32.00% | Add with water. |
| polenta-salt | [Salt](ref:ingredient/salt) | 4 g | 1.60% | Season the cooking liquid. |
| polenta | [Polenta](ref:ingredient/polenta) | 110 g | 44.00% | Use coarse polenta; whisk in gradually. |
| polenta-butter | [Butter](ref:ingredient/butter) | 10 g | 4.00% | Use unsalted butter off heat. |
| parmesan | [Parmesan](ref:ingredient/parmesan) | 17.5 g | 7.00% | Finely grate and stir in off heat. |

### Principles

- [Starch gelatinisation](ref:principle/starch-gelatinisation) — Long, low cooking hydrates the coarse polenta before it sets.

### Method

1. Bring the water, milk, and salt to a low boil in a heavy pot.
2. Whisk in coarse polenta in a steady stream to prevent lumps.
3. Reduce heat and cook for 35–40 min, stirring every 2–3 min, until thick and fully tender.
4. Off heat, stir in unsalted butter and finely grated Parmesan.

### Phase Outputs

| Key | Phase Output | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cooked-polenta | Cooked polenta | Firm, fully cooked polenta ready to spread and chill. |

## PHASE C — SET AND CUT POLENTA

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| tray-oil | [Olive oil](ref:ingredient/olive-oil) | 4 g | 1.60% | Lightly coat a shallow tray. |

### Method

1. Oil a shallow tray, aiming for a polenta depth of about 2 cm.
2. Spread the hot cooked polenta evenly and smooth the top.
3. Cool to room temperature, then chill until firm for at least 2 h, ideally overnight.
4. Cut into rectangles, squares, or wedges for frying.

### Phase Outputs

## PHASE D — COOK THE MUSHROOM RAGOUT

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ragout-oil | [Olive oil](ref:ingredient/olive-oil) | 12.5 g | 5.00% | Heat in a wide sauté pan. |
| ragout-butter | [Butter](ref:ingredient/butter) | 15 g | 6.00% | Use unsalted butter with the oil. |
| shallot | [Shallot](ref:ingredient/shallot) | 40 g | 16.00% | Finely dice and sweat without browning. |
| garlic | [Garlic](ref:ingredient/garlic) | 5 g | 2.00% | Mince and add after shallot. |
| mushrooms | [Fresh mushrooms](ref:ingredient/fresh-mushrooms) | 250 g | 100.00% | Slice and brown in a wide pan. |
| wine | [White wine](ref:ingredient/white-wine) | 25 g | 10.00% | Use dry wine; reduce nearly dry. |
| cream | [Heavy cream](ref:ingredient/heavy-cream) | 110 g | 44.00% | Simmer briefly until glossy. |
| fresh-thyme | [Thyme](ref:ingredient/thyme) | 1.5 g | 0.60% | Use fresh leaves near the end. |
| lemon | [Lemon juice](ref:ingredient/lemon-juice) | 4 g | 1.60% | Finish the sauce. |
| pepper | [Black pepper](ref:ingredient/black-pepper) | 1 g | 0.40% | Season the cream. |
| final-salt | [Salt](ref:ingredient/salt) | As needed | — | Adjust after reduction. |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Sweating](ref:technique/sweating) | Shallot for 3–4 min without browning | Soften the aromatic base. |
| [Reduction](ref:technique/reduction) | Wine nearly dry; porcini liquor reduced by about one-third | Concentrate savoury mushroom flavour while keeping sauce spoonable. |

### Principles

- [Maillard reaction](ref:principle/maillard-reaction) — Evaporating fresh mushroom moisture allows browning instead of steaming.
- [Fat as flavour carrier](ref:principle/fat-as-flavour-carrier) — Oil, butter, and cream carry the mushroom and thyme aroma.

### Method

1. Heat olive oil and unsalted butter over medium heat in a wide sauté pan.
2. Add shallot with a pinch of the final salt and sweat for 3–4 min without browning; add garlic for 30 s.
3. Add the fresh mushrooms and cook over medium-high heat until their moisture releases and they begin to brown. Avoid crowding.
4. Add dry white wine and reduce until nearly dry.
5. Add the 140 g reserved porcini liquor and chopped rehydrated porcini; simmer until reduced by about one-third.
6. Add cream, fresh thyme leaves, and black pepper; simmer gently for 3–5 min until glossy and spoonable.
7. Finish with lemon juice and adjust salt.

### Phase Outputs Used

| Phase Output | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Rehydrated porcini | Add all of `rehydrated-porcini` after reducing the wine. |
| Porcini liquor | Add all 140 g of `porcini-liquor` with the porcini solids. |

### Phase Outputs

| Key | Phase Output | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| mushroom-ragout | Mushroom ragout | Glossy, spoonable sauce for plating. |

## PHASE E — FRY POLENTA AND PLATE

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| frying-oil | [Neutral oil](ref:ingredient/neutral-oil) | 17.5 g | 7.00% | Heat over medium heat for frying. |
| frying-butter | [Butter](ref:ingredient/butter) | 10 g | 4.00% | Use unsalted butter for the final minute of basting. |

### Method

1. Preheat a wide pan with neutral oil over medium heat.
2. Fry the set polenta pieces for 3–4 min per side until deeply golden and crisp, letting a crust form before turning.
3. Add unsalted butter for the final minute and baste lightly, then drain briefly on a rack or paper.
4. Plate the fried polenta with mushroom ragout over or alongside; finish with extra thyme leaves if desired.

### Phase Outputs Used

| Phase Output | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Set polenta | Fry all of `set-polenta`. |
| Mushroom ragout | Spoon `mushroom-ragout` over or beside the polenta. |

## FAILURE MODES

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Polenta falls apart in the pan | It was undercooked or insufficiently chilled. | Cook until thick and tender, then chill at least 2 h. |
| Mushrooms taste steamed | The pan was crowded or too cool. | Use a wide pan over medium-high heat to evaporate moisture. |
| Polenta is lumpy | It was added too fast without whisking. | Stream it gradually into actively whisked liquid. |
| Fried polenta stays pale and soft | Pan was too cool or pieces moved too soon. | Preheat fully and let the crust form before turning. |
| Thyme flavour is dull | Thyme simmered too long in cream. | Add fresh leaves near the end and simmer briefly. |
