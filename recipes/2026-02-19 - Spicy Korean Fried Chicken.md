Double-fried chicken in a starch coating with a sweet, spicy yangnyeom sauce.

## PHASE A — SEASON CHICKEN

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| chicken-wings | [Chicken wings](ref:ingredient/chicken-wings) | 500 g | 100.00% | Use as the wing option. |
| chicken-drumettes | [Chicken drumettes](ref:ingredient/chicken-drumettes) | 500 g | 100.00% | Use instead as the drumette option. |
| salt | [Salt](ref:ingredient/salt) | 5 g | 1.00% | Season the chicken. |
| black-pepper | [Black pepper](ref:ingredient/black-pepper) | 1 g | 0.20% | Season the chicken. |
| garlic-powder | [Garlic powder](ref:ingredient/garlic-powder) | 2.5 g | 0.50% | Season the chicken. |
| rice-wine | [Rice wine](ref:ingredient/rice-wine) | 7.5 g | 1.50% | Add optional aromatic seasoning. |

### Method

1. Choose either chicken wings or chicken drumettes, combine with the seasonings and rice wine, and rest for 10–20 min at room temperature.

## PHASE B — BATTER AND COAT

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| cornstarch | [Cornstarch](ref:ingredient/cornstarch) | 35 g | 7.00% | Form the crisp coating. |
| gluten-free-flour | [Gluten-free flour mix](ref:ingredient/gluten-free-flour-mix) | 15 g | 3.00% | Support the crisp coating. |
| baking-powder | [Baking powder](ref:ingredient/baking-powder) | 1 g | 0.20% | Lighten the batter. |
| egg | [Egg](ref:ingredient/egg) | 25 g | 5.00% | Bind the batter. |
| water | [Water](ref:ingredient/water) | 60 g | 12.00% | Make a cold, lightly runny batter. |

### Method

1. Combine the dry coating ingredients, whisk in egg and cold water, coat the seasoned chicken, let excess drip off, and rest it on a rack for 5 min.

## PHASE C — DOUBLE FRY

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| frying-oil | [Neutral oil](ref:ingredient/neutral-oil) | As needed | — | Fry the coated chicken. |

### Technique Applications

| Technique | Controls | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Double frying](ref:technique/double-frying) | 160 C for 7–8 min, then 185–190 C for 2–3 min | Cook the chicken then dehydrate the coating. |

### Principles

- [Starch dehydration](ref:principle/starch-dehydration) — The second fry dries the coating for lasting crispness.

### Method

1. Fry at 160 C until cooked through but pale, drain, then fry at 185–190 C until deeply golden and audibly crisp.

## PHASE D — MAKE SAUCE AND SERVE

### Ingredient Uses

| Key | Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| gochujang | [Gochujang](ref:ingredient/gochujang) | 20 g | 4.00% | Build the sauce. |
| ketchup | [Ketchup](ref:ingredient/ketchup) | 30 g | 6.00% | Build the sauce. |
| light-soy | [Light soy sauce](ref:ingredient/light-soy-sauce) | 15 g | 3.00% | Season the sauce. |
| honey | [Honey](ref:ingredient/honey) | 22.5 g | 4.50% | Sweeten the sauce. |
| brown-sugar | [Brown sugar](ref:ingredient/brown-sugar) | 22.5 g | 4.50% | Use instead of honey to sweeten the sauce. |
| rice-vinegar | [Rice vinegar](ref:ingredient/rice-vinegar) | 15 g | 3.00% | Balance the sauce. |
| garlic | [Garlic](ref:ingredient/garlic) | 5 g | 1.00% | Grate into the sauce. |
| ginger | [Fresh ginger](ref:ingredient/fresh-ginger) | 2.5 g | 0.50% | Grate into the sauce. |
| gochugaru | [Gochugaru](ref:ingredient/gochugaru) | 5 g | 1.00% | Add heat. |
| sesame-oil | [Sesame oil](ref:ingredient/sesame-oil) | 7.5 g | 1.50% | Stir in off heat. |

### Principles

- [Sauce adhesion](ref:principle/sauce-adhesion) — A reduced sauce coats the crisp chicken without soaking it.

### Method

1. Simmer all sauce ingredients except sesame oil for 2–3 min until slightly thickened, using either honey or brown sugar, stir in sesame oil off heat, toss the hot fried chicken in the warm sauce, and serve immediately.

## FAILURE MODES

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soggy coating | The chicken was insufficiently drained or over-sauced. | Drain after the second fry and toss lightly just before serving. |
| Pale, soft coating | The second fry was skipped or too cool. | Complete the 185–190 C second fry. |
