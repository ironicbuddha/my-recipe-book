# TOMATO & ONION CURRY CHUTNEY

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields approximately 650 g chutney  
Sweet-sour tomato and onion accompaniment tempered with warm curry spices

---

## INGREDIENTS

### CHUTNEY

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Ripe tomato, diced | 600 g | 100% |
| Onion, thinly sliced | 300 g | 50% |
| Neutral oil | 30 g | 5% |
| Brown sugar | 60 g | 10% |
| Malt or apple cider vinegar | 80 g | 13.3% |
| Curry powder | 10 g | 1.7% |
| Mustard seed | 5 g | 0.8% |
| Ground cumin | 3 g | 0.5% |
| Ground turmeric | 2 g | 0.3% |
| Fresh ginger, finely grated | 15 g | 2.5% |
| Garlic, finely grated | 8 g | 1.3% |
| Fine salt | 9 g | 1.5% |
| Red chilli, finely chopped | 10 g | 1.7% |

---

## PROCEDURE

### TEMPER

1. Heat oil over medium heat. Add mustard seed and cook until it begins to pop.
2. Add onion and salt; cook for 12-15 min until soft and lightly golden.
3. Add curry powder, cumin, turmeric, ginger, garlic, and chilli; cook for 1 min.

### REDUCE

4. Add tomato, sugar, and vinegar.
5. Simmer uncovered for 35-45 min, stirring frequently, until thick but still spoonable.
6. Cool, then refrigerate for at least 12 h before serving.

---

## NOTES

- Reconstructed quantities; tomato sweetness and water content will change the final reduction time.
- The chutney should balance sweetness, acidity, salt, and warm spice rather than taste like tomato jam.
- **Story:** This is one of the project's curry-table accompaniments, designed to bring bright sweet-sour contrast to a rich main dish.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **Smooth:** Pulse briefly after cooling while retaining some texture.
- **Raisins:** Add 60 g raisins for the final 20 min of cooking.
