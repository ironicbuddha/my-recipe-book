# BANANA & DESICCATED COCONUT CURRY ACCOMPANIMENT

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields approximately 500 g; 6 accompaniment portions  
Ripe banana, toasted coconut, lime, and gentle chilli for cooling sweet contrast beside curry

---

## INGREDIENTS

### ACCOMPANIMENT

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Ripe but firm banana, sliced | 350 g | 100% |
| Desiccated coconut | 80 g | 22.9% |
| Lime juice | 35 g | 10% |
| Plain yoghurt | 100 g | 28.6% |
| Fine salt | 3 g | 0.9% |
| Ground cumin, toasted | 1 g | 0.3% |
| Red chilli, finely chopped | 5 g | 1.4% |
| Mint leaves, finely sliced | 10 g | 2.9% |

---

## PROCEDURE

### TOAST COCONUT

1. Toast desiccated coconut in a dry pan over medium-low heat for 3-5 min until pale gold. Cool completely.

### ASSEMBLE

2. Whisk lime juice, yoghurt, salt, cumin, and chilli.
3. Fold banana gently through the dressing.
4. Fold in 60 g toasted coconut and mint.
5. Transfer to a serving bowl and finish with remaining 20 g coconut.

---

## NOTES

- The yoghurt element is reconstructed and may not have been present in the original project version.
- Assemble no more than 30 min before serving to prevent the banana softening.
- **Story:** This old-school curry-table side leans into the project's affection for food that carries nostalgia as strongly as flavour.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **Dry version:** Omit yoghurt and increase lime juice to 45 g.
- **Pineapple:** Replace 100 g banana with 100 g finely diced pineapple.
