# PRESSURE-COOKER BLACK BEANS FOR TACOS

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields approximately 900 g cooked beans  
Tender, savoury black beans with enough concentrated liquor to spoon into tacos

---

## INGREDIENTS

### BEANS

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dried black beans, rinsed | 400 g | 100% |
| Water | 1200 g | 300% |
| White onion, halved | 150 g | 37.5% |
| Garlic, crushed | 15 g | 3.8% |
| Bay leaf | 1 g | 0.3% |
| Ground cumin | 3 g | 0.8% |
| Dried oregano | 2 g | 0.5% |
| Fine salt | 10 g | 2.5% |
| Neutral oil | 20 g | 5% |

### FINISH

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cooked black beans with liquor | 900 g | 100% |
| Lime juice | 25 g | 2.8% |
| Fine salt | 3 g | 0.3% |
| Coriander leaves, chopped | 20 g | 2.2% |

---

## PROCEDURE

### PRESSURE COOK

1. Combine beans, water, onion, garlic, bay leaf, cumin, oregano, salt, and oil in a pressure cooker.
2. Cook at high pressure for 35 min.
3. Allow pressure to release naturally for 20 min, then release any remaining pressure.

### REDUCE AND FINISH

4. Discard onion and bay leaf. Simmer uncovered for 10-20 min until the liquor lightly coats the beans.
5. Stir in lime juice and coriander. Adjust salt.
6. For taco service, crush approximately 20% of the beans into the liquor to make the mixture cohesive.

---

## NOTES

- Bean age changes pressure-cooking time substantially; add 5-10 min if the beans remain chalky.
- Reconstructed as an unsoaked method. Do not fill the pressure cooker above half capacity when cooking dried beans.
- **Story:** These beans were developed as a practical taco component rather than a brothy bowl, so the final reduction matters.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **Smoky:** Add 10 g minced chipotle in adobo before pressure cooking.
- **Refried texture:** Mash 500 g finished beans in 30 g hot oil until thick and spreadable.
