# AIR-FRYER ROAST POTATOES

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields 4 side portions; approximately 750 g finished potatoes  
Parboiled, roughed-up potatoes with crisp craggy edges and fluffy centres

---

## INGREDIENTS

### POTATOES

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Floury potatoes, peeled and cut into 50 mm pieces | 1000 g | 100% |
| Water | 2000 g | 200% |
| Fine salt | 20 g | 2% |
| Bicarbonate of soda | 4 g | 0.4% |
| Duck fat or neutral oil | 70 g | 7% |
| Fine salt, for finishing | 5 g | 0.5% |
| Black pepper | 2 g | 0.2% |

---

## PROCEDURE

### PARBOIL

1. Bring water, 20 g salt, and bicarbonate of soda to a boil.
2. Add potatoes and simmer for 8-10 min until the exteriors are tender but the centres remain firm.
3. Drain and steam-dry for 5 min.

### ROUGH AND COAT

4. Shake potatoes in the pot until a starchy paste forms on the edges.
5. Fold through fat or oil. Cool uncovered for 10 min.

### AIR FRY

6. Heat air fryer to 200 °C.
7. Cook potatoes in a single loose layer for 25-35 min, turning every 8-10 min, until deeply golden and crisp.
8. Season immediately with finishing salt and pepper.

---

## NOTES

- Cooking time is reconstructed and depends on air-fryer load and potato variety.
- Do not crowd the basket; cook in two batches if necessary.
- **Story:** This project side chases classic roast-potato contrast with the weeknight convenience of an air fryer.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **Garlic and rosemary:** Toss with 5 g rosemary and 10 g roasted-garlic paste for the final 5 min.
- **Beef fat:** Replace duck fat or oil with rendered beef fat.
