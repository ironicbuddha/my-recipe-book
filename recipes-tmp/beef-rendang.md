# BEEF RENDANG

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields 8 portions; approximately 1.8 kg finished rendang  
Beef slowly cooked in coconut milk and aromatics until the sauce fries down to a dark, clinging paste

---

## INGREDIENTS

### SPICE PASTE

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Shallot or red onion, chopped | 300 g | 100% |
| Garlic | 40 g | 13.3% |
| Ginger | 60 g | 20% |
| Galangal | 50 g | 16.7% |
| Red chilli, seeded as desired | 100 g | 33.3% |
| Lemongrass, tender inner core | 50 g | 16.7% |
| Ground coriander | 10 g | 3.3% |
| Ground cumin | 5 g | 1.7% |
| Ground turmeric | 5 g | 1.7% |
| Neutral oil | 80 g | 26.7% |

### RENDANG

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Beef chuck, cut into 50 mm pieces | 1600 g | 100% |
| Coconut milk | 1200 g | 75% |
| Spice paste | 700 g | 43.8% |
| Lemongrass, bruised | 40 g | 2.5% |
| Makrut lime leaves | 5 g | 0.3% |
| Tamarind concentrate | 30 g | 1.9% |
| Palm sugar | 45 g | 2.8% |
| Fine salt | 22 g | 1.4% |
| Toasted desiccated coconut, finely ground | 100 g | 6.3% |

---

## PROCEDURE

### SPICE PASTE

1. Blend onion, garlic, ginger, galangal, chilli, lemongrass, spices, and oil to a fine paste.
2. Fry the paste in a wide heavy pot over medium heat for 12-15 min until aromatic and the oil begins to separate.

### SLOW COOK

3. Add beef and turn to coat. Add coconut milk, bruised lemongrass, lime leaves, tamarind, sugar, and salt.
4. Bring to a gentle simmer. Cook uncovered over low heat for 2.5-3.5 h, stirring more frequently as the liquid reduces.

### FRY DOWN

5. When the beef is tender and the coconut oil separates, add ground toasted coconut.
6. Continue cooking and folding gently for 20-40 min until the paste is dark, dry, and clings to the beef.
7. Remove lemongrass and lime leaves. Rest for at least 30 min before serving.

---

## NOTES

- The formulation is reconstructed; rendang is finished by reduction and frying, not merely by tenderising the beef.
- A wide pot makes the final evaporation controllable. Stir carefully once the beef is tender.
- **Story:** Rendang sits in the project as a slow-cooked counterpoint to quick curries—the long reduction is the recipe's whole damn thesis.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **Make ahead:** Cool after the slow-cook phase, refrigerate overnight, then complete the fry-down the next day.
- **Milder:** Reduce fresh chilli to 50 g and add 50 g red pepper.
