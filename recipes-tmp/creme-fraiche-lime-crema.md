# CRÈME FRAÎCHE LIME CREMA

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields approximately 330 g crema  
Cool, tangy finishing sauce for tacos, nachos, and grilled food

---

## INGREDIENTS

### CREMA

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Crème fraîche | 250 g | 100% |
| Lime juice | 35 g | 14% |
| Lime zest | 2 g | 0.8% |
| Water | 30 g | 12% |
| Fine salt | 3 g | 1.2% |
| Garlic, finely grated | 2 g | 0.8% |
| Ground cumin | 1 g | 0.4% |

---

## PROCEDURE

### MIX

1. Whisk crème fraîche until smooth.
2. Whisk in lime juice, zest, water, salt, garlic, and cumin.
3. Rest under refrigeration for 20 min.
4. Adjust with 5-15 g water to reach a spoonable or squeezable consistency.

---

## NOTES

- Reconstructed quantities; lime acidity varies, so add the final 10 g juice to taste.
- Keeps refrigerated for up to 3 days.
- **Story:** This reusable project component brings cool acidity to the richer taco and nacho recipes.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **Herb crema:** Blend in 20 g coriander leaves.
- **Smoky crema:** Add 10 g chipotle in adobo.
