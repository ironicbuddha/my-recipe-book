# BUTTER-BRAISED LEEKS

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields 4 side portions; approximately 650 g finished leeks  
Leeks gently braised until silky, sweet, and glazed with their cooking liquor

---

## INGREDIENTS

### LEEKS

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Trimmed leeks, white and pale-green parts | 800 g | 100% |
| Unsalted butter | 80 g | 10% |
| Vegetable stock | 250 g | 31.3% |
| Fine salt | 8 g | 1% |
| White pepper | 1 g | 0.1% |
| Thyme | 2 g | 0.3% |
| Lemon juice | 15 g | 1.9% |

---

## PROCEDURE

### PREPARATION

1. Cut leeks into 120-150 mm lengths and halve lengthwise, keeping the root ends intact.
2. Wash thoroughly between the layers and drain.

### BRAISE

3. Melt 50 g butter in a wide pan. Arrange leeks cut-side down in one layer.
4. Add stock, salt, white pepper, and thyme. Bring to a gentle simmer.
5. Cover with a lid or parchment cartouche and braise at 160 °C for 30-40 min, until completely tender.

### GLAZE

6. Transfer leeks carefully to a warm plate.
7. Reduce the braising liquid over medium heat until lightly syrupy.
8. Whisk in remaining 30 g butter and lemon juice. Spoon over the leeks.

---

## NOTES

- Reconstructed quantities; the exact stock needed depends on pan width.
- Avoid boiling aggressively, which can separate the layers before the core is tender.
- **Story:** This is the project's quiet, classical vegetable side—the sort of simple treatment that lets good seasonal produce do the talking.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **Brown-butter finish:** Brown the final 30 g butter before whisking it into the reduced liquor.
- **Mustard glaze:** Add 10 g Dijon mustard with the lemon juice.
