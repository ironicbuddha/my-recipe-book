# LASAGNA BOLOGNESE WITH BECHAMEL

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields 8 portions; approximately 2.8 kg finished lasagna  
Fresh 00-flour pasta layered with slow-cooked ragù, béchamel, and Parmigiano Reggiano  
The ragù, pasta, and béchamel are consolidated here as components of one canonical recipe

## PROCEDURE

### RAGÙ

1. Render pancetta over medium-low heat for 5 min. Add olive oil, onion, carrot, and celery; cook for 12-15 min until soft but not browned.
2. Add beef and pork. Cook over medium-high heat, breaking up the meat, until its moisture evaporates and the meat begins to brown.
3. Stir in tomato paste and cook for 2 min. Add wine and reduce until nearly dry.
4. Add milk and simmer until absorbed. Add passata, stock, salt, pepper, and nutmeg.
5. Simmer uncovered over very low heat for 2-3 h, stirring periodically, until rich and spoonable. Cool before assembly.

### PASTA

6. Mix flour and salt. Add egg and knead for 8-10 min until smooth.
7. Wrap and rest for 30 min at room temperature.
8. Roll progressively to approximately 1 mm thick. Cut sheets to fit the baking dish.
9. Blanch sheets in salted boiling water for 30 sec, chill in cold water, and dry on clean cloths.

### BÉCHAMEL

10. Warm milk to 70-80 °C.
11. Melt butter, whisk in flour, and cook for 2 min without colouring.
12. Whisk in warm milk gradually. Simmer for 8-10 min until smooth and lightly thickened.
13. Season with salt, nutmeg, and white pepper.

### ASSEMBLY AND BAKING

14. Heat oven to 180 °C. Butter a 330 mm x 230 mm baking dish.
15. Spread a thin layer of béchamel in the dish. Add pasta, ragù, béchamel, and Parmigiano; repeat for 5-6 pasta layers.
16. Finish with béchamel and Parmigiano. Dot with butter.
17. Bake for 40-45 min until bubbling and deeply browned at the edges.
18. Rest for 20-30 min before cutting.

## NOTES

- The quantities and timing are reconstructed; test the moisture balance before promoting this recipe from draft.
- Ragù and béchamel should both be loose enough to hydrate the fresh pasta without flooding the finished lasagna.
- **Story:** This consolidates the project's separate lasagna, ragù, béchamel, and fresh-pasta work into one keeper protocol.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **Make ahead:** Assemble, refrigerate for up to 24 h, then add approximately 10 min to the covered portion of baking.
- **Drier ragù:** Add 100-150 g extra stock during assembly rather than loosening the entire batch.
