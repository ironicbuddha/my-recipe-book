# NACHO CHEESE SAUCE

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields approximately 650 g sauce  
Smooth, pourable cheddar sauce stabilised with sodium citrate for clean reheating

---

## INGREDIENTS

### CHEESE SAUCE

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cheddar, finely grated | 400 g | 100% |
| Whole milk | 250 g | 62.5% |
| Sodium citrate | 10 g | 2.5% |
| Pickled jalapeño brine | 25 g | 6.3% |
| Smoked paprika | 2 g | 0.5% |
| Garlic powder | 1 g | 0.3% |
| Cayenne pepper | 0.5 g | 0.1% |

---

## PROCEDURE

### BASE

1. Combine milk and sodium citrate in a saucepan.
2. Heat to 75-80 °C, whisking until the sodium citrate dissolves.

### EMULSION

3. Add cheddar in four additions, blending or whisking each addition smooth before adding the next.
4. Blend in jalapeño brine, paprika, garlic powder, and cayenne.
5. Hold at 60 °C for service, or cool rapidly and refrigerate.

### REHEAT

6. Reheat gently to 65 °C while stirring. Add 10-30 g milk if the sauce is too thick.

---

## NOTES

- The formulation is reconstructed. Cheddar moisture varies; reserve up to 50 g extra milk for adjustment.
- Do not boil after adding cheese or the emulsion may become grainy.
- **Story:** This sauce is the molten glue in the project's nacho system—engineered to stay smooth instead of congealing into orange roof tiles.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **No sodium citrate:** Replace it with a roux made from 25 g butter and 25 g flour; the sauce will be less fluid when reheated.
- **Hot:** Blend in 30 g minced pickled jalapeño.
