# SALSA VERDE

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields approximately 350 g sauce  
Italian-style parsley, caper, anchovy, and lemon sauce for roast meat, fish, and vegetables

---

## INGREDIENTS

### SALSA VERDE

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Flat-leaf parsley leaves | 100 g | 100% |
| Extra-virgin olive oil | 180 g | 180% |
| Capers, rinsed and drained | 35 g | 35% |
| Anchovy fillets | 25 g | 25% |
| Garlic | 8 g | 8% |
| Lemon juice | 35 g | 35% |
| Dijon mustard | 15 g | 15% |
| Fine salt | 2 g | 2% |
| Black pepper | 1 g | 1% |

---

## PROCEDURE

### CHOP

1. Dry parsley thoroughly.
2. Finely chop parsley, capers, anchovy, and garlic by hand, keeping visible texture.

### EMULSIFY

3. Whisk lemon juice and mustard together.
4. Stir in chopped ingredients, then fold in olive oil.
5. Season with salt and pepper. Rest for 20 min before serving.

---

## NOTES

- The project history does not identify whether the original was Italian or tomatillo-based; this draft uses the Italian style associated with roast meats.
- Do not blend to a smooth purée; hand-chopping preserves texture and colour.
- **Story:** Salsa verde appears repeatedly around the project's meat and vegetable cooking, earning a reusable protocol of its own.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **Mint:** Replace 25 g parsley with 25 g mint leaves.
- **Without anchovy:** Omit anchovy and increase capers by 15 g.
