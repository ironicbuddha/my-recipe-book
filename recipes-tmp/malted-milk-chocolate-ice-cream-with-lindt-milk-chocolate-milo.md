# MALTED MILK CHOCOLATE ICE CREAM WITH LINDT MILK CHOCOLATE & MILO

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields approximately 750 mL finished ice cream  
Milk-chocolate ice cream layered with malt from both malted milk powder and Milo  
Still in development in the original project history

---

## INGREDIENTS

### MALTED MILK CHOCOLATE BASE

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Whole milk | 300 g | 100% |
| Cream, approximately 35% fat | 150 g | 50% |
| Lindt milk chocolate, finely chopped | 120 g | 40% |
| Milo powder | 45 g | 15% |
| Malted milk powder | 30 g | 10% |
| Sucrose | 50 g | 16.7% |
| Dextrose | 20 g | 6.7% |
| Ice cream stabiliser | 1.5 g | 0.5% |
| Fine salt | 1.5 g | 0.5% |

---

## PROCEDURE

### DRY MIX

1. Whisk Milo, malted milk powder, sucrose, dextrose, stabiliser, and salt together.

### MIX AND PASTEURISE

2. Combine milk and cream. Blend in the dry mixture.
3. Heat while stirring to 82 °C and hold for 30 sec.
4. Pour the hot mixture over the chopped milk chocolate. Rest for 1 min, then blend until glossy and homogeneous.

### AGE AND CHURN

5. Cool to below 4 °C as quickly as possible.
6. Refrigerate for 6-12 h.
7. Blend the cold base for 20 sec, then churn to approximately -6 °C.
8. Pack into a chilled container and harden at -18 °C for at least 4 h.

---

## NOTES

- This recipe was explicitly still in development; the quantities are a best-effort starting formulation, not a tested final protocol.
- Milo and milk chocolate both add sugar. Do not increase either without rebalancing sweetness and freezing point.
- Milk chocolate can make the base feel waxy if overused; test texture after a full 24 h hardening cycle.
- **Story:** This is pure project ice-cream-lab energy: Lindt for clean milk chocolate, Milo for nostalgia, and malt because apparently subtlety had the evening off.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **Chocolate-malt ripple:** Reserve 100 g base before churning, blend with 20 g Milo, cook to 85 °C, cool, and layer sparingly after churning.
- **Crunch:** Fold in 80 g frozen chocolate-coated malted cereal during the final 30 sec of churning.
