# VANILLA BEAN ICE CREAM

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields approximately 750 mL finished ice cream  
The project's smoother vanilla base, balanced for a dense texture and clean vanilla flavour

---

## INGREDIENTS

### ICE CREAM BASE

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Whole milk | 330 g | 100% |
| Cream, approximately 35% fat | 200 g | 60.6% |
| Sucrose | 85 g | 25.8% |
| Dextrose | 25 g | 7.6% |
| Skim milk powder | 25 g | 7.6% |
| Egg yolk | 50 g | 15.2% |
| Vanilla bean | 5 g | 1.5% |
| Ice cream stabiliser | 1.5 g | 0.5% |
| Fine salt | 1 g | 0.3% |

---

## PROCEDURE

### INFUSE

1. Split the vanilla bean and scrape the seeds into the milk. Add the pod.
2. Heat milk and vanilla to 70 °C. Cover and infuse for 30 min.

### MIX AND PASTEURISE

3. Whisk sucrose, dextrose, skim milk powder, stabiliser, and salt together.
4. Add cream to the infused milk. Blend in the dry mixture.
5. Whisk egg yolk in a separate bowl. Temper with the warm dairy mixture, then recombine.
6. Heat while stirring to 82-84 °C and hold for 30 sec. Do not boil.

### AGE AND CHURN

7. Remove the vanilla pod. Blend for 30 sec, then cool the base to below 4 °C as quickly as possible.
8. Refrigerate for 6-12 h.
9. Churn according to the machine's instructions until the draw temperature is approximately -6 °C.
10. Pack into a chilled container and harden at -18 °C for at least 4 h.

---

## NOTES

- This is a best-effort reconstruction of the named smoother 750 mL base; stabiliser blends differ, so follow the manufacturer's maximum dosage if lower.
- Mix the stabiliser thoroughly with the sugars before adding it to prevent clumping.
- **Story:** Vanilla is the control recipe in the project's growing ice-cream lab: simple enough that every texture decision is exposed.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **Without egg:** Omit yolk, increase milk by 35 g and skim milk powder by 15 g.
- **Vanilla paste:** Replace the bean with 8 g high-quality vanilla paste.
