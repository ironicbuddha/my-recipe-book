# MAPLE-BOURBON BUTTER PECAN ICE CREAM

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields approximately 900 mL finished ice cream  
Maple custard ice cream folded with buttery toasted pecans and a restrained bourbon finish

---

## INGREDIENTS

### MAPLE ICE CREAM BASE

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Whole milk | 300 g | 100% |
| Cream, approximately 35% fat | 180 g | 60% |
| Sucrose | 70 g | 23.3% |
| Maple syrup | 70 g | 23.3% |
| Dextrose | 20 g | 6.7% |
| Skim milk powder | 30 g | 10% |
| Egg yolk | 45 g | 15% |
| Ice cream stabiliser | 1.5 g | 0.5% |
| Fine salt | 1 g | 0.3% |

### MAPLE-BOURBON BUTTER PECANS

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Pecan halves | 160 g | 100% |
| Unsalted butter | 35 g | 21.9% |
| Maple syrup | 50 g | 31.3% |
| Bourbon | 30 g | 18.8% |
| Fine salt | 2 g | 1.3% |

---

## PROCEDURE

### BUTTER PECANS

1. Toast pecans at 160 °C for 8-10 min. Cool and chop into 8-12 mm pieces.
2. Melt butter over medium heat. Add maple syrup and cook for 1 min.
3. Remove from heat, add bourbon and salt, then return to low heat for 2 min.
4. Fold in pecans. Spread on a lined tray and cool completely; freeze for 30 min before churning the ice cream.

### ICE CREAM BASE

5. Whisk sucrose, dextrose, skim milk powder, stabiliser, and salt together.
6. Combine milk, cream, and maple syrup. Blend in the dry mixture and heat to 70 °C.
7. Temper egg yolk with the warm mixture, recombine, and heat while stirring to 82-84 °C for 30 sec.
8. Blend for 30 sec and cool to below 4 °C as quickly as possible.
9. Refrigerate for 6-12 h.

### CHURN AND PACK

10. Churn to approximately -6 °C.
11. Fold in 180 g cold maple-bourbon butter pecans during the final 30 sec.
12. Pack and harden at -18 °C for at least 4 h.

---

## NOTES

- This consolidates the project's butter pecan and maple-bourbon mix-in versions; all quantities are reconstructed.
- Too much residual alcohol softens ice cream. Cook the bourbon briefly and do not increase it without rebalancing the base.
- **Story:** Butter pecan becomes the ice-cream lab's more grown-up scoop here—maple warmth, toasted nuts, and just enough bourbon to wave from the back row.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **Classic butter pecan:** Omit bourbon and replace it with 20 g maple syrup.
- **Stronger maple:** Add 2 g maple extract to the cold aged base.
