# HOME-STYLE CHICKEN CURRY WITH COCONUT MILK, POTATOES & PEAS

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields 6 portions; approximately 2.2 kg finished curry  
A gentle, comforting chicken curry with a coconut-rich gravy, soft potatoes, and peas

---

## INGREDIENTS

### CHICKEN

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bone-in chicken thighs and drumsticks | 1200 g | 100% |
| Fine salt | 18 g | 1.5% |
| Ground turmeric | 4 g | 0.3% |
| Neutral oil | 40 g | 3.3% |

### CURRY BASE

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Onion, thinly sliced | 350 g | 100% |
| Garlic, finely grated | 25 g | 7.1% |
| Ginger, finely grated | 25 g | 7.1% |
| Curry powder | 20 g | 5.7% |
| Ground cumin | 5 g | 1.4% |
| Ground coriander | 6 g | 1.7% |
| Tomato, chopped | 300 g | 85.7% |
| Coconut milk | 800 g | 228.6% |
| Chicken stock | 300 g | 85.7% |
| Potato, peeled and cut into 35 mm pieces | 600 g | 171.4% |
| Frozen peas | 200 g | 57.1% |
| Garam masala | 5 g | 1.4% |
| Lemon juice | 25 g | 7.1% |
| Coriander leaves, chopped | 30 g | 8.6% |

---

## PROCEDURE

### SEASON AND BROWN

1. Season chicken with salt and turmeric; rest for 20 min.
2. Heat oil in a heavy pot. Brown chicken in batches for 3-4 min per side. Reserve.

### BUILD CURRY

3. Cook onion in the same pot over medium heat for 15-20 min until golden.
4. Add garlic and ginger; cook for 2 min. Add curry powder, cumin, and coriander; cook for 1 min.
5. Add tomato and cook for 8-10 min until reduced and glossy.
6. Add coconut milk and stock. Return chicken to the pot and simmer gently, covered, for 25 min.

### POTATOES AND PEAS

7. Add potatoes and simmer uncovered for 25-30 min until tender and the chicken reaches at least 74 °C.
8. Add peas and cook for 4 min.
9. Stir in garam masala and lemon juice. Rest for 10 min, then finish with coriander.

---

## NOTES

- Spice blend and ratios are reconstructed; the strength of curry powder varies widely.
- Keep the simmer gentle after adding coconut milk to avoid splitting the gravy.
- **Story:** The project names this explicitly as a home-style curry: generous, familiar, and built around potatoes soaking up the coconut gravy.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **Boneless:** Use 900 g boneless thigh and reduce the first simmer to 15 min.
- **Hotter:** Add 20 g sliced green chilli with the garlic and ginger.
