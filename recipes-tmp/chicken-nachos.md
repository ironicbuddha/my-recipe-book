# CHICKEN NACHOS

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields 4 generous portions; approximately 1.5 kg finished nachos  
Layered tortilla chips, spiced chicken, black beans, cheese sauce, and fresh garnishes

---

## INGREDIENTS

### SPICED CHICKEN

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Boneless skinless chicken thigh | 600 g | 100% |
| Neutral oil | 25 g | 4.2% |
| Lime juice | 25 g | 4.2% |
| Ground cumin | 4 g | 0.7% |
| Smoked paprika | 4 g | 0.7% |
| Garlic powder | 3 g | 0.5% |
| Fine salt | 9 g | 1.5% |
| Black pepper | 2 g | 0.3% |

### ASSEMBLY

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Tortilla chips | 300 g | 100% |
| Cooked spiced chicken, chopped | 500 g | 166.7% |
| Cooked black beans, drained | 250 g | 83.3% |
| Nacho cheese sauce | 400 g | 133.3% |
| Cheddar or Monterey Jack, grated | 150 g | 50% |
| Black bean, corn, mint, and Peppadew salsa | 250 g | 83.3% |
| Crème fraîche lime crema | 150 g | 50% |
| Pickled jalapeño, sliced | 50 g | 16.7% |
| Coriander leaves | 20 g | 6.7% |

---

## PROCEDURE

### CHICKEN

1. Combine chicken with oil, lime juice, spices, salt, and pepper. Rest for 20 min.
2. Sear over medium-high heat for 5-6 min per side until the core reaches 74 °C.
3. Rest for 5 min, then chop into 15-20 mm pieces.

### LAYER AND BAKE

4. Heat oven to 220 °C.
5. Spread half the chips in a wide tray. Layer with half the chicken, beans, cheese sauce, and grated cheese.
6. Repeat with remaining chips, chicken, beans, cheese sauce, and grated cheese.
7. Bake for 6-8 min until hot and bubbling but before the chips soften.

### FINISH

8. Spoon salsa and crema over the nachos.
9. Finish with jalapeño and coriander; serve immediately.

---

## NOTES

- Reconstructed assembly quantities; keep wet garnishes concentrated in small spoonfuls to preserve crispness.
- Two thinner layers distribute toppings better than one overloaded layer.
- **Story:** This is the project's maximalist Tex-Mex platter, designed to bring several standalone components together without turning the chips into wet cardboard.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **Half batch:** Scale to 50% and bake on a 300 mm tray.
- **Sharper finish:** Add 60 g finely diced pickled red onion after baking.
