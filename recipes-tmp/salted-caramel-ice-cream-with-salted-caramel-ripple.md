# SALTED CARAMEL ICE CREAM WITH SALTED CARAMEL RIPPLE

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields approximately 950 mL finished ice cream  
Caramel custard ice cream layered with a soft, salty caramel ripple  
The ice cream, caramel, and ripple are consolidated as one canonical recipe

---

## INGREDIENTS

### SALTED CARAMEL ICE CREAM BASE

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Whole milk | 300 g | 100% |
| Cream, approximately 35% fat | 180 g | 60% |
| Sucrose | 110 g | 36.7% |
| Dextrose | 25 g | 8.3% |
| Skim milk powder | 25 g | 8.3% |
| Egg yolk | 50 g | 16.7% |
| Ice cream stabiliser | 1.5 g | 0.5% |
| Fine salt | 2 g | 0.7% |

### SALTED CARAMEL RIPPLE

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Sucrose | 150 g | 100% |
| Glucose syrup | 50 g | 33.3% |
| Cream, warm | 150 g | 100% |
| Unsalted butter | 40 g | 26.7% |
| Fine salt | 3 g | 2% |

---

## PROCEDURE

### CARAMELISE SUGAR FOR BASE

1. Heat 110 g sucrose in a heavy saucepan to a dark amber caramel.
2. Carefully add 180 g warm cream in stages. Stir until smooth, then add milk.

### MIX AND PASTEURISE BASE

3. Whisk dextrose, skim milk powder, stabiliser, and salt together. Blend into the caramel dairy mixture.
4. Temper egg yolk with the warm mixture, recombine, and heat while stirring to 82-84 °C for 30 sec.
5. Blend for 30 sec and cool to below 4 °C as quickly as possible.
6. Refrigerate for 6-12 h.

### CARAMEL RIPPLE

7. Combine sucrose and glucose syrup. Cook to a dark amber caramel.
8. Carefully add warm cream in stages and stir smooth.
9. Remove from heat and blend in butter and salt.
10. Cool completely. The ripple should be thick but pourable at 20 °C.

### CHURN AND LAYER

11. Churn the aged base to approximately -6 °C.
12. Pack one-third of the ice cream into a chilled container and drizzle with 50 g ripple. Repeat twice, ending with ripple.
13. Fold once with a narrow spatula to marble; do not mix completely.
14. Harden at -18 °C for at least 4 h.

---

## NOTES

- Reconstructed formulation; caramel colour is the main flavour control. Stop before it smells burnt or acrid.
- Any remaining ripple keeps refrigerated for up to 2 weeks.
- **Story:** This merges three stages of the project's salted-caramel experiments into one deliberately excessive keeper: caramel in the scoop and caramel running through it.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **Without ripple:** Churn the base as written and omit the layering phase.
- **Bitter caramel:** Take both caramels 5-10 °C darker, judging carefully by aroma and colour.
