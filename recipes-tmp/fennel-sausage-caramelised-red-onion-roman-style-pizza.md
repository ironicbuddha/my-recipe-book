# FENNEL SAUSAGE & CARAMELISED RED ONION ROMAN-STYLE PIZZA

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields one 300 mm x 400 mm tray pizza; 4 portions  
Crisp, airy Roman-style base topped with fennel sausage, sweet red onion, mozzarella, and pecorino

---

## INGREDIENTS

### DOUGH

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bread flour | 400 g | 100% |
| Water, cold | 320 g | 80% |
| Fine salt | 10 g | 2.5% |
| Instant yeast | 2 g | 0.5% |
| Extra-virgin olive oil | 12 g | 3% |

### CARAMELISED RED ONION

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Red onion, thinly sliced | 300 g | 100% |
| Extra-virgin olive oil | 25 g | 8.3% |
| Fine salt | 3 g | 1% |
| Balsamic vinegar | 15 g | 5% |

### TOPPING

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Pizza dough | 744 g | 100% |
| Fennel pork sausage, casing removed | 300 g | 40.3% |
| Caramelised red onion | 240 g | 32.3% |
| Low-moisture mozzarella, grated | 250 g | 33.6% |
| Pecorino Romano, finely grated | 50 g | 6.7% |
| Fennel seed, lightly crushed | 3 g | 0.4% |
| Extra-virgin olive oil | 20 g | 2.7% |
| Parsley leaves | 15 g | 2% |

---

## PROCEDURE

### DOUGH

1. Combine flour, 300 g water, and yeast until no dry flour remains. Rest for 20 min.
2. Dissolve salt in remaining 20 g water and work it into the dough, followed by olive oil.
3. Perform four stretch-and-folds at 20 min intervals. Refrigerate, covered, for 18-24 h.
4. Bring dough to room temperature for 2 h. Stretch gently into a well-oiled 300 mm x 400 mm tray.
5. Proof for 45-60 min until aerated.

### ONION AND SAUSAGE

6. Cook onion, oil, and salt over medium-low heat for 25-30 min until soft and deeply coloured. Deglaze with balsamic vinegar and cool.
7. Brown sausage in 20-30 g pieces over medium-high heat until nearly cooked; cool.

### BAKE

8. Heat oven and baking steel or stone to 275 °C, or the oven's maximum temperature, for 45 min.
9. Distribute mozzarella, onion, sausage, fennel seed, and Pecorino over the proofed dough.
10. Bake for 12-16 min until the underside is crisp and the top is browned.
11. Finish with olive oil and parsley. Rest for 3 min before cutting.

---

## NOTES

- Dough hydration and baking time are reconstructed and will need adjustment for the flour and oven used.
- Keep the onion and sausage cool before topping to protect the proofed dough.
- **Story:** This pizza captures the project's Roman-pizza experiments in one specific, memorable fennel-sausage combination.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **Bianca:** Use as written, without tomato.
- **Sharper finish:** Replace 10 g finishing oil with 10 g red-wine vinegar dressed through 30 g rocket.
