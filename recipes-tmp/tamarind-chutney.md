# TAMARIND CHUTNEY

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields approximately 500 g chutney  
Glossy sweet-sour tamarind sauce with date, jaggery, and warm spice

---

## INGREDIENTS

### CHUTNEY

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Tamarind concentrate | 100 g | 100% |
| Water | 350 g | 350% |
| Pitted dates, chopped | 150 g | 150% |
| Jaggery or dark brown sugar | 100 g | 100% |
| Ground cumin, toasted | 5 g | 5% |
| Ground ginger | 2 g | 2% |
| Kashmiri chilli powder | 2 g | 2% |
| Fine salt | 5 g | 5% |
| Garam masala | 2 g | 2% |

---

## PROCEDURE

### SIMMER

1. Combine tamarind concentrate, water, dates, jaggery, cumin, ginger, chilli, and salt.
2. Simmer gently for 20-25 min until the dates are soft and the mixture is lightly syrupy.

### BLEND AND STRAIN

3. Blend until smooth.
4. Pass through a fine sieve, pressing firmly on the solids.
5. Return to the heat and simmer to a pourable chutney consistency.
6. Stir in garam masala, cool rapidly, and refrigerate.

---

## NOTES

- Reconstructed formulation; commercial tamarind concentrates vary sharply in strength and acidity.
- The chutney thickens as it cools. Stop the reduction while it is slightly looser than the target texture.
- **Story:** This is the dark, sweet-sour half of the project's chutney pairing, built to sit opposite the fresh mint version.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **Without dates:** Omit dates, increase jaggery to 160 g, and reduce water to 300 g.
- **Sharper:** Add 15 g lime juice after cooling.
