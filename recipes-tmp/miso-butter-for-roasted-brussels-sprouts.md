# MISO BUTTER FOR ROASTED BRUSSELS SPROUTS

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields 4 side portions; approximately 700 g finished sprouts  
Hard-roasted Brussels sprouts glazed with savoury-sweet miso butter and rice vinegar

---

## INGREDIENTS

### ROASTED BRUSSELS SPROUTS

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved | 800 g | 100% |
| Neutral oil | 40 g | 5% |
| Fine salt | 8 g | 1% |
| Black pepper | 2 g | 0.3% |

### MISO BUTTER

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unsalted butter, softened | 100 g | 100% |
| White miso | 50 g | 50% |
| Honey | 20 g | 20% |
| Rice vinegar | 20 g | 20% |
| Soy sauce | 10 g | 10% |
| Garlic, finely grated | 5 g | 5% |
| Sesame oil | 5 g | 5% |

### FINISH

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Roasted Brussels sprouts | 650 g | 100% |
| Miso butter | 120 g | 18.5% |
| Toasted sesame seeds | 15 g | 2.3% |

---

## PROCEDURE

### MISO BUTTER

1. Beat butter, miso, honey, rice vinegar, soy sauce, garlic, and sesame oil until homogeneous.
2. Reserve 120 g for this recipe; refrigerate the remainder.

### ROAST

3. Heat oven and a heavy baking tray to 230 °C.
4. Toss sprouts with oil, salt, and pepper.
5. Place cut-side down on the hot tray and roast for 18-25 min until deeply browned and tender.

### GLAZE

6. Transfer hot sprouts to a bowl and toss with miso butter until glossy.
7. Return to the oven for 2 min, then finish with sesame seeds.

---

## NOTES

- Reconstructed formulation; miso salinity varies, so taste before adding extra salt.
- The butter is reusable and keeps refrigerated for up to 7 days.
- **Story:** This recipe preserves the project's miso butter as a standalone component while keeping its original Brussels-sprout destination attached.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **Citrus:** Add 3 g finely grated orange zest to the butter.
- **Spicy:** Add 8 g chilli crisp after roasting.
