# CARNE ASADA TACOS

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields 12 tacos; 4 portions  
Charred citrus-garlic beef served in warm corn tortillas with onion, coriander, and lime

---

## INGREDIENTS

### CARNE ASADA

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Beef skirt or flank steak | 800 g | 100% |
| Orange juice | 100 g | 12.5% |
| Lime juice | 50 g | 6.3% |
| Neutral oil | 40 g | 5% |
| Garlic, finely grated | 15 g | 1.9% |
| Ground cumin | 4 g | 0.5% |
| Ground coriander | 4 g | 0.5% |
| Smoked paprika | 4 g | 0.5% |
| Dried oregano | 2 g | 0.3% |
| Fine salt | 12 g | 1.5% |
| Black pepper | 2 g | 0.3% |

### ASSEMBLY

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cooked carne asada | 650 g | 100% |
| Corn tortillas, approximately 25 g each | 300 g | 46.2% |
| White onion, finely diced | 120 g | 18.5% |
| Coriander leaves, chopped | 30 g | 4.6% |
| Lime wedges | 160 g | 24.6% |
| Salsa | 200 g | 30.8% |

---

## PROCEDURE

### MARINATE

1. Combine orange juice, lime juice, oil, garlic, spices, salt, and pepper.
2. Coat beef and refrigerate for 2-4 h. Remove from refrigeration 30 min before cooking.

### GRILL

3. Heat a grill or cast-iron pan until very hot.
4. Drain excess marinade from beef. Grill for 3-5 min per side, depending on thickness, to a core temperature of 54-57 °C.
5. Rest for 8 min. Slice thinly across the grain, then cut into bite-size pieces.

### ASSEMBLY

6. Warm tortillas on the hot grill for 20-30 sec per side and hold in a cloth.
7. Divide beef among tortillas. Add onion, coriander, salsa, and lime.

---

## NOTES

- Marinade and cooking times are reconstructed; do not marinate longer than 8 h because the citrus can soften the surface excessively.
- Slice across the grain for tenderness.
- **Story:** Carne asada anchors the project's taco work and connects directly to the black beans, salsa, and crema documented alongside it.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **Hotter:** Add 10 g minced chipotle in adobo to the marinade.
- **Plancha service:** Chop the rested beef and return it to a very hot plancha for 30 sec to crisp the edges.
