# CHUNKY TOMATO BURGER SAUCE

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields approximately 500 g sauce  
Reduced tomato, onion, pickle, and mustard sauce with enough texture to stay put on a burger

---

## INGREDIENTS

### TOMATO BASE

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Canned chopped tomato | 400 g | 100% |
| Onion, finely diced | 120 g | 30% |
| Neutral oil | 20 g | 5% |
| Tomato paste | 30 g | 7.5% |
| Brown sugar | 25 g | 6.3% |
| Apple cider vinegar | 35 g | 8.8% |
| Worcestershire sauce | 15 g | 3.8% |
| Fine salt | 6 g | 1.5% |
| Smoked paprika | 2 g | 0.5% |
| Black pepper | 1 g | 0.3% |

### FINISH

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Reduced tomato base | 420 g | 100% |
| Dill pickle, finely diced | 60 g | 14.3% |
| Dijon mustard | 25 g | 6% |
| Mayonnaise | 50 g | 11.9% |

---

## PROCEDURE

### COOK TOMATO BASE

1. Sweat onion in oil over medium heat for 8 min without browning.
2. Add tomato paste and paprika; cook for 2 min.
3. Add tomato, sugar, vinegar, Worcestershire sauce, salt, and pepper.
4. Simmer uncovered for 25-35 min until thick and chunky. Cool completely.

### FINISH

5. Fold pickle, mustard, and mayonnaise through the cold tomato base.
6. Refrigerate for 1 h before serving.

---

## NOTES

- The sauce is reconstructed from its project title; reduce it enough that no free liquid pools on a plate.
- Keeps refrigerated for up to 5 days.
- **Story:** This is a deliberately chunky burger sauce—more relish than pink mayonnaise, and built to taste of actual tomato.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **Smooth:** Blend the cooked tomato base before cooling, then add finely minced pickle.
- **Hot:** Add 15 g minced pickled jalapeño.
