# PRESSURE-COOKER CHARRO BEANS

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields approximately 1.6 kg; 8 side portions  
Brothy pinto beans enriched with bacon, tomato, chilli, and warm spices

---

## INGREDIENTS

### BEANS

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dried pinto beans, rinsed | 500 g | 100% |
| Water | 1500 g | 300% |
| White onion, diced | 200 g | 40% |
| Garlic, finely chopped | 20 g | 4% |
| Bacon, diced | 200 g | 40% |
| Tomato, diced | 300 g | 60% |
| Jalapeño, finely diced | 30 g | 6% |
| Chipotle in adobo, minced | 20 g | 4% |
| Ground cumin | 4 g | 0.8% |
| Dried oregano | 3 g | 0.6% |
| Bay leaf | 1 g | 0.2% |
| Fine salt | 12 g | 2.4% |

### FINISH

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cooked charro beans | 1500 g | 100% |
| Coriander leaves, chopped | 30 g | 2% |
| Lime juice | 30 g | 2% |

---

## PROCEDURE

### AROMATIC BASE

1. Render bacon in the pressure-cooker pot over medium heat for 8 min.
2. Add onion and jalapeño; cook for 6 min. Add garlic, cumin, oregano, and chipotle; cook for 1 min.
3. Add tomato and cook for 5 min, scraping the base clean.

### PRESSURE COOK

4. Add beans, water, bay leaf, and salt.
5. Cook at high pressure for 40 min.
6. Allow a 20 min natural pressure release, then release remaining pressure.

### FINISH

7. Discard bay leaf. Simmer uncovered until the broth reaches the desired concentration.
8. Stir in coriander and lime juice. Adjust salt.

---

## NOTES

- Reconstructed as an unsoaked method; old beans may require another 5-10 min at high pressure.
- Keep charro beans brothy. They should not reduce to the texture of taco beans.
- **Story:** This is the more generous, smoky cousin to the project's black taco beans: built to be eaten by the bowl or alongside grilled meat.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **Chorizo:** Replace 100 g bacon with 150 g fresh chorizo.
- **Beer-braised:** Replace 330 g water with 330 g lager.
