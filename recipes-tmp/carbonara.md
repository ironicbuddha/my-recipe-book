# CARBONARA

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields 2 portions; approximately 500 g finished pasta  
Roman-style spaghetti emulsified with guanciale, egg, Pecorino Romano, and black pepper  
Scaled from the project's 200 g pasta version

---

## INGREDIENTS

### PASTA AND GUANCIALE

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dried spaghetti | 200 g | 100% |
| Guanciale, cut into batons | 100 g | 50% |
| Fine salt, for pasta water | 8 g | 4% |
| Water | 2000 g | 1000% |

### EGG AND CHEESE MIXTURE

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Egg yolk | 72 g | 100% |
| Whole egg | 50 g | 69.4% |
| Pecorino Romano, finely grated | 80 g | 111.1% |
| Black pepper, coarsely ground | 3 g | 4.2% |
| Reserved pasta water | 100 g | 138.9% |

---

## PROCEDURE

### PREPARATION

1. Combine yolks, whole egg, Pecorino, and half the black pepper to form a thick paste.
2. Bring water and salt to a boil.

### GUANCIALE

3. Place guanciale in a cold pan. Cook over medium-low heat for 8-10 min until the fat renders and the meat is crisp at the edges.
4. Remove the pan from heat, keeping the guanciale and rendered fat together.

### PASTA AND EMULSION

5. Cook spaghetti until 2 min short of al dente. Reserve at least 150 g pasta water.
6. Transfer pasta to the guanciale pan and toss over low heat for 30 sec.
7. Remove from heat and cool for 30 sec. Add the egg mixture and toss vigorously.
8. Add reserved pasta water in 15-20 g increments until the sauce is glossy and fluid, with no visible curds.
9. Divide between warm bowls and finish with remaining black pepper.

---

## NOTES

- The pan should be below approximately 70 °C when the egg mixture is added; residual heat finishes the sauce.
- The quantity is reconstructed from the project's named two-portion version and should be tuned to the saltiness of the cheese and guanciale.
- **Story:** Carbonara appears in the project as both a solo bowl and a 200 g two-person version; this is the consolidated latter format.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **Single portion:** Scale every ingredient to 50%.
- **More yolk-forward:** Replace the whole egg with 36 g additional yolk.
