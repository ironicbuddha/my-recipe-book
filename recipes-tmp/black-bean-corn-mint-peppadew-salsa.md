# BLACK BEAN, CORN, MINT & PEPPADEW SALSA

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields approximately 700 g salsa  
Sweet corn, black beans, fresh mint, and piquant Peppadew peppers in a lime dressing

---

## INGREDIENTS

### SALSA

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cooked black beans, drained | 250 g | 100% |
| Corn kernels | 250 g | 100% |
| Peppadew peppers, drained and diced | 100 g | 40% |
| Red onion, finely diced | 80 g | 32% |
| Mint leaves, finely sliced | 20 g | 8% |
| Coriander leaves, chopped | 20 g | 8% |
| Lime juice | 50 g | 20% |
| Extra-virgin olive oil | 30 g | 12% |
| Fine salt | 6 g | 2.4% |
| Ground cumin | 2 g | 0.8% |
| Black pepper | 1 g | 0.4% |

---

## PROCEDURE

### CORN

1. Heat a dry cast-iron pan over high heat.
2. Char corn for 4-6 min, stirring only occasionally, then cool completely.

### DRESSING

3. Whisk lime juice, olive oil, salt, cumin, and black pepper.
4. Soak red onion in the dressing for 10 min.

### ASSEMBLY

5. Fold beans, corn, Peppadew, mint, and coriander through the dressed onion.
6. Rest for 15 min, then adjust salt and lime before serving.

---

## NOTES

- Reconstructed quantities; Peppadew sweetness varies, so lime may need adjustment.
- Add mint only after all cooked ingredients are cool to prevent discolouration.
- **Story:** The Peppadew and mint combination gives this project salsa a distinctly South African, pantry-led personality.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **Hotter:** Add 15 g finely diced fresh jalapeño.
- **Creamy:** Fold in 150 g diced avocado immediately before serving.
