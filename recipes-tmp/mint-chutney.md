# MINT CHUTNEY

**DRAFT — reconstructed from project history**

Yields approximately 300 g chutney  
Fresh mint and coriander chutney sharpened with lime, green chilli, and ginger

---

## INGREDIENTS

### CHUTNEY

| Ingredient | Quantity | Scaling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mint leaves | 80 g | 100% |
| Coriander leaves and tender stems | 120 g | 150% |
| Plain yoghurt | 80 g | 100% |
| Lime juice | 45 g | 56.3% |
| Green chilli | 20 g | 25% |
| Fresh ginger | 15 g | 18.8% |
| Ground cumin, toasted | 3 g | 3.8% |
| Fine salt | 5 g | 6.3% |
| Sugar | 5 g | 6.3% |
| Ice-cold water | 30 g | 37.5% |

---

## PROCEDURE

### BLEND

1. Chill the blender jar and ingredients for 15 min.
2. Blend yoghurt, lime juice, chilli, ginger, cumin, salt, sugar, and water until smooth.
3. Add mint and coriander. Blend in short pulses just until smooth and bright green.
4. Chill immediately and rest for 15 min before adjusting salt and acidity.

---

## NOTES

- Reconstructed formulation; cold ingredients and minimal blending protect the green colour.
- Best used the day it is made.
- **Story:** This is the fresh, green edge of the project's curry accompaniment set, cutting through slow-cooked richness.

---

## VARIATIONS

- **Dairy-free:** Replace yoghurt with 60 g water and 20 g neutral oil.
- **Sweeter:** Increase sugar to 15 g for samosa or chaat service.
